26\. NÄNABUSHU AND WINDIGO.

Nanabushu and Windigo

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by probably Midāsugaⁿj (Michelson’s own hedge, carried) — Series 2 (Nos. 19–32), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ningutingigu papimācạgāmät īnābit iwiti ạgämikistcigạmi awiya owâbạmān ‘iᵉi’ᵘ pimācạgāmänit, ā‘pidcisa minditōwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo babimaazhagaamed inaabid iwidi agaami-gichi-gami awiya owaabamaan i’iw bimaazhagaamenid, aapiji sa mindidowan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And once, while walking along the shore (and) looking towards the other side of the sea, he saw one passing along the coast, tremendously huge was the being.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo (and once) babimaazhagaamed (as he walked along the shore) inaabid (looking off) iwidi agaami-gichi-gami (over there across the great sea) awiya owaabamaan (he saw someone) i’iw bimaazhagaamenid (passing along the shore), aapiji sa mindidowan (tremendously big was that one).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Kāwīn kanagä ä‘kōnīngitawitiyänit a‘kōsisīwạn ‘iᵉi’ᵘ mi‘tigōᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin ganage eko-niingidawidiyenid akoozisiiwan i’iw mitigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nowhere near to his buttocks were the trees in their height.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin ganage (not at all, nowhere near) eko-niingidawidiyenid (as high as his buttocks) akoozisiiwan i’iw mitigoon (did the trees reach in height).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Ānīc, mīwạniniᵘ Wīndigo.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish, mii wa iniw Wiindigo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Well, of course it was Wīndigo.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish (well now), mii wa iniw Wiindigo (it was that Wiindigo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Wägunänīwinän upīpāgimān:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wegoneniiwinen obiibaagimaan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Like a fool he called aloud to him :*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wegoneniiwinen (foolishly, what-on-earth-like) obiibaagimaan (he called out to him):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Nicīmisa, wạtcaka∙ä∙ā∙nowạn kāmāmīdcitạmạn!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nishiime sa, wajaka’eaanowan gaa-maamiijidaman!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“O my younger brother ! upon the dry tail of a beaver did you ease yourself.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nishiime sa (O my little brother), wajaka’eaanowan (upon a dried beaver tail) gaa-maamiijidaman (is what you have eased yourself upon)!</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Pämācagāmäyạn, woiⁱ!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bemaazhagaameyan, woii!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*You passer along the shore, halloo !”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bemaazhagaameyan (you who pass along the shore), woii!” (halloo!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) ānīc, nāmā wāsa inäntạm Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish, naa maa waasa inendam Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Well, at a safe distance away Nänabushu thought (he was).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish (well), naa maa waasa inendam Nenaboozhoo (at a good distance off Nenaboozhoo thought himself to be).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Ā‘pidcidạc unickimigōn Wīndigo Nänabucōwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji dash onishkimigoon Wiindigo Nenaboozhoowan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, very angry was Wīndigo made by Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji dash onishkimigoon Wiindigoo (and very angry was Wiindigo made) Nenaboozhoowan (by Nenaboozhoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) “Kägä‘t māma‘kātc.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Geget maamakaaj.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“It is really absurd.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Geget maamakaaj (truly it is astonishing/absurd).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Kunigä, ‘Nīnä‘ta nimạnitōwi,’ inänamutug ‘aᵉa’ᵘ Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gonige, ‘Niin eta ni-manidoow,’ inendamodog a’aw Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Perhaps ‘I am the only manitou existing,’ may be Nänabushu’s thought.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gonige (perhaps), ‘Niin eta ni-manidoow,’ (‘I alone am a manidoo,’) inendamodog a’aw Nenaboozhoo (so must Nenaboozhoo be thinking).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Ạmbäsanō, ningagīwi‘tạgāmäba‘tō.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ambe sa noo, ninga-giiwitaagaamebatoo.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Well, I will run round (to where he is), keeping to the shore.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ambe sa noo (well, come now), ninga-giiwitaagaamebatoo.” (I will run around the shore.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Kägä‘t mādcība‘tō Wīndigo, ki‘tciwība udōdi‘tān imā pimācạgāmänit īni’ᵘ Nänabucōwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget maajiibatoo Wiindigoo, gichi-wiiba odoo-ditaan imaa bimaazhagaamenid iniw Nenaboozhoowan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Truly off went running Wīndigo, in a very little while he arrived at the place where Nänabushu was coming along the shore.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget maajiibatoo Wiindigoo (truly off ran Wiindigo), gichi-wiiba odoo-ditaan (very quickly he reached) imaa bimaazhagaamenid iniw Nenaboozhoowan (the place where Nenaboozhoo was coming along the shore).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Wägunänīwinän äcitcāngitiyäcing ‘aᵉa’ᵘ Wīndigō.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wegoneniiwinen ezhi-jaangidiyeshing a’aw Wiindigoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*What did Wīndigo do but get down and lay with his bottom up.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wegoneniiwinen (what should he do but) ezhi-jaangidiyeshing a’aw Wiindigoo (Wiindigo lay down with his bottom pointing up).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Nāgạtcigu cigwa pagạmācạgāmäwạn Nänabucōwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Naagaj igo zhigwa bagamaazhagaamewan Nenaboozhoowan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*It was but a short while when up came Nänabushu to where he was on the shore.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Naagaj igo zhigwa (after a while, now) bagamaazhagaamewan Nenaboozhoowan (Nenaboozhoo came up along the shore).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Cigwa owâbạmigōn; ānīn gä∙i∙cinạng awiya kītcāngitiyäcinōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa owaabamigoon; aaniin ge-izhinang awiiya gii-jaangidiyeshinoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then (by Nänabushu) was he observed ; what was he to see but somebody lying with bottom pointing up.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa owaabamigoon (then he was seen by him); aaniin ge-izhinang (what should he see but) awiiya gii-jaangidiyeshinoon (someone lying with bottom pointing up).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Mīgu iᵘ ānawi∙i∙cigāsō‘tawāt omạdwäkạnōnigōn Nänabuco:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii go iw aanawi-izhi-gaazootawaad omadwe-kanoonigoon Nenaboozhoo:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly, when he made an attempt to hide from him, Nänabushu heard (Wīndigo) saying to him:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii go iw aanawi-izhi-gaazootawaad (and so when in vain he tried to hide from him) omadwe-kanoonigoon Nenaboozhoo (Nenaboozhoo heard him being addressed):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) “Ondās, a‘kawä, ninganawạtcīn ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kīya’ᵘ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ondaas, akawe, ninga-nawajiin i’iw giiyaw.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Come hither, wait, I want to make a small meal out of you.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ondaas (come here), akawe (wait), ninga-nawajiin i’iw giiyaw (I will make a little meal of your body).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) Wäwīp, ontās.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wewiib, ondaas.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Make haste, come!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wewiib (hurry), ondaas.” (come here.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Ä‘tawā, Nänabucu ạnigāgīmōta‘tämo ạnināⁿzi‘kawāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Etawaa, Nenaboozhoo ani-gaagiimoodademo ani-naazikawaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Alas ! Nänabushu began weeping silently on his way over to where (Wīndigo) was.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Etawaa (alas), Nenaboozhoo ani-gaagiimoodademo (Nenaboozhoo went along weeping quietly) ani-naazikawaad (as he made his way over to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) Cigwa imā odōdisān; kägätsa mạngitcītīwạn, micicạgu ‘iᵉi’ᵘ odä∙i∙ni.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa imaa odoo-disaan; geget sa mangijiidiiwan, mizhisha go i’iw ode’ini.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Presently he came to where (Wīndigo) was ; truly big was his anus, and in plain view was his heart.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa imaa odoo-disaan (now he came to where he was); geget sa mangijiidiiwan (truly big was his anus), mizhisha go i’iw ode’ini (and in plain view was his heart).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Ugạnōnigōn: “Nänabucu, mạnisän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Oganoonigoon: “Nenaboozhoo, manisen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*By him was he addressed, saying: “Nänabushu, go gather some fire-wood.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Oganoonigoon (he was addressed): “Nenaboozhoo, manisen (Nenaboozhoo, gather firewood).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Ninga∙a∙‘kawä∙ạ∙bwän ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kīya’ᵘ.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ninga-akawe-abwen i’iw giiyaw.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*I will first roast your body.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ninga-akawe-abwen i’iw giiyaw.” (first I will roast your body.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Kägä‘t äjigu‘pīt Nänabucu mạnisät.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget ezhi-gopiid Nenaboozhoo manised.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Truly then up from the shore went Nänabushu to gather fire-wood.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget ezhi-gopiid Nenaboozhoo (truly then up from the shore went Nenaboozhoo) manised (to gather firewood).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) Ä‘tawā, mōjạg mawi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Etawaa, moozhag mawi.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Poor fellow! all the while was he crying.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Etawaa (poor fellow), moozhag mawi (all the while he wept).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Cigwasa nībiwa kā∙u∙‘kwā‘kwisi‘tōd īni’ᵘ misạn, ugạnōnigōn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa sa niibiwa gaa-okwaakwisidood iniw misan, oganoonigoon:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After he had piled up much fire-wood, he was addressed:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa sa niibiwa gaa-okwaakwisidood iniw misan (now after he had piled up much firewood) oganoonigoon (he was addressed):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) “Mī∙i∙ᵘ icibōdawän.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii iw izhi-boodawen.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Now, do you kindle a fire.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii iw izhi-boodawen.” (now then, kindle a fire.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Nänabucu äjibōdawät.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenaboozhoo ezhi-boodawed.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nänabushu then kindled the fire.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenaboozhoo ezhi-boodawed (Nenaboozhoo then kindled the fire).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) Kā‘pōdawät uganōnigōn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-poodawed oganoonigon:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After he had kindled the fire, he was addressed:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-poodawed (after he had kindled the fire) oganoonigon (he was addressed):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) “Nänabucu, mī∙i’∙ᵘ ici∙ạ∙ntawâbạntạn ‘iᵉi’wä käwạtabwānā‘kuyān ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kīyawic.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nenaboozhoo, mii i’iw izhi-andawaabandan i’iwe ge-wadabwaanaakoyaan i’iw giiyawizh.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Nänabushu, now go you hence to seek for something which I can use for a spit to roast your old body on.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nenaboozhoo, mii i’iw izhi-andawaabandan (Nenaboozhoo, now go and look for) i’iwe ge-wadabwaanaakoyaan (something I can use as a roasting-spit) i’iw giiyawizh (for your wretched body).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Ā‘pidci wänā‘kwa‘k, māgicā tạgitā‘kwitin ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kiwīsäncis.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji wenaakwak, maagizhaa dagidaakwidin i’iw giwiisenshis.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*(Let it be) very straight, else perhaps your old spleen might be forced out.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji wenaakwak (very straight), maagizhaa dagidaakwidin i’iw giwiisenshis.” (or else perhaps your old spleen might be forced out.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Kägä‘t ạnimādcā Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget ani-maajaa Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Truly away went Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget ani-maajaa Nenaboozhoo (truly off went Nenaboozhoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Ānīc mōcạg mawi, ä‘pītcisägisit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish moozhag mawi, epiichi-segizid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, all the time was he weeping, he was so scared.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish moozhag mawi (now all the while he wept), epiichi-segizid (so frightened was he).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) Ā‘pidcigu ‘iᵉi’wä wayāninā‘kwạtinik ‘iᵉi’wä mi‘tig umi‘kān, kayä iᵘ säsạgati‘kwạniwạninik.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji go i’iwe wayaaninaakwadinig i’iwe mitig omikaan, gaye iw zesagadikwaniwaninig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So a stick with a very big curve he found, and it had many twigs.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji go i’iwe wayaaninaakwadinig i’iwe mitig (a very crooked stick) omikaan (he found), gaye iw zesagadikwaniwaninig (and one with many branching twigs).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Äjimādcāt icāt äcimīnāt; ō∙ō∙ udinān: “Tawa‘t, tawa‘t, tawa‘t!”¹</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-maajaad izhaad ezhi-miinaad; o’ow odinaan: “Dawat, dawat, dawat!”(1)**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then he started going over to give it to (Wīndigo); this he said to him: “Oh, oh, oh!”(1)*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-maajaad izhaad ezhi-miinaad (then he set off, going to give it to him); o’ow odinaan (this he said to him): “Dawat, dawat, dawat!”(1) (“Dawat, dawat, dawat!”(1))</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) Ä∙ī∙nditcin ‘aᵉa’ᵘ Nänabuco.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**E’iindijin a’aw Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Such was the way Nänabushu acted.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">E’iindijin a’aw Nenaboozhoo (such was the way Nenaboozhoo behaved).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) “Kāwīninawīn tagitā‘kwitin iwä kiwīsäncic?</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gaawiin ina wiin dagidaakwidin iwe giwiizenshizh?**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Would not your old spleen be forced out by that?*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaawiin ina wiin dagidaakwidin iwe giwiizenshizh? (would not your wretched old spleen be forced out by that?)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) Bä‘kānạg iwä mi‘tig nạndawâbạⁿdạn, wänā‘kwạk.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bekaanag iwe mitig nandawaabandan, wenaakwak.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*For a different one do you seek, one that is straight.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bekaanag iwe mitig nandawaabandan (look for a different stick), wenaakwak.” (one that is straight.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Ä‘tawā Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Etawaa Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Hapless Nänabushu!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Etawaa Nenaboozhoo (poor, hapless Nenaboozhoo)!</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) Mīdạc gägät ạnimawit, pīnicigu wāsa ạnitạgucin.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget ani-mawid, biinish igo waasa ani-dagoshin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon truly off he went crying, (going) till he a long way off was come.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget ani-mawid (thereupon truly off he went weeping), biinish igo waasa ani-dagoshin (until he had come a long way off).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) Pāmāgu ạnī∙i∙nābit, tä‘tiwä, cingusạn kībimiba‘tōwạn äjigạnōnāt:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Baamaa go ani-inaabid, detiwe, zhingosan gii-bimibatoowan ezhi-ganoonaad:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And suddenly, while looking about as he went, why, here was a Weasel whom he addressed as it went running past:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Baamaa go ani-inaabid (presently, looking about as he went), detiwe (why, there!), zhingosan gii-bimibatoowan (a weasel went running past) ezhi-ganoonaad (whom he addressed):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) “Nistcīmiˢtcā, nīnip nōngum.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nishiime sa, ni-nib noongom.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My little brother, I am now going to die.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nishiime sa (my little brother), ni-nib noongom.” (I am going to die now.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(42) Kibi‘tcisäwạn īni’ᵘ cingusạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gibichisewan iniw zhingosan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In its flight stopped the Weasel.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gibichisewan iniw zhingosan (the weasel stopped short in its dash).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(43) Otā‘tagạnābạmigōn äjigạnōnigut:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Odaataganaabamigoon ezhi-ganoonigod:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*By it was he gazed up at when by it he was addressed:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Odaataganaabamigoon (he was gazed up at by it) ezhi-ganoonigod (when it addressed him):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(44) “Nänabucu, wägunäc wâ∙u∙ndcinibuyạn?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nenaboozhoo, wegonesh waa-onji-niboyan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Nänabushu, why are you going to die?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nenaboozhoo, wegonesh waa-onji-niboyan?” (Nenaboozhoo, why is it you are going to die?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(45) “A‘tawā, mīginīni∙i’∙ᵘ wī∙a∙‘kawinawạtcīt waᵉa’ᵘ Wīndigō ‘iᵉi’ᵘ nīya’ᵘ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Atawaa, mii go niin i’iw wii-akawe-nawajiid wa’aw Wiindigoo i’iw niiyaw.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Alas! because a light meal does that Wīndigo intend to make of my body.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Atawaa (alas), mii go niin i’iw wii-akawe-nawajiid wa’aw Wiindigoo i’iw niiyaw (it is that this Wiindigo means first to make a little meal of my body).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(46) Kāwīnina kitānisāsī?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin ina gidaa-nisaasii?”**</span>
