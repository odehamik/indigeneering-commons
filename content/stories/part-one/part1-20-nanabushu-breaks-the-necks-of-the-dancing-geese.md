20\. NÄNABUSHU BREAKS THE NECKS OF THE DANCING GEESE.

<span class="l-fiero">**Naanabozhoo obookogwebinaan iniw niimi’idiijin nikag**</span>

Nänabushu Breaks the Necks of the Dancing Geese (2)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by probably Midāsugaⁿj (Michelson’s own hedge, carried) — Series 2 (Nos. 19–32), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Mīsạgu ‘i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ bā‘pic ạnimādcānit. Nänabucu ạnibạbimusät cigwadạc umạdābī sībīns; udạnibạbima∙ạ∙tōn kägä‘pī∙i∙gu miˢ‘tcānig ‘i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ sībi. Cigwadạc udäbābạtān ‘i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ sāga∙i∙gạn, ā‘pidci unicicinini imā wändcisāgitawānig ‘iᵉi´ᵘ sībi. Inābit Nänabuco mīnisi∙u∙tāwạngānig; owâbamāᵉ ni‘känsaᵉ, ā‘pidcisa pā‘tinīnōwaᵉ. Nänabucu wägunänīwinän, nātạgāmäba‘tō, udawī∙u∙dā‘pinān īni´ᵘ cīngubīn; u‘kunācicing ugackīwäginān. Uciwạni‘kä iᵘ cingubī, ā‘pidcisa ubwāwạnänaᵉ iᵘ cingubī. Mīdạc ‘iᵉi´ᵘ änīcimādcāt udani∙ā∙sunān ‘i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ umi‘kwābān. Änisāgäwät kīgito aᵘ kiˢ‘tcini‘ka: “Nänabucu sāgäwät. Ucimuyu‘k, kägūgu kīga∙i∙gunān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa go i’iw baapish ani-maajaanid. Naanabozhoo ani-babimosed zhigwa dash omadaabii ziibiins; odani-babimaa’adoon gegapii igo michaanig i’iw ziibi. Zhigwa dash odebaabandaan i’iw zaaga’igan, aapiji onizhishinini imaa wenji-zaagidawaanig i’iw ziibi. Inaabid Naanabozhoo minisi-odaawangaanig; owaabamaan niikaansan, aapijisa baatiininowan. Naanabozhoo wegonen-iwinen, naadagaameba’too, odawii-odaapinaan iniw zhingobiin; okonaazhishing* *ogashkiiweginaan*. Ozhiwanike iw zhingobii, aapijisa obwaawanenan* iw zhingobii. Mii dash i’iw ani-izhi-maajaad odani-aasoonan i’iw omikwaaban. Ani-zaagewed giigido a’aw gichi-nika: “Naanabozhoo zaagewed. Ozhimoyok, gegoo giga-i’igonaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so in course of time he was upon his way. While Nänabushu was travelling about, he soon came out upon a brook; he followed its course till at last it (opened out into) a large river. Then presently he came in sight of a lake, very beautiful was the place where the river flowed out (into the lake). While Nänabushu was looking about, (he saw) an island of sand; he saw some Goslings, very numerous were they. Nänabushu, simpleton that he was, went running out towards the land, he went to get some balsam-boughs; in his old soiled blanket he wrapped them. He made a pack of the balsams, a very heavy pack did the balsams make. And so when he started on his way, he had his hands on the tump-line (running from the forehead back over the shoulders). When he came into view (round a point of land), up spoke a big Goose: “Nänabushu is coming into view (round the point). Do you flee, for something shall we be told.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa go i’iw baapish (and so it was at that time) ∙ ani-maajaanid (as he set off moving along). Naanabozhoo ani-babimosed (Nänabushu, as he walked along) ∙ zhigwa dash omadaabii ziibiins (then he came out upon a little river); ∙ odani-babimaa’adoon (he followed it along) ∙ gegapii igo michaanig i’iw ziibi (and big indeed grew that river). Zhigwa dash odebaabandaan i’iw zaaga’igan (then he came in sight of the lake), ∙ aapiji onizhishinini imaa wenji-zaagidawaanig i’iw ziibi (very lovely was the place where the river flowed out). Inaabid Naanabozhoo minisi-odaawangaanig (as Nänabushu looked, an island of sand); ∙ owaabamaan niikaansan (he saw the goslings), ∙ aapijisa baatiininowan (very many were they). Naanabozhoo wegonen-iwinen (Nänabushu, foolish one), ∙ naadagaameba’too (he ran off toward shore), ∙ odawii-odaapinaan iniw zhingobiin (he wanted to take those balsam boughs); ∙ okonaazhishing* *ogashkiiweginaan* (in his soiled blanket he wrapped them — stems flagged). Ozhiwanike iw zhingobii (he made a pack of the balsam), ∙ aapijisa obwaawanenan* iw zhingobii (very heavy was that balsam pack). Mii dash i’iw ani-izhi-maajaad (and so as he set off) ∙ odani-aasoonan i’iw omikwaaban (he leaned forward on his tump-line). Ani-zaagewed giigido a’aw gichi-nika (as he came into view, the big goose said): “Naanabozhoo zaagewed (Nänabushu is coming into view). ∙ Ozhimoyok (flee, you all), ∙ gegoo giga-i’igonaan (something he will tell us).”</span>

*(1) For other versions see Nos. 11 and 48.*

<span class="l-orig">(2) Kāwīndạc wīn pạsigu∙u∙sīwạg īgi´ᵘ ni‘känsạg. Kuniginin, ogạnōnigōwān Nänabucōwạn: “Kägä‘tsagu nintinigāwạgäntạm ānuwâbạmagwānin īgi´ᵘ nicīmäyạg. Ạmbäsạnō kanīmi∙i∙ninim. Mī∙o∙mā tcibạgitōndạmān īni´ᵘ nagạmunạn. Ninga∙u∙ci‘tōn ‘iᵉi´mā kätacitāyān tcinīmi∙i∙nạgu‘k. Ta∙u∙nicicin nīmi∙i∙tīwigạmik kä∙u∙ci‘tōyān.” Nänabucu mīsa cigwa mādci‘tād uci‘tōd, cingubī wâkā‘kināt; tcitägu pācitcipạgisusinik mī´∙i∙ᵘ äpītā‘tōd ‘i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ unīmi∙i∙tīwigạmig.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin dash wiin bazigwii-siiwag igiw niikaansag. Goniginiin, oganoonigowaan Naanabozhoowan: “Geget sa go nindani-gaawendam *anoo-waabamagwaanin* igiw nishiimeyag. Geget iginin baapinaakamigad wendoosaayaan; mii sa iwidi niimi’idiiwaad anishinaabeg. Gagwaanisagimaaminwaa’igaadeniwan iniw nagamonan negamowaadin. Mii sa go naa onow *beemoondaman*-in iniw nagamonan. Ambesano ga-nimi’ininim. Mii omaa ji-bagidoondamaan iniw nagamonan. Ninga-ozhi’toon i’imaa ge-dazhitaayaan ji-niimi’inagook. Da-onizhishin niimi’idiiwigamig ge-ozhi’tooyaan.” Naanabozhoo mii sa zhigwa maajii’taad ozhi’tood, zhingobii waakaa’kinad; ji-de go *baashijibagizosinik* mii’iw ebiidaatood i’iw oniimi’idiiwigamig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*But the Goslings did not fly away. Lo, they were addressed by Nänabushu saying: “Truly am I sad at heart whenever I fail to see my little brothers. Now, truly a merry time is going at the place from whence I come; for at that place are the people dancing together. Wonderfully good are the songs that they sing. Pray, let me make you dance. Now, these that I have on my back are those very songs. And down at this spot will I lay the songs. I will fix a place yonder where I will devote (myself) to making you dance. Handsome will be the dance-lodge that I will make.” Nänabushu thereupon set to work making it, with balsams he made a stockade; at only such a height that it could not be taken at a leap was how high he made his dance-lodge.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin dash wiin bazigwii-siiwag igiw niikaansag (but those goslings did not fly up). Goniginiin (lo, behold), ∙ oganoonigowaan Naanabozhoowan (they were spoken to by Nänabushu): “Geget sa go nindani-gaawendam (truly I am made sad) ∙ *anoo-waabamagwaanin* igiw nishiimeyag (whenever I fail to see those, my little brothers — stem flagged). Geget iginin baapinaakamigad wendoosaayaan (truly it is the day-event for which I have come); ∙ mii sa iwidi niimi’idiiwaad anishinaabeg (it is yonder the people dance). Gagwaanisagi-maaminwaa’igaadeniwan iniw nagamonan negamowaadin (wonderfully well-fashioned are the songs when they sing them). Mii sa go naa onow *beemoondaman*-in iniw nagamonan (it is these songs that I carry — stem flagged). Ambesano ga-nimi’ininim (come now, I will sing-dance for you). Mii omaa ji-bagidoondamaan iniw nagamonan (right here I will set down these songs). Ninga-ozhi’toon i’imaa ge-dazhitaayaan (I will make a place where you will perform) ∙ ji-niimi’inagook (so that I dance you all). Da-onizhishin niimi’idiiwigamig ge-ozhi’tooyaan (lovely will be the dance-lodge I make).” Naanabozhoo mii sa zhigwa maajii’taad ozhi’tood (Nänabushu then began making it), ∙ zhingobii waakaa’kinad (bending balsams around it); ∙ ji-de go *baashijibagizosinik* (just so it could not be leaped over — stem flagged) ∙ mii’iw ebiidaatood i’iw oniimi’idiiwigamig (that was how high he built his dance-lodge).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) “Mī´∙i∙ᵘ cigwa tcimādci‘taiyạng tcinīmi∙i∙tiyạnk. Ạmbäsa, mī´∙i∙ᵘ ici∙a∙gwā‘taiyu‘k.” Kägä‘t ni‘känsạg agwä‘tāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii iw zhigwa ji-maajii’taayang ji-niimi’idiyang. Ambesa, mii iw izhi-agwaataayok.” Geget niikaansag agwe’taawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“It is now time for us to begin dancing together. Hither, now come you out of the water.” To be sure, the Goslings came out of the water.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii iw zhigwa ji-maajii’taayang ji-niimi’idiyang (it is now time for us to begin to dance together). ∙ Ambesa (come now), ∙ mii iw izhi-agwaataayok (come you all up out of the water).” Geget niikaansag agwe’taawag (truly the goslings came up out of the water).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) “Ạmbäsạnō, kawīndạmōninim kä∙a∙ī∙cīyäg. Mīgū i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ käni∙i∙nạ∙a∙mān a∙ī∙cīyu‘k ‘i∙ᵉi´∙ᵘ tcinīmiyäg. Ayangwāmisiyu‘k. Kägu´ wīwạnīwisi‘kägun. Mīsạgu iᵘ cigwa tcipīndigäyäk ‘iᵉi´mā nīmi∙i∙tīwigạmigunk.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ambesano, ga-wiindamooninim ge-a-izhiiyeg*. Mii go i’iw ge-ni-ina’amaan a-izhiiyok* i’iw ji-niimiyeg. Ayaangwaamisiyok. Gego wiiwaniiwisikegon. Mii sa go iw zhigwa ji-biindigeyeg i’imaa niimi’idiiwigamigong.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Harken! I will tell you how you are to act. According as I sing, so do you when you dance. Do you take pains. Don’t you fail in anything. Now is the time for you to enter into the dance-lodge.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ambesano (come now), ∙ ga-wiindamooninim ge-a-izhiiyeg* (I will tell you what you are to do). ∙ Mii go i’iw ge-ni-ina’amaan (just as I beat the song along) ∙ a-izhiiyok* i’iw ji-niimiyeg (so do you when you dance). ∙ Ayaangwaamisiyok (take care, you all). ∙ Gego wiiwaniiwisikegon (don’t any of you fail). ∙ Mii sa go iw zhigwa ji-biindigeyeg i’imaa niimi’idiiwigamigong (now is the time for you to enter into the dance-lodge).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Äjipīndigäwāt ni‘känsạg, kayä wīn māng; kayä wīn dạc cingibis äjipīndigät. Ka‘kina kā‘pīndigänit Nänabucu ugīpa∙ā∙n udickwāndäm. Cigwa´ Nänabucu wīmādcī∙ạ∙m a‘kawä mīnawā ugạnōnāᵉ: “Mīgu iᵘ känia∙ī∙na∙ạ∙mān, a∙ī∙cīyu‘k. A‘pīdạc pāsinawändisuyān mī∙a∙‘pī käpasigwīyān. Mī wīnigu iᵘ nạnāsawaya∙ī∙ käbabātạcicimoyān. Mīsa cigwa tcimādcī∙ạ∙mān:”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-biindigewaad niikaansag, gaye wiin maang; gaye wiin dash zhingibis ezhi-biindiged. Gakina gaa-biindigenid Naanabozhoo ogii-baa’aan odishkwaandem. Zhigwa Naanabozhoo wii-maaji-am aakawe miinawaa oganoonaan: “Mii go iw ge-ni-ana’amaan, a-izhiiyok*. Apii dash baasinawendisoyaan mii apii ge-bazigwiiyaan. Mii wiin igo iw *nanaasawaya’ii* ge-babaa-dazhi-shimoyaan. Mii sa zhigwa ji-maajii-amaan:”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then in went the Goslings, likewise the Loon; and also the Diver entered in. When all had gone inside, Nänabushu closed up the doorway. Now, Nänabushu was about to sing, but first he addressed them again, saying: “Therefore according to what I say in my song, that you do. And when I become thrilled, then shall I rise to my feet. Thereupon in amongst you shall I dance. It is time for me now to begin singing:”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-biindigewaad niikaansag (so in went the goslings), ∙ gaye wiin maang (and the loon too); ∙ gaye wiin dash zhingibis ezhi-biindiged (and the diver too went in). Gakina gaa-biindigenid Naanabozhoo ogii-baa’aan odishkwaandem (when all had entered, Nänabushu closed up his doorway). Zhigwa Naanabozhoo wii-maaji-am (now Nänabushu was about to start the song) ∙ aakawe miinawaa oganoonaan (first he spoke to them again): “Mii go iw ge-ni-ana’amaan (so just as I sound the song along), ∙ a-izhiiyok* (do you so). ∙ Apii dash baasinawendisoyaan (and when I become carried away) ∙ mii apii ge-bazigwiiyaan (then is when I will rise up). ∙ Mii wiin igo iw *nanaasawaya’ii* ge-babaa-dazhi-shimoyaan (it is just so, side to side, that I will dance about — stem flagged). ∙ Mii sa zhigwa ji-maajii-amaan (now then it is time for me to begin the song):”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Pạsạngwābicimowinạn nīnpīdōnạn, nicīmisitug! Nạbanägātäcimowinạn, nicimisitug, ninpītōnạn.”</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“A dance with the eyes closed do I fetch! / A dance upon one leg, O my little brothers do I fetch.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Pasangwaabishimowinan nimbiidoonan (a closed-eye dance I bring), ∙ nishiimisidog (O my little brothers)! / Nabanegaadeshimowinan (a one-legged dance), ∙ nishiimisidog (O my little brothers), ∙ nimbiidoonan (I bring).” ⟨song carried verbatim⟩</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash zhigwa aapiji ojiikimaan*, mii sa go geget gakina pasangwaabishimonid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon then were they much thrilled with the song, and so all of course danced with their eyes closed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash zhigwa aapiji ojiikimaan* (and so then he stirred them deeply), ∙ mii sa go geget gakina pasangwaabishimonid (and truly all of them danced with closed eyes).</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Of course, up close together came their necks, while at the same time they were dancing.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget ayaansigwe’taawag (truly their necks came up close together) ∙ bekish niiminid (at the same time as they danced).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Nänabucu äjikīgitut: “Ä‘ e´ⁱ, mīcigwa pāsinạwänintisuyān!” Äjipasigwīt Nänabucu pạpānīmi, Nänabucu udạninātcicimu‘tawāᵉ. <span class="l-eng">*Nänabushu then spoke up: “Behold, now am I in a transport of delight!” Rising to his feet, Nänabushu moved about, dancing here and there, approaching (the Goslings) while he danced. <span class="l-gloss">Naanabozhoo ezhi-giigidod (Nänabushu then spoke): “A’a’ii (behold!), ∙ mii zhigwa baasinawenindizoyaan (now I am carried away with delight)!” Ezhi-bazigwiid Naanabozhoo babaa-niimi (rising up, Nänabushu danced about), ∙ Naanabozhoo odani-naajishimotawaan (Nänabushu went dancing toward them). Ishkwaandeng iwidi dazhi-shimo a’aw maang (by the doorway yonder that loon was dancing). ∙ Indigo nandago* omadwegwewebinaa (it seemed as though he heard the sound of the flinging — *nandago* flagged), ∙ iw nitam maang (that first one, the loon). ∙ Wegonen-iwinen bangii (foolishly, a little).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) äjitōckābit ä‘tawā, inäbit māng, na∙i∙tāg ubimibō‘kugwänāni Nänabucōwạn ni‘känsaᵉ. Äjikīgitut māng: “Ä‘ēᵉeⁱ, Nänabucu kidickwạnigunān!” Mīsa´ a‘kawä ä‘kitut māng, mīgu iᵘ ānawitäbācitcipạgisut.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-doshkaabid etawaa, inaabid maang, naa-itaa obimi-bookogwenaani Naanabozhoowan niikaansan. Ezhi-giigidod maang: “Aa’ii, Naanabozhoo gidishkwaaninigonaan!” Mii sa aakawe ekidod maang, mii go iw aanawi-debaashi-jibagizod.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*By the doorway yonder the Loon was dancing. Now, it seemed as if he heard the sound of the breaking of necks, thus it seemed to the Loon. Happening to open his eyes a little, why, when the Loon looked, it was to see Nänabushu at just the time when he was among the Goslings breaking their necks. Then up spoke the Loon: “Look out! by Nänabushu are we being killed off.” Now, when the Loon first spoke, he then cleared (the balsam enclosure), just barely getting over.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-doshkaabid etawaa (as he opened his eyes a little), ∙ inaabid maang (the loon looked), ∙ naa-itaa obimi-bookogwenaani Naanabozhoowan niikaansan (and lo, Nänabushu was breaking the necks of the goslings). Ezhi-giigidod maang (the loon spoke up): “Aa’ii (look out!), ∙ Naanabozhoo gidishkwaaninigonaan (Nänabushu is finishing us off)!” Mii sa aakawe ekidod maang (so as soon as the loon said it), ∙ mii go iw aanawi-debaashi-jibagizod (he just barely cleared the place, getting out).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Ā‘tāᵃ, Nänabucu mīwạnigu iniwäti māngwạn nwā‘pinạnāt. ‘Tā, māng ānupapisingwa∙i∙gät.
