<span class="l-fiero">**Nenabozho Animaajaad Owiijikiwenyan, Kaye Ogookomisan**</span>

Nänabushu leaves his Brother, and also his Grandmother.

<span class="l-eng">*But Nänabushu, on his part, only gave a laugh; as he went he observed, and as he went he was at the same time seeking for some kind of a log to lay across the stream so that they might be able to cross.
