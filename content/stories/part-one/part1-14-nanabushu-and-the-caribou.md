14\. NÄNABUSHU AND THE CARIBOU.

Nanabushu and the Caribou (1)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Wâsagunäckạnk — Series 1 (Nos. 1–18), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Mīänicmāmādcāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii eni-izhi-maamaajaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he continued steadily on his way.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii eni-izhi-maamaajaad (thereupon he kept right on moving along).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Ningutingigu ạnipạpimusät, tcimicawaskusiwagāni wäditạnk.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo ani-babimosed, ji-mishawashkosiwagaani wedidang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once while he was walking along, to a wide field of high grass he came.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo ani-babimosed (now, once as he was walking along), ji-mishawashkosiwagaani wedidang (he came to where there was a wide field of high grass).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Äji∙i∙nābit i∙i∙witi wayä ‘kwāckusi’ᵘ tci∙a∙yābä ati‘kwạn kīpimatābīckusiwäwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-inaabid i’iwidi wayekwaashkosiw gichi-ayaabe adikwan gii-pimadaabiishkosiwewan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*As he looked toward the other end of the meadow, (he saw) a big bull Caribou come walking out upon the stretch of grass,*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-inaabid i’iwidi wayekwaashkosiw (as he looked off to the far end of the meadow), gichi-ayaabe adikwan (a big bull caribou) gii-pimadaabiishkosiwewan (came walking out along the grass).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Kägätsa umisawīnawān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget sa omisawenimaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*and so he (Nänabushu) wanted to get him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget sa omisawenimaan (and truly he wanted him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Ạmạntcigic kä∙i∙nā‘pinanāwạgipanän?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Amanj igo’sh ge-inaapinanaawagipanen?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Wonder how can I get hold of him!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Amanj igo’sh ge-inaapinanaawagipanen?” (“wonder how I could get hold of him!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Äciwâbạmigut, “Intacītug käpasämit,” inändạm ‘aᵘ ạdi‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-waabamigod, “Indash iidog ge-pasemid,” inendam aw adik.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When (Nänabushu) was seen, “Without fail he will have something to say to me,” thought the Caribou.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-waabamigod (when he was seen by him), “Indash iidog ge-pasemid,” (“without fail he will have something to say to me,”) inendam aw adik (thought the caribou).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) “Tạgạntawā ninga∙a∙cimā,” ạnimādcība‘tō ‘aᵉa’ᵘ ạdi‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Daga ndawaa ninga-ashimaa,” ani-maajiibatoo a’aw adik.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“I think I will draw him on.” Off running started the Caribou.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Daga ndawaa ninga-ashimaa,” (“come, I think I will draw him on,”) ani-maajiibatoo a’aw adik (off running started the caribou).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Cigwa kägä‘t umadwäkạnōnigōn: “‘Ai, nicīmisa’, amantcwīn ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kītōtạmạn ningutci wayāpaminānin?</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa geget omadwe-kanoonigoon: “Ai, ni-shiimisa, amanj wiin i’iw gii-doodaman ningoji wayaabaminaanin?**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then truly the voice of Nänabushu was heard saying to him: “Hey, my little brother, would that I knew why you act so whenever I see you anywhere!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa geget omadwe-kanoonigoon (then truly the voice of the other was heard calling to him): “Ai, ni-shiimisa, (“hey, my little brother,) amanj wiin i’iw gii-doodaman (wonder why you do that) ningoji wayaabaminaanin? (wherever I see you?)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) A‘kawä, kīwitibātcimo‘tạwin!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Akawe, giwii-dibaajimotawin!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Wait, I wish to tell you something!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Akawe, giwii-dibaajimotawin! (wait, I want to tell you something!)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Kägä‘t ki‘tci∙i∙nakamigạt o∙ō∙witi wäntusäyān,” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget gichi-inakamigad o’owidi wendoseyaan,” odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Truly, a great time is going on over there from whence I have come,” he said to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget gichi-inakamigad o’owidi wendoseyaan,” (“truly a great event is going on over there where I have come from,”) odinaan (he said to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) “Awawa, undās, pīcān!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Awawa, ondaas, bi-izhaan!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Ay, hither, come here!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Awawa, ondaas, bi-izhaan! (“come now, hither, come here!)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Kawīn kītānanīsānänimisī.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin gidaa-naniizaanenimisii.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*You have no cause to fear me.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin gidaa-naniizaanenimisii.” (you have no need to fear me.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Mīdạc kägä‘t kā‘pīji∙i∙cānit i∙i∙māⁿ ayāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget gaa-bi-izhi-izhaanid i’imaa ayaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*It was true that he went to where (Nänabushu) was.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget gaa-bi-izhi-izhaanid (and so truly he went over) i’imaa ayaad (to where the other was).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) “Tiwä’, kägä‘tigu tci∙i∙na‘kamigạt pitcīnāgu, kīnisitiwạg; ki‘tciwâwīyạg kī∙u∙ndcinạnitiwạg; kipickwạ‘tagīnu‘tātiwạg.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Diwe, geget igo gichi-inakamigad bijiinaago, gii-nisidiwag; gichi-waawiiyag gii-onji-nanidiwag; gipishkwatagiinodaadiwag.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Ah, but there truly was a great time going on yesterday, they were killing one another; for no reason were they slaying one another; they shot one another indiscriminately with arrows.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Diwe, geget igo gichi-inakamigad bijiinaago, (“why, truly a great thing went on yesterday,) gii-nisidiwag; (they were killing one another;) gichi-waawiiyag gii-onji-nanidiwag; (for no real reason they were slaying one another;) gipishkwatagiinodaadiwag.” (they shot one another every which way.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Ānīc mīgu∙i∙māⁿ äpītcitanātcimu‘tawāt äci∙i∙‘kwa‘täckawāt īni’ᵘ umi‘tigwābīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish mii go imaa epiichi-danaajimotawaad ezhi-ikwateshkawaad iniw omitigwaabiin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, while he was telling him the story, he was stringing his bow.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish mii go imaa epiichi-danaajimotawaad (now, while he was telling him the story there) ezhi-ikwateshkawaad iniw omitigwaabiin (he was stringing his bow).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) “O∙ō∙wisagu tōtamōbạnīg.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“O’ow isa go doodamobaniig.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“This was the very way they did at the time.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“O’ow isa go doodamobaniig.” (“this was the very way they did it.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) Ä‘cinạnimā‘kwīnōtawāt i∙i∙māⁿ wācä‘pīnit.¹</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-nanimaakwiinoodawaad i’imaa waashepiinid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*All the while he kept aiming there at his (2) side.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-nanimaakwiinoodawaad (meanwhile he kept aiming at him) i’imaa waashepiinid (there at his side).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) “Mīsa wuᵉo’ äntōtamowā‘pạn,” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii sa o’ow endoodamowaapan,” odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“This was just the way they did,” he said to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii sa o’ow endoodamowaapan,” (“this was just the way they did,”) odinaan (he said to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Äcipimwāt iimāⁿ wācä‘pīnit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-bimwaad i’imaa waashepiinid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then he shot him in the side.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-bimwaad i’imaa waashepiinid (then he shot him there in the side).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) “Mîcạnîm Nänabucu!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mishanim Nenaboozhoo!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Confound Nänabushu!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mishanim Nenaboozhoo! (“confound Nanabushu!)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Kägä‘t ạnạgīnänimạgibạn.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget aana gii-nenimagiban.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Truly, that was the very thing I thought he would do.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget aana gii-nenimagiban.” (truly, that was just what I thought he would do.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Ānīc mīsa’ kā∙i∙cinisāt, äcimātcī‘kawāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish mii sa gaa-izhi-nisaad, ezhi-maajiikawaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So after he had killed (the Caribou), he then set to work flaying (and) cutting him up.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish mii sa gaa-izhi-nisaad (so after he had killed him), ezhi-maajiikawaad (he set to work skinning and cutting him up).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Papänạginạngä i‘kāmōwạn; äcipạpimagōtōt īni’ᵘ uwīninōmạn; äcigạpācimāt ka‘kina.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bapenaginange ikaamoowan; ezhi-pabimagoodood iniw owiininoman; ezhi-gabaashimaad gakina.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Exceedingly fat was (the Caribou); then he went (and) hung up his fat; then he boiled it all.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bapenaginange ikaamoowan (exceedingly fat was he); ezhi-pabimagoodood iniw owiininoman (then he went and hung up his fat); ezhi-gabaashimaad gakina (then he boiled all of it).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) Kā∙i∙cikīziswāt, ugītackwämạg ugī∙ạ∙gwāwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-izhi-giizizwaad, ogiidashkwemag ogii-agwaawaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After he had finished cooking it, then out upon a sheet of birch-bark he dipped the meat.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-izhi-giizizwaad (after he had finished cooking it), ogiidashkwemag ogii-agwaawaan (he dipped the meat out onto a sheet of birch-bark).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Mīdạc kā∙i∙‘kitut a‘pī kā∙u∙nabī‘tawāt: “Tibigic käuntạmāwạgän?” kī∙i∙‘kitō.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gaa-ikidod apii gaa-onabiitawaad: “Dibigizh ge-ondamaawagen?” gii-ikido.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And this was what he said when he came to where it lay: “Wonder from what part of the body I shall take (what I am to eat)!” he said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gaa-ikidod apii gaa-onabiitawaad (and this is what he said when he sat down to it): “Dibigizh ge-ondamaawagen?” (“wonder from what part I shall take my eating!”) gii-ikido (he said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) “Intawāsa uctigwāning ninga∙u∙ntạmā.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Indawaa sa oshtigwaaning ninga-ondamaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“I think that from the head I will take what I am to eat of him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Indawaa sa oshtigwaaning ninga-ondamaa. (“I think I will eat from the head.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Kāmāwīn nintāminu‘tcigäsī, nintāpā‘pī∙i∙gōg nīnimucäⁿyạg² ugī∙a∙cäwābạmān,” gī∙i∙nändạm.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa maawiin nindaa-minochigesii, nindaa-paapi’igoog niinimooshenyag (3) ogii-ashewebamaan,” gii-inendam.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Perhaps it would not be proper for me to do that, for I should be made fun of by my loves (3) on account of my having begun at the opposite end,” (thus) he thought.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa maawiin nindaa-minochigesii, (perhaps it would not be right for me to do that,) nindaa-paapi’igoog niinimooshenyag (I would be laughed at by my loves) ogii-ashewebamaan,” (for starting at the wrong end,”) gii-inendam (he thought).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) “Nindābā‘pi∙i∙gōg nīnimōcäⁿyạg,” kīnändạm.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nindaa-baapi’igoog niinimooshenyag,” gii-nendam.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“I should be laughed at by my loves,” (thus) he thought.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nindaa-baapi’igoog niinimooshenyag,” (“I would be laughed at by my loves,”) gii-nendam (he thought).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) “Ạmạntcisawīn i∙i∙witi upi‘kwạnāng undạmạk?</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Amanj isa wiin i’iwidi obikwanaang ondamag?**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Wonder if it would be well for me to eat of him at the back!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Amanj isa wiin i’iwidi obikwanaang ondamag? (“wonder if it would be well to eat of him at the back?)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Kāwīnsa, kạnạbạtc nindābā‘pi∙i∙gōg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin sa, ganabaj nindaa-baapi’igoog.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*No, indeed, for probably I should then be laughed at.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin sa, ganabaj nindaa-baapi’igoog. (no indeed, for probably I would be laughed at.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Kạnạbạtc ugīkāntcwäbamān ki‘tciayābä∙ạ∙di‘kwạn, nindā∙i∙gōg māwīn nīnimucäⁿyạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ganabaj ogii-kaanjwebamaan gichi-ayaabe-adikwan, nindaa-igoog maawiin niinimoshenyag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*‘Perhaps he shoved the big bull Caribou forward while eating upon him,’ would be said of me perhaps by my loves.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ganabaj ogii-kaanjwebamaan gichi-ayaabe-adikwan, (‘perhaps he shoved the big bull caribou forward while eating on him,’) nindaa-igoog maawiin niinimoshenyag. (would be said of me perhaps by my loves.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Ạmạntcisawīn pimi‘tcaya∙ī∙undạmạk?” kī∙i∙nändạm.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Amanj isa wiin bimichaya’ii ondamag?” gii-inendam.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Wonder if it would be well for me to eat of him at the side!” (thus) he thought.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Amanj isa wiin bimichaya’ii ondamag?” (wonder if it would be well to eat of him at the side?”) gii-inendam (he thought).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) “Kāwīnsa, kạnạbạtc nindāpā‘pī∙‘i∙gōk.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gaawiin sa, ganabaj nindaa-paabi’igoog.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“No indeed, for perhaps I should be laughed at.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaawiin sa, ganabaj nindaa-paabi’igoog. (“no indeed, for perhaps I would be laughed at.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Ugīpimitcwäbamān nindā∙i∙gōk ki‘tci∙ā∙yābä∙ạ∙di‘kwạn, nīnimōcäⁿyạg nindā∙i∙gōg.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-pimijwebamaan nindaa-igoog gichi-ayaabe-adikwan, niinimooshenyag nindaa-igoog.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*‘He tried to push a great bull caribou sidewise when eating upon him,’ would be said of me by my loves.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-pimijwebamaan nindaa-igoog gichi-ayaabe-adikwan, (‘he pushed a great bull caribou sidewise while eating on him,’ would be said of me,) niinimooshenyag nindaa-igoog.” (it would be said of me by my loves.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) Mägwāgu tạnwäwä‘tōd mīsīwä tcīgā‘tig ugībạbā∙a∙‘tōn umạma∙i∙bimān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Megwaa go danwewetood miziwe jiigaatig ogii-babaa-atoon omama’ibimaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And while engaged in this talk, he laid round about the foot of a tree all the grease (which he had boiled down).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Megwaa go danwewetood (and while he kept talking away) miziwe jiigaatig ogii-babaa-atoon omama’ibimaan (he went about laying all his rendered grease around the foot of a tree).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) Mīdạc ạ‘pī äjinōndāgwatinig kisībā‘kwạt, “Kägä‘tsa ningi‘tcisasiskimigun.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash apii ezhi-noondaagwadinig giziibaakwad, “Geget sa nin-gichi-sasiskimigon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so when the creaking of trees rubbing (together) was heard, “Truly by some one am I sought beseechingly.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash apii ezhi-noondaagwadinig giziibaakwad, (and so when the creaking of the trees was heard,) “Geget sa nin-gichi-sasiskimigon. (“truly someone is beseeching me hard.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) Kạnạbạtcsa kayä wīn wīwīsini.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ganabaj sa gaye wiin wii-wiisini.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Perhaps, indeed, some one too wants to eat.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ganabaj sa gaye wiin wii-wiisini.” (perhaps it too wants to eat.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Kāpa‘kwäcwāt i∙i∙māⁿ uwīninōwinit, äci∙a∙‘kwāntawät.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-pakwezhwaad i’imaa owiininoowinid, ezhi-akwaandawed.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After slicing off a piece from a fatty part (of the caribou), then up the tree he climbed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-pakwezhwaad i’imaa owiininoowinid (after slicing off a piece from a fatty part of him), ezhi-akwaandawed (then up the tree he climbed).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) Mīdạc iwiti awipōsinạng;¹ kayägu wīninōn ugīsindā‘kwicimān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash iwidi awi-poozinang; (4) gaye go wiininoon ogiziindaakwishimaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so there he went and placed the fat; (4) and he put the fat in where it was creaking.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash iwidi awi-poozinang; (and so there he went and set the fat;) gaye go wiininoon ogiziindaakwishimaan (and he wedged the fat into where it was creaking).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) Äciki‘tcipītānimạdînik, äcita‘kwạmigut ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kisībā‘kwat.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-gichi-piidaanimadinig, ezhi-dakwamigod i’iw giziibaakwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When a great gust of wind came up, he was then caught fast by the creaking tree.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-gichi-piidaanimadinig (when a great gust of wind came up), ezhi-dakwamigod i’iw giziibaakwad (he was caught fast by the creaking tree).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) Aⁱ, mīsa imāⁿ ä∙i∙ntanagōsit!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ay, mii sa imaa e-indanagoozid!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Ah, and so there he hung!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ay, mii sa imaa e-indanagoozid! (ah, and so there he hung!)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(42) Pīnicigu kabäya∙ī∙ ạgōsi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Biinish igo gabeya’ii agoozi.**</span>
