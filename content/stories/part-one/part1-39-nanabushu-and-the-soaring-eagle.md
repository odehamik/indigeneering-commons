39\. NÄNABUSHU AND SOARING-EAGLE.

Nanabushu and the Soaring Eagle

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Wâsagunäckạnk — Series 4 (No. 39), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa pane gaawiin gegoo oga-miijisiin megiziwaash (and so nothing at all would Soaring-Eagle have to eat).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniish ge-diyaang?” (“what will become of us?”)</span>
