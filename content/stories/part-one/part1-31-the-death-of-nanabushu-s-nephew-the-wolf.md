31\. THE DEATH OF NÄNABUSHU’S NEPHEW, THE WOLF.¹

The Death of Nenabosho’s Nephew, the Wolf (2)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by probably Midāsugaⁿj (Michelson’s own hedge, carried) — Series 2 (Nos. 19–32), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ā‘tayā, kägä‘t minwäntạm Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aatayaa, geget minwendam Nenabosho.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Ah, truly pleased was Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aatayaa (ah!), geget minwendam Nenabosho (truly pleased was Nenabosho).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Wayābạninig kusiwaᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wayaabaninig goziwag(i).**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*On the morrow they moved camp.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wayaabaninig (on the next morning) goziwag (they moved camp).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Päcigigu utōcimạn kayä wīnawā ingutcigu icimādcāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bezhig igo odoozhiman gaye wiinawaa ingoji igo izhi-maajaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And with one of his nephews he went off in another direction.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bezhig igo odoozhiman (one of his nephews) gaye wiinawaa (along with them) ingoji igo izhi-maajaawag (off in another direction they went).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Ugạnōnigōn īni’ᵘ udōcimạn: “Nänabucu, päcugu kīganibāmin.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Oganoonigoon iniw(ii) odoozhiman: “Nenabosho, besho go giga-nibaamin.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He was addressed by his nephew saying: “Nänabushu, not far away shall we stop for the night.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Oganoonigoon iniw odoozhiman (he was addressed by his nephew): “Nenabosho, besho go giga-nibaamin (“Nenabosho, not far off shall we sleep for the night.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Misa’ ä∙i∙na∙ạ∙nāt udōcimạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa ena’anaad odoozhiman.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so he followed in the tracks of his nephew.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa ena’anaad odoozhiman (and so he followed the trail of his nephew).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Kumāgu a‘pī cigwa owâbạmān ạni‘täwạnini‘k nạmạdạbinit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gomaa go apii zhigwa owaabamaan anidewaninig*(iii) namadabinid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Some distance on the way he saw (his nephew) seated in a spot free from snow.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gomaa go apii zhigwa (some distance along by now) owaabamaan anidewaninig namadabinid (he saw him sitting in a spot bare of snow).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) “Nōs, mī∙o∙mā tcinibaiyạnk,” Nänabucu udigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Noos, mii omaa ji-nibaaying,” Nenabosho odigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My father, here is a place for us to sleep,” Nänabushu was told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Noos (“my father), mii omaa ji-nibaaying (here is where we will sleep),” Nenabosho odigoon (Nenabosho was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Kā∙i∙skwāwīsiniwāt, awäningwāmi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-ishkwaa-wiisiniwaad, aweningwaami.***</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After they had finished eating, he made ready to sleep.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-ishkwaa-wiisiniwaad (after they had finished eating), aweningwaami (he settled in to sleep).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Kīciginibāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-zhi-gii-nibaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He then had a nap.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-zhi-gii-nibaad (then he had a sleep).</span>

*Page verified 2026-08-12 at 14× (printed p.250): Kīciginibāt — the gi IS printed; the shorter reading gii-zhi-nibaad is refuted. The segmentation (zhi-gii vs zhigi-) is a parse question for the lexicon stage; the letters are settled.*

<span class="l-orig">(10) Mägwāgu nạnāmạdạbit pāmāgu wändcimawinit Nänabucōwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Megwaa go nanaamadabid baamaa go wenjimawinid Nenaboshoowan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And while (the Wolf) was yet sitting up, all of a sudden into weeping burst Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Megwaa go nanaamadabid (while he was still sitting up) baamaa go wenjimawinid Nenaboshoowan (all at once Nenabosho burst out weeping).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Ajidcīcābināt. “Cī,</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Azhijiizhaabinaad.***</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then he waved to him with the hand.(2)*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Azhijiizhaabinaad (then he waved him off with his hand).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) indạskā nīn nimānābạmigutug,” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Shii, indashkaa niin nimaanaabamigodog,”(iv) odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Foh, (I) fancy that he probably is having a bad dream about me,” he said of him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Shii (“foh), indashkaa niin nimaanaabamigodog (I suppose he is having a bad dream about me),” odinaan (he said of him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Cigwa tayänibānit, kuskusiwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa dayenibaanid,(v) goshkoziwan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When (Nänabushu) had sleep enough, he then woke up.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa dayenibaanid (when he had slept enough), goshkoziwan (he woke up).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Ānīc, tana‘kạmigisi ‘aᵉa’ᵘ ma∙ī∙ngạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish, danakamigizi a’aw ma’iingan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, busy at work was the Wolf.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish, danakamigizi a’aw ma’iingan (now, busy at work was the wolf).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) “Ānīn änābạndạmạn kīmawiyạn?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniin enaabandaman(vi) gii-mawiyan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“What were you dreaming about, that you should weep?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniin enaabandaman (“what were you dreaming) gii-mawiyan? (that you wept?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) “Ä‘tawā, nindōcim kimānābạmin. Wâbạng ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mōⁿs wī‘piminicawạt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Edawaa, nindoozhim gi-maanaabamin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Ah, my nephew, I had a bad dream about you.  
To-morrow you will pursue a moose.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Edawaa (“alas), nindoozhim (my nephew) gi-maanaabamin (I had a bad dream about you).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) Ạmbäsạnō, kägu’ ickwā pagitō‘kän ‘iᵉi’ᵘ mi‘tigōns sībīnsing mīsawāgu cigwa täbābạmạt ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mōⁿs;</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ambe sa noo, gego ishkwaa pagidooken i’iw mitigoons ziibiinsing mii sa wa go zhigwa debaabamad a’aw mooz.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Please don’t you delay throwing a stick into the brook, even though you are then in sight of the moose.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Waabang (tomorrow) a’aw mooz wii-bimi-nazhawad (you will go in pursuit of that moose).  
Waabang a’aw mooz wii-bimi-nazhawad.(vii)  
Ambe sa noo (please now), gego ishkwaa pagidooken i’iw mitigoons ziibiinsing (do not put off throwing the little stick into the brook) mii sa wa go zhigwa debaabamad a’aw mooz (even when you have the moose in sight).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) mīgu iᵘ wī∙i∙cimindcimäntạn.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii go iw wii-izhi-minjimendan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, do try to keep that in mind.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii go iw wii-izhi-minjimendan.” (do try to keep that in mind.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Cigwasa’ wâbạnini.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa sa waabanini.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now the morrow was at hand.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa sa waabanini (now the morrow had come).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) Kigicäp mādcāwạn wī‘piminica∙i∙gänit, Nänabucu ạnimādcā, udōcimạn pima∙ạ∙nāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gigizheb maajaawan wii-biminizha’igenid, Nenabosho ani-maajaa, odoozhiman bima’anaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In the morning, when (the Wolf) departed to go in pursuit (of game), Nänabushu set out; in the trail of his nephew he followed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gigizheb maajaawan wii-biminizha’igenid (in the morning he set off to go in pursuit), Nenabosho ani-maajaa (Nenabosho started out), odoozhiman bima’anaad (following along the trail of his nephew).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Cigwasa’ äci‘kawänit ‘iᵉi’ᵘ uci‘kawānigubạnän īni’ᵘ mōⁿsōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa sa ezhikawenid i’iw ozhikawenigobanen(viii) iniw moozoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, by the trail he made, (the Wolf) was stalking the moose.(3)*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa sa ezhikawenid (now, by the trail he had made) i’iw ozhikawenigobanen iniw moozoon (the trail by which he was stalking the moose).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Ānīc, ā‘pidci sīgwạnini.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish aapiji ziigwanini.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*By the way, it was well on towards spring.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish aapiji ziigwanini (by the way, it was well on toward spring).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Mīsa iᵘ ä∙i∙na∙ạ∙nāt kayä mī∙i∙wä iᵘ äcinạmä‘tōnit īni’ᵘ udōcimạn; mi‘tigōns ạni∙a∙‘pagitōnit i∙i∙mā pạngī sībīnsiwa‘kamigānik;</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa iw ena’anaad gaye mii iwe iw ezhi-nametoonid iniw odoozhiman; mitigoons ani-apagidoonid i’imaa bangii ziibiinswakamigaanig;**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*That was how he trailed up (his nephew), and that was how he could  tell by the trail (what) his nephew was doing; (he saw) that (his  nephew) had flung a small stick ahead on going down into the dry bed of a little brook;*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa iw ena’anaad (that was how he followed his trail) gaye mii iwe iw ezhi-nametoonid iniw odoozhiman (and that was how he could tell by the trail what his nephew was doing); mitigoons ani-apagidoonid (a little stick he had flung ahead) i’imaa bangii ziibiinswakamigaanig (down there at the little dry brook-bed);</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) cigwasa täpābạmānigubạn īni’ᵘ mōⁿsōn;</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa sa debaabamaanigoban iiniw moozoon;**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*(He saw) where (the Wolf) had come in sight of the moose at the time;*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa sa debaabamaanigoban iiniw moozoon (where then he had caught sight of the moose);</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) mīdạc kägä‘t ki‘tci änigu‘k mādcānigubạnän īni’ᵘ odōcimạn;</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget gichi-enigok maajaanigobanen iiniw odoozhiman;**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*and then really with great speed was his nephew going at the time;*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget gichi-enigok maajaanigobanen iiniw odoozhiman (and then truly with great speed his nephew had gone);</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) mīdạc ‘iᵉi’ᵘ cigwa täbibinānigubạnän ‘aᵉa’ᵘ ma∙ī∙ngạn ā‘pidci pạngī sībīnsiwa‘kạmigānik.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash i’iw zhigwa debibinaanigobanen(ix) a’aw ma’iingan aapiji bangii ziibiinswakamigaanig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then now was the Wolf overtaking (the moose) at where there was a dry bed of a very small brook.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash i’iw zhigwa debibinaanigobanen a’aw ma’iingan (and then the wolf was overtaking it) aapiji bangii ziibiinswakamigaanig (at the bed of a very small dry brook).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Mīgu’ mīnōtc ācawikwāskwạnigubạnän, kā∙i∙jidiskī‘kāg i∙i’∙ᵘ sībīns, mīdạc imā nānāwạgām kā∙i∙jipạngicink.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii go miinooj aazhawigwaashkwanigobanen, gaa-izhi-dishkiikaag i’iw  ziibiins, mii dash imaa naanaawagaam gaa-izhi-bangishing.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*But in an unguarded moment, when he tried to take it with a leap, apart spread the brook, and so far out yonder in the middle of the stream he fell.(4)*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii go miinooj aazhawigwaashkwanigobanen (but in a careless moment, when he tried to leap across), gaa-izhi-dishkiikaag i’iw ziibiins (the little brook spread apart), mii dash imaa naanaawagaam gaa-izhi-bangishing (and there in the very middle of the stream he fell).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) ‘Pạnägu kāsāswänik īni’ᵘ u‘tawagạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Pane go gaa-saswenig(x) iiniw otawagan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And at once there was ringing in his ears.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Pane go gaa-saswenig iiniw otawagan (and at once there was a ringing in his ears).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) Mīsạgu ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kāwīn kīmōskạmusīg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa go i’iw gaawiin gii-mooshkamosig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then he did not come up to the surface.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa go i’iw gaawiin gii-mooshkamosig (and then he did not come up to the surface).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Ānīc mīsa kīwạnändạnk tci∙a∙‘pagitōd iᵘ mi‘tigōns.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish mii sa gii-wanendang ji-apagidood iw mitigoons.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Well, this was because he had forgotten to throw the little stick (ahead of him).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish mii sa gii-wanendang ji-apagidood iw mitigoons (well, this was because he had forgotten to throw the little stick).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Nänabucu cigwa odäbābạndān sībi pimi‘tigwayānik, ‘pạnä udōcimạn pä‘kubīkawänit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenabosho zhigwa odebaabandaan ziibi bimitigweyaanig, pane odoozhiman bekobiikawenid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nänabushu then came in sight of a river that went flowing by, straight on down to the water he trailed his nephew.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenabosho zhigwa odebaabandaan ziibi bimitigweyaanig (Nenabosho then caught sight of a river flowing by), pane odoozhiman bekobiikawenid (straight down to the water he trailed his nephew).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) “Ä‘tawā! mīmāwīnini∙i∙ᵘ kīnisābāwät ‘aᵉa’ᵘ nindōcim.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Edawaa! Mii maawiin iniw gii-nisaabaawed(xi) a’aw nindoozhim.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Alas! it is possible that that nephew of mine has drowned.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Edawaa! (“alas!) Mii maawiin iniw gii-nisaabaawed a’aw nindoozhim.” (it may be that that nephew of mine has drowned.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) Äni∙i∙ji∙ā∙cawa∙u∙t; miziwä nīzātciwạn ānu∙i∙cā.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eni-izhi-aazhawa’od; miziwe niisaajiwan aano-izhaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then on over to the other side he went; everywhere downstream he went, but in vain.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eni-izhi-aazhawa’od (then he crossed over to the other side); miziwe niisaajiwan aano-izhaa (everywhere downstream he went, but in vain).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Mīsa’ kāwīn inkutci unạmäāsīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa gaawiin ingoji oname’aasiin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so nowhere saw he a sign of him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa gaawiin ingoji oname’aasiin (and so nowhere did he find a trace of him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) Gitci∙ä∙nigu‘k äjimawit Nänabucu; mīsa’ kabägījik pabāmawit, ānunạntunäwāt īni’ᵘ udōcimạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gichi-enigok ezhi-mawid Nenabosho; mii sa gabe-giizhik babaamawid, aano-nandonewaad iiniw odoozhiman.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*With great affliction then wept Nänabushu; whereupon all day long he wandered weeping, as he sought in vain for his nephew.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gichi-enigok ezhi-mawid Nenabosho (with great force then wept Nenabosho); mii sa gabe-giizhik babaamawid (whereupon all day long he wandered about weeping), aano-nandonewaad iiniw odoozhiman (seeking his nephew in vain).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) Skwatci midāsugun ānunạntunä∙i∙gät, mīsa’ kāwīn umi‘kawāsīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Shkwaach midaasogon aano-nandone’iged, mii sa gaawiin omikawaasiin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*For full ten days he sought, but without result, for he did not find him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Shkwaach midaasogon aano-nandone’iged (for a full ten days he searched in vain), mii sa gaawiin omikawaasiin (and so he did not find him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) Ā‘pidcisa cigwa kawanāndạm.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji sa zhigwa gawanaandam (gwanaandam).**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Completely now was he starving.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji sa zhigwa gawanaandam (now he was utterly starving).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Ningutingigu nīsātciwạn icimādcāt ‘iᵉi’ᵘ sībi, ningutingsa papimācạgāmät, owâbạmān ugīskimạnisīn ạgōsinit i∙i∙mā nibī‘kāng imā inābinit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo niisaajiwan izhi-maajaad i’iw ziibi, ningoding sa  babimaazhagaamed owaabamaan ogiishkimanisiin agoozinid i’imaa  nibiikaang imaa inaabinid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once while down the course of the river he was going, once while he was walking along the shore, he saw a kingfisher perched aloft (and) looking down into the water.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo niisaajiwan izhi-maajaad i’iw ziibi (now once, going along down the course of the river), ningoding sa babimaazhagaamed (once while walking along the shore) owaabamaan ogiishkimanisiin agoozinid (he saw a kingfisher perched aloft) i’imaa nibiikaang imaa inaabinid (looking down there into the water).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) Wägunänīwinän ugāgīmā∙ā∙n; päcu’ odōdisān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wegoneniiwinen ogaagiimaa’aan;(xii) besho odoodisaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*What should he do but slip stealthily up to it; nigh up to it he came.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wegoneniiwinen ogaagiimaa’aan (what should he do but sneak up on it); besho odoodisaan (close up to it he came).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) Ānawi∙i∙cinawatināt, pạngī∙i∙gu upicigupinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aanawii-izhi-nawadinaad, bangii igo obishkobinaan.(xiii)**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In an attempt to grab it he just missed catching it.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aanawii-izhi-nawadinaad (when he tried to grab it), bangii igo obishkobinaan (he just barely missed catching it).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) Mīdạc ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kā∙i∙‘pināgubạnän, mī∙i∙ᵘ kīnīskawäyāntipät aᵉa’ᵘ ugīskimạnisī.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash i’iw gaa-ipinaagobanen, mii iw gii-niishkaweyaandibed* a’aw ogiishkimansii.**</span>
