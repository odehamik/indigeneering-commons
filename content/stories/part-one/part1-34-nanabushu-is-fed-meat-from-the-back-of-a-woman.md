34\. NÄNABUSHU IS FED MEAT FROM THE BACK OF A WOMAN.

Nanabosho is fed Meat from the Back of a Woman

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by probably Midāsugaⁿj (Michelson’s own hedge, carried) — Series 3 (Nos. 33–38), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ānīc, a∙ī∙ntāwạg imā ạnicinābäg, Nänabucu unīdcānisaᵉ nīciwaᵉ wīwạn kayä.</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish, a’yiindaawag imaa anishinaabeg (now, some people were living there), Nenabosho oniijaanisa niizhiwag (Nenabosho’s two children) wiiwan gaye (and his wife).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish, mii sa zhigwa ji-biboonini (well, it was now getting to be deep winter), mii sa gaawiin wiikaa naa obiidoosiin gegoo (and never once did he bring anything home).</span>
