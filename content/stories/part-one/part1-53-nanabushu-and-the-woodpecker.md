53\. NÄNABUSHU AND THE WOODPECKER.

Nänabushu and the Woodpecker (3)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penessi — Series 7 (Nos. 44–56), Fort William (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Kī∙ạ∙nimādcādạc Nänabujū.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-ani-maajaa dash Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So on his way went Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-ani-maajaa dash Nenaboozhoo (so on his way went Nänabushu).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mīnawā ogī∙ạ∙ni∙o∙disān mämäᵉn tāᵃnit wīmbina‘kadōnk.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miinawaa ogii-ani-odisaan memen daanid wiimbinakadoong.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Next he went to visit the red-head at where he was in the hole of a tree.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miinawaa ogii-ani-odisaan (and next he went off to visit him) memen daanid (the red-head, where he was) wiimbinakadoong (in the hollow of a tree).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Mīdạc kī‘pindigāt ändānit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-piindiged endaanid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so he went into where the (red-head) lived.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-piindiged (and so he went inside) endaanid (where he lived).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Mämäᵉdec kī∙a∙‘kwāndawä īmāⁿ pā‘täcīngwā‘kung.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Meme dash gii-akwaandawe imaa baate-zhingwaakong.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, the red-head climbed up a dead pine-tree.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Meme dash gii-akwaandawe (now the red-head climbed up) imaa baate-zhingwaakong (there on a dead pine-tree).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Ogạnawâbạmān ändōdạminit īni’ᵘ mämäᵉn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Oganawaabamaan endoodaminid iniw memen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He watched what the red-head was doing.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Oganawaabamaan (he watched him) endoodaminid iniw memen (what that red-head was doing).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Ạnigagwäti‘kwä∙u∙ imāⁿ mi‘tigung; ā‘pidcidạc spîmīng kitci∙ä∙nigu‘k mạdwä’kwä∙u∙t mī∙i∙māⁿ wändcipạngicininit äsibạnạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ani-gagwedikwe’o imaa mitigong; aapiji dash ishpiming gichi-enigok madwekwe’od mii imaa wenji-pangishininid esiban.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*(The red-head) kept testing where to peck on his way up the tree; and when very high up was heard the sound of him pecking with all his might, then down from there came falling a raccoon.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ani-gagwedikwe’o (he kept testing where to peck — *stem flagged*) imaa mitigong (there on the tree); aapiji dash ishpiming (and very high up) gichi-enigok (with all his might) madwekwe’od (the sound of him pecking was heard — *stem flagged*) mii imaa wenji-pangishininid esiban (then from there came falling a raccoon).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Mīwạnini kā‘kījiswād kī∙a∙cạmād Nänabujūn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii wa nini gaa-kiizhizwaad gii-ashamaad Nenaboozhoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*That was what he cooked when he fed Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii wa nini gaa-kiizhizwaad (that was the very thing he cooked) gii-ashamaad Nenaboozhoon (when he fed Nänabushu).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Äckwāwīsinit Nänabujū, “Kayä nīn nīngạtacạmāⁿ mämä.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eshkwaa-wiisinid Nenaboozhoo, “Gaye niin ningad-ashamaa meme.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When Nänabushu was done eating, “I too will feed the red-head.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eshkwaa-wiisinid Nenaboozhoo (when Nänabushu was done eating), “Gaye niin ningad-ashamaa meme.” (“I too will feed the red-head.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Nīⁿjidạc u‘kạnạn ogī∙u∙dā‘pinānạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Niizh dash okanan ogii-odaapinaanan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, two bones he took.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Niizh dash okanan (now, two bones) ogii-odaapinaanan (he took up).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Mēdạc kā∙i∙jikāciga∙ạ∙ng nänīdawaya∙ī∙, mīdạc kīa‘tōd udänigumāng.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gaa-izhi-zhiga’ang eyiidawaya’ii, mii dash gii-atood odenigomaang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so when he had sharpened them at both ends, he accordingly placed them in his nostrils.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gaa-izhi-zhiga’ang eyiidawaya’ii (and so when he had sharpened them at both ends), mii dash gii-atood (then he placed them) odenigomaang (in his nostrils).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Mēdạc a‘kwāndawät imāⁿ mi‘tigung käya wīn kagwäti‘kwäᵘ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash akwaandawed imaa mitigong gaye wiin gagwedikwe.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when he climbed up the tree, he also tested where to peck.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash akwaandawed imaa mitigong (and so when he climbed up the tree) gaye wiin gagwedikwe (he too tested where to peck — *stem flagged*).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Ā‘pidcidạc icpimīng äyāt, mī∙i’∙ᵘ ki‘tci∙ä∙nigu‘k ajimạdwä‘kwä∙u∙t.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji dash ishpiming e-yaad, mii iw gichi-enigok ezhi-madwekwe’od.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when very high up he was, he then heard pecking with all his might.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji dash ishpiming e-yaad (and when he was very high up), mii iw gichi-enigok (then with all his might) ezhi-madwekwe’od (he was heard pecking — *stem flagged*).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Mīidạc äjinisitizut.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ezhi-nisidizod.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon, he killed himself.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ezhi-nisidizod (and thereupon he killed himself).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Kāwīn ogīnisāsīn äsibạnạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin ogii-nisaasiin esibanan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He did not kill a raccoon.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin ogii-nisaasiin esibanan (he did not kill a raccoon).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Mämändạc ogīpimādci∙i∙gōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Memen dash ogii-pimaaji’igoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And by the red-head was he brought back to life.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Memen dash ogii-pimaaji’igoon (and by the red-head he was brought back to life).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Mī∙i’∙ᵘ kī∙ạ∙nimādcad.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii iw gii-ani-maajad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so on his way he went.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii iw gii-ani-maajad (and so on his way he went).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) Mīsạgunā ä‘kōzịt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa go naa ekoozid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And that is as far as (the story) goes.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa go naa ekoozid (and that is as far as it goes).</span>

(1) For other versions see Nos. 35 (p. 305) and 42 (p. 357).
