6\. NÄNABUSHU AND THE DANCING BULLRUSHES.¹

Nänabushu and the Dancing Bullrushes (1)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Wâsagunäckạnk — Series 1 (Nos. 1–18), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ninguting pạpîmusät, “Ānīn?” kī∙i∙nändạm.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding babimosed, “Aaniin?” Gii-inendam.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*While he was once walking along, “What (is it)?” he thought.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding babimosed (once while he was walking along), “Aaniin?” gii-inendam (“What (is it)?” he thought).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) “Kunigä?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gonige?”**</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gonige?” (“Wonder (what it can be)?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) O∙ō∙widạc kī∙i∙nändạm: “Kunigä kāwīn ningakạckitōsīn.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**O’ow idash gii-inendam: “Gonige gaawiin ninga-gashkitoosiin!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, this he thought: “I wonder if I am unable to do it!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">O’ow idash gii-inendam (and this he thought): “Gonige gaawiin ninga-gashkitoosiin!” (“I wonder if I am unable to do it!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Ningutingigu papîmusät, ugīwâbạmā ‘iᵉi’ᵘ ininiwaᵉ kiˢtci∙o∙sāmīninit wīnīmi∙i∙tiwaᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo babimosed, ogii-waabamaa i’iw ininiwa’ Gisji-oSaamiininit wii-niimi’idwa’**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And as he was once walking along, he saw some men gathered in a throng to dance together.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo babimosed (and once as he was walking along), ogii-waabamaa i’iw ininiwa’ (he saw those men) Gisji-oSaamiininit (gathered in a throng) wii-niimi’idwa’ (who were going to dance together).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) O∙ō∙widạc ogī∙i∙gōn īni’ᵘ päcig: “Ānīn kayä kīnina, Nänabucu, kīgạnīm?” ugīgōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**O’ow idash ogii-igoon iniw bezhig: “Aaniin gaye giin ina, Nenaboozhoo, Giga-niim?” ogii-goon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And this he was asked by one of them: “You too, Nänabushu, are you going to dance?” he was asked.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">O’ow idash ogii-igoon iniw bezhig (and this he was asked by one of them): “Aaniin gaye giin ina, Nenaboozhoo (“What about you too, Nanabushu), giga-niim?” (are you going to dance?”) ogii-goon (he was asked).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Äyeᵉ, nisīmsā nīnganīm kayänīn,” ugī∙i∙nān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aye´, nishiime sa ninga-niim gaye niin,” ogii-inaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Yes, my little brother, I too am going to dance,” he said to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aye´, nishiime sa ninga-niim gaye niin (“Yes, my little brother, I too am going to dance),” ogii-inaan (he said to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Ka‘kina pingwācāgitiwaᵉ, mīdạc ka‘kina äci∙u∙nit i∙‘i∙ᵘ pạpikwāngäna pạta‘kibinwä∙u∙nit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gakina bingwaashaagidiwa’, mii dash gakina ezhi’onid i’iw babikwaangenaa batakibine’onid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*They were all naked; and the only dress they all had was a feather, with all but the top stripped from the shaft, standing perched upon their heads.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gakina bingwaashaagidiwa’ (they were all naked), mii dash gakina ezhi’onid i’iw (and the only dress they all wore was) babikwaangenaa batakibine’onid (a feather stripped to the top, standing perched upon their heads).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) “Kayä kīnina kīwīnīm?” kī∙i∙nā ‘aᵉa’ᵘ Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gaye giin ina giwii-niim?”  Gii-inaa a´aw Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“And do you also wish to dance?” they said to Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaye giin ina giwii-niim?” (“And do you also wish to dance?”) Gii-inaa a´aw Nenaboozhoo (Nanabushu was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) “Mīnagu iᵘ ītug cigwa wīwītō‘kāsoyạn?</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii na go iw iidog zhigwa wii-wiidookaazoyan?**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Without doubt it must be your wish to participate?*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii na go iw iidog zhigwa wii-wiidookaazoyan? (“Without doubt it must be your wish now to take part?</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Kinōndạmina mini‘k ‘iᵉi’wisa mini‘k wanīmi∙i∙tink?</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**(10) Gi-noondam ina minik i’iw isa minik waa-niimi’iding?**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Have you heard how many days they will be dancing?*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(10) Gi-noondam ina minik i’iw isa minik waa-niimi’iding? (Have you heard how many days they will be dancing?)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Nicwāsugin wīnīmi∙i∙tim.”</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Eight days will they be dancing.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(11) Nishwaasogan wii-niimi’idim.” (Eight days they will be dancing.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Mīdạc kayä wīn kā∙i∙ci∙u∙t, kīpạta‘kibinwä∙u∙t.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**(12) Mii dash gaye wiin gaa-izhi’od, gii-batakibinwe’od.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he himself dressed in the same costume, he had a feather standing upon his head.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(12) Mii dash gaye wiin gaa-izhi’od (thereupon he himself dressed the same way), gii-batakibinwe’od (he had a feather standing upon his head).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Mīdạc a‘pī mādci‘tānit, nāwayaī kā∙i∙jōcigābawit kayäwīn kīnīmit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**(13) Mii dash apii maajitaanid, naawaya’ii gaa-izhooshigaabawid gaye qiin gii-niimid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, when they began, in their midst was where he stood and also danced.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(13) Mii dash apii maajitaanid (and now, when they began), naawaya’ii gaa-izhooshigaabawid (in their midst was where he stood) gaye qiin gii-niimid (and he too danced).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Mīdạc a‘pī kīmādci‘tānit, ānīc mīsa gayä wīn i∙i∙māⁿ kā∙i∙ndacīwäcimut; babänạginạngä ina‘kạmigisiwaᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**(14) Mii dash apii gii-maajitaanid, aaniish mii sa gaye wiin i’imaa gaa-indashiiweshimot; baabnaginange inakamigiziwa’.**</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(14) Mii dash apii gii-maajitaanid (now, when they began), aaniish mii sa gaye wiin i’imaa gaa-indashiiweshimot (thereupon then and there did he dance); baabnaginange inakamigiziwa’ (a rousing time did they have).</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Of a truth, a great dance they had together there.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(15) Geget sa gichi-niimi’idiwag i’imaa (of a truth, a great dance they had together there).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Mīdạc i∙i∙māⁿ ändạciwäcimut, nīyōgun kānīmit.</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And now, there where he was dancing, four days did he dance.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(16) Mii dash i’imaa endazhiweshimod (and now, there where he was dancing), niiyogon gaa-niimid (four days he danced).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(17) Aaniish i’imaa wiijiiwaad (so there he accompanied them) i’iw isa gaa-niiyogonagadanig (for a space of four days).</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And that was when they said to him: “We beg of you, display your zeal,” they said to Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(18) Mii dash apii gii-inind (and that was when he was told): “Ambe sa noo ayaagwaamizin (“Come now, display your zeal),” gii-inaa aw Nenaboozhoo (Nanabushu was told).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(19) “Gego noonde-aanishiidangen.” (“Don’t let up before the affair is over.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) Mīdạc a‘pī cigwā kī∙u∙ji∙u∙nit; kā∙i∙jimîskwā‘kwiyuwänit, ānīc mīgu gayä wīn kā∙i∙ji∙u∙t ‘aᵉa’ᵘ Nänabucu.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**(20) Mii dash apii zhigwa gii-ozhi’onid; gaa-izhi-miskwaakwiyowenid, aaniish mii go gaye wiin gaa-izhi’od a’aw Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, that was when they began to paint themselves; after they were painted red, then the same to himself did Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(20) Mii dash apii zhigwa gii-ozhi’onid (now, that was when they began to dress/paint themselves); gaa-izhi-miskwaakwiyowenid (after they had painted themselves red), aaniish mii go gaye wiin gaa-izhi’od a’aw Nenaboozhoo (then the same did Nanabushu to himself).</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He kept right on dancing there at the place, for not even yet was he weary.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(21) Gii-naaniimid mii imaa (he kept right on dancing there at the place), aanish gaawiin aanawi mashi ayekozisii (for not even yet was he weary).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Mīdạc kägä‘t änigu‘k nīminit.</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so truly with much fervor he danced.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">(23) Abaapish gaa ningodwaasogonagadinig (by the time the sixth day was up), mii dash apii e-ni-waabaninig (and when the morning light was coming on) zhigwa gikendang ayekozid (he began to realize that he was growing tired).</span>
