51\. NÄNABUSHU AND THE MOOSE-HEAD.

Nanabushu and the Moose-Head

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penessi — Series 7 (Nos. 44–56), Fort William (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Mīdạc kī∙ạ∙nimādcād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-ani-maajaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he started on his way.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gii-ani-maajaad (he set out on his way).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mīdạc kīnạgickawād ininiwạn, ā‘pidci uniciciwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-nagishkawaad ininiwan, aapiji onizhishiwan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then he met with a man, very handsome was he.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and then) gii-nagishkawaad ininiwan (he met a man), aapiji onizhishiwan (very handsome was he).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) ‘Ā, mīgwanaᵉ uctigwāning!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aa, miigwana’ oshtigwaaning!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Ah, the feathers upon his head!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aa (ah), miigwana’ (the feathers) oshtigwaaning (upon his head)!</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Nänabuju ogạnōnān: “‘Ā, nīdci, ānīndi äjāyạn?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenaboozhoo oganoonaan: “Aa, niijii, aaniindi ezhaayan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nanabushu spoke to him, saying: “Well, my friend, whither are you bound?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenaboozhoo oganoonaan (Nanabushu spoke to him): “Aa (well), niijii (my friend), aaniindi ezhaayan (where are you going)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Ā, ạnicāsạgo nimbạbāmādis; kīnîdạc, ānindi äjāyạn?”¹</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aa, anishaa sa go nimbabaamaadis; giin idash, aaniindi ezhaayan?” (1)**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Oh, I am just simply travelling about; and you, where are you going?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aa (oh), anishaa sa go (just for nothing) nimbabaamaadis (I am travelling about); giin idash (and you), aaniindi ezhaayan (where are you going)?” (1)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Kā, anicāgunā gayä nīn nimbạbāmādis.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gaa, anishaa go naa gaye niin nimbabaamaadis.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Oh, I too am simply wandering aimlessly about.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaa (oh), anishaa go naa (just simply) gaye niin (I too) nimbabaamaadis (am wandering about).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Mi‘tigwābīn oda‘kunān ‘aᵉa’ᵘ inini, Nänabujūdạc ogạnōnān: “Tā, nīdci käᵉgätsa’ unicici kimi‘tigwāb.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mitigwaabiin odakonaan a’aw inini, Nenaboozhoo dash oganoonaan: “Daa, niijii geget sa onizhishi gi-mitigwaab.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*A bow the man held in his hand, and Nanabushu addressed him, saying: “Why, my friend, truly handsome is your bow.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mitigwaabiin odakonaan (a bow he was holding) a’aw inini (that man), Nenaboozhoo dash oganoonaan (and Nanabushu spoke to him): “Daa (why), niijii (my friend), geget sa onizhishi (truly handsome it is) gi-mitigwaab (your bow).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Skumā bīc, nīngạgạgwätāgîbinā.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Shkomaa biish, ninga-gagwedaagibiinaa.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Just you hand it over to me, I want to see how it pulls.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Shkomaa biish (just hand it over), ninga-gagwedaagibiinaa (I will test its pull).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) “Ā, kāwīn! Kāwīka awiya nindawi∙ā∙sī.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aa, gaawiin! Gaa wiikaa awiya nindawi’aasii.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Ah, no! never do I turn it over to any one.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aa (ah), gaawiin (no)! Gaa wiikaa (never) awiya nindawi’aasii (do I hand it to anyone).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) “Ā, mānō, nīdci, kạnạgägō ạdcinā!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aa, maanoo, niijii, gaa na gegoo ajina!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Oh, please, my friend, just only for a moment!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aa (oh), maanoo (please), niijii (my friend), gaa na gegoo ajina (just for a little while)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Kînwäⁿj ugītạcimān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ginwenzh ogii-dashimaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*A long while, he coaxed him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ginwenzh (a long while) ogii-dashimaan (he coaxed him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Käga‘pī ugīmīnigōn īni’ᵘ mi‘tigwābīn, Nänabujūdạc ugạgwädāgibinān mi‘tigwābīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii ogii-miinigoon iniw mitigwaabiin, Nenaboozhoo dash ogagwedaagibiinaan mitigwaabiin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*At last he was given the bow, and Nanabushu tested the spring of the bow.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii (at last) ogii-miinigoon iniw mitigwaabiin (he was given the bow), Nenaboozhoo dash ogagwedaagibiinaan mitigwaabiin (and Nanabushu tested the pull of the bow).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) “Tāᵃ, käᵉgätsa’ minwāgizi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Taa geget sa minwaagizi.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Why, to be sure, it pulls finely.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Taa (why), geget sa minwaagizi (truly it pulls well).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Skumā wīn ī∙i∙wä kidasawān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Shkomaa wiin i’iwe gidasawaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Just (hand me) over that arrow of yours.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Shkomaa wiin (just) i’iwe gidasawaan (that arrow of yours).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Kînwäⁿj ānawi ogīsāgi‘tāgōn, käga‘pī ogīmīnigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ginwenzh aanawi ogii-saagitaagoon, gegapii ogii-miinigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*With all his pleading, yet a long while was it withheld from him, but finally it was given to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ginwenzh aanawi (a long while, even so) ogii-saagitaagoon (it was withheld from him), gegapii ogii-miinigoon (at last it was given to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Mī∙i∙dạc kā∙i∙jinābisitōd ī∙i∙māⁿ ạtcābīnk; mī∙i∙dạc kạgwätāgibinät Nänabucū mi‘tigwâbin, mī∙i’∙ᵘ kā∙i∙jipîmwād īni’ᵘ ininiwạn kānạgickawā‘pạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii idash gaa-izhi-naabisidood i’imaa ajaabiing; mii idash gagwedaagibiinaad Nenaboozhoo mitigwaabiin, mii iw gaa-izhi-bimwaad iniw iniwan gaa-nagishkawaapan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he fitted it in place on the bowstring ; and when Nanabushu pulled upon the bow, he thereupon shot the man whom he had met.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii idash gaa-izhi-naabisidood (and so he fitted it in place) i’imaa ajaabiing (there on the bowstring); mii idash gagwedaagibiinaad Nenaboozhoo mitigwaabiin (and as Nanabushu drew the bow), mii iw gaa-izhi-bimwaad (then he shot him) iniw iniwan gaa-nagishkawaapan (that man whom he had met).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) Mōⁿzunk kī∙i∙jināguziwạn a‘pī gānisāt; ā‘pidci wīninōwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Moozong gii-izhinaagoziwan apii gaa-nisaad; aapiji wiininoowan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Like a moose he looked, after (Nanabushu) had slain him ; he was ever so fat.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Moozong gii-izhinaagoziwan (like a moose he looked) apii gaa-nisaad (when he had killed him); aapiji wiininoowan (very fat was he).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) Kistcimînwändạm Nänabujū kistciwīsinit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gichi-minwendam Nenaboozhoo gichi-wiisinid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Highly pleased was Nanabushu to have a great heap of food.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gichi-minwendam Nenaboozhoo (greatly pleased was Nanabushu) gichi-wiisinid (to eat a great deal).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Mī∙i∙dạc kā∙i∙jipigickijwād īni’ᵘ mōⁿzōn, ā‘pidcidạc wänicicink wīyās ugīkijizān wāmīdcit; pimidä gayä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii idash gaa-izhi-pi-gishkizhwaad iniw moozoon, aapiji dash wenizhishing wiiyaas ogii-kiizhizaan waa-miijid; bimide gaye.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly he cut the moose up into pieces, and very nice was the meat he cooked to eat ; and the grease too (was savory).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii idash gaa-izhi-pi-gishkizhwaad iniw moozoon (and so he cut up the moose), aapiji dash wenizhishing wiiyaas (and very fine meat) ogii-kiizhizaan waa-miijid (he cooked, what he would eat); bimide gaye (and the grease too).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) Kā‘kījidänik, mīdạc kī∙ạ∙gwā∙i∙zä‘kwät.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-giizhidenig, mii dash gii-agwaa’izekwed.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When it was done, he accordingly took it out of the kettle.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-giizhidenig (when it was cooked), mii dash gii-agwaa’izekwed (then he took it out of the kettle).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Cī’gwa wīmādạndcigät, kizībā‘kwạt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa wii-maadanjiged, giziibaakwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Just as he was on the point of eating, there was a creaking noise.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa wii-maadanjiged (just as he was about to begin eating), giziibaakwad (there was a creaking sound).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) “Tciēⁿ, tciēⁿ,” inwänik.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ji’iin, ji’iin,” inwenig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Tci?n, tci?n!” was the way it sounded.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ji’iin, ji’iin,” (a vocable, carried as sounded) inwenig (so it sounded).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Kāwin omino‘tạnzīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin ominotanziin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He did not like the sound.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin ominotanziin (he did not like the sound of it).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) “Ictä, pîzān tāga!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ishte, bizaan daga!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Now, do you keep silent !”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ishte (now then), bizaan daga (do be still)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Äckamigu kijīwä ī∙i’∙ᵘ kizībā‘kwạt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eshkam igo gizhiiwe i’iw giziibaakwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Still louder grew the noise of the creaking.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eshkam igo gizhiiwe (louder and louder it sounded) i’iw giziibaakwad (that creaking).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) Nänabujudạc kīpạsigwī, ogīmạnijān pạngī ojōbīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenaboozhoodazh gii-pasigwii, ogii-manizhaan bangii ozhoobiin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nanabushu rose to his feet, sliced off a little bit of (fatty) tenderloin.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenaboozhoodazh gii-pasigwii (and Nanabushu rose to his feet), ogii-manizhaan bangii ozhoobiin (he sliced off a little of the tenderloin).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Mīdạc ädạnk ī∙i’∙ᵘ kizībā‘kwạt: “Käᵉgätsa’ kidōmbīgis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash edang i’iw giziibaakwad: “Geget sa gidoombiigis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then he said to the creaking noise : “Really, too much of a noise are you making.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash edang i’iw giziibaakwad (and so he said to the creaking): “Geget sa gidoombiigis (truly you are making too much noise).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) Kīwạnickwäm wīwīsiniyān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-wanishkwem wii-wiisiniyaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*You are annoying me when I want to eat.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-wanishkwem (you are disturbing me) wii-wiisiniyaan (when I want to eat).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) Owä gayä gīn mīdcin.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Owe gaye giin miijin.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*This too do you eat.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Owe gaye giin (this too, you) miijin (eat).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Mīdạc Nänabuju a‘tōd ī∙i’∙ᵘ pimidä kizībā‘kwạt ogīta‘kwạmigun.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash Nenaboozhoo atood i’iw bimide giziibaakwad ogii-dakwamigon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when Nanabushu placed the fat in where the creaking noise was made, he was caught fast.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash Nenaboozhoo atood i’iw bimide (and when Nanabushu put the fat) giziibaakwad ogii-dakwamigon (into the creaking place, it bit and held him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Mī∙i∙māⁿ kī∙ạ∙gōdcink kistciginwäⁿc, pīnic ạnōdc awäsīyạg — ma∙ī∙ngạnạg; kwingwa∙ā∙gäg, udcīgạg, wâgucạg — gītạgwicinōg kī∙ạ∙mwāwād mōzōn; kāgāgiwạg gayä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii imaa gii-agoojing gichi-ginwenzh, biinish anooj awesiiyag – ma’iinganag; gwiingwa’aageg, ojiigag, waagoshag – gii-dagwishinoog gii-amwaawaad moozoon; gaagaagiwag gaye.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly there he hung for a great while, until all sorts of game-folk – wolves, martens, fishers, foxes – arrived to eat the moose; ravens too (arrived).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii imaa gii-agoojing gichi-ginwenzh (and so there he hung a very long while), biinish anooj awesiiyag (until all sorts of animals) – ma’iinganag (wolves); gwiingwa’aageg (wolverines/martens), ojiigag (fishers), waagoshag (foxes) – gii-dagwishinoog gii-amwaawaad moozoon (arrived and ate the moose); gaagaagiwag gaye (ravens too).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Pānimā ka‘kina kā‘kidạmunt ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mōⁿs mī∙i’∙ᵘ pîdcīnạg, kīpạgidạmigut Nänabujū ī∙i’∙ᵘ kizībā‘kwạt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Baanimaa gakina gaa-gidamond a’aw mooz mii iw bijiinag, gii-pagidamigod Nenaboozhoo i’iw giziibaakwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Not till the whole of the moose was eaten up, was Nanabushu freed from the grip of the creaking place.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Baanimaa gakina gaa-gidamond a’aw mooz (not until the whole moose was eaten up) mii iw bijiinag (only then), gii-pagidamigod Nenaboozhoo i’iw giziibaakwad (was Nanabushu released by the creaking place).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) A‘pidci wīwisini kī∙i∙jā iimāⁿ abini‘pạn īni’ᵘ mōzōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji wii-wiisini gii-izhaa i’imaa abinipan iniw moozoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Very eager was he to eat, and he went over to where the moose had been.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji wii-wiisini (very eager to eat was he) gii-izhaa i’imaa abinipan iniw moozoon (he went there where the moose had been).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Mīyä‘ta u‘kạnan ä‘tänig, ustigwānigägạn kayä a‘täni.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii eta okanan etenig, oshtigwaanigegan gaye ateni.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Only its bones were left, its skull was there too.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii eta okanan etenig (only its bones were there), oshtigwaanigegan gaye ateni (and its skull lay there too).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) Mēdạc äjiwâbạmād wawâbigạnōdcīyaᵉ pīndigänit imāⁿ mons ustigwānig.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ezhi-waabamaad wawaabiganoojiiya’ biindigenid imaan mooz oshtigwaaning.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he saw some mice (2) go into the head of the moose.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ezhi-waabamaad wawaabiganoojiiya’ (then he saw mice) biindigenid imaan mooz oshtigwaaning (going into there, the moose’s head).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) Owâbandạn pạngī a‘tänig winindîp.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Owaabandaan bangii atenig wiinindib.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He saw that a little bit of the brains was left.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Owaabandaan bangii atenig (he saw that a little was left) wiinindib (of the brains).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) “A‘pägic mīdciyān!” inändạm mīdạc äjikạnōnāt wawâbigạnōdciyaᵉ: “Kitāgạckitō nāwāna gayä nīn ī∙i’∙ᵘ tcī∙i∙niginiyān äyāniginiyäg?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Apegish miijiyaan!” inendam mii dash ezhi-ganoonaad waawaabiganoojiiya’: “Gidaa-gashkitoonaawaa na gaye niin i’iw ji-iniginiyaan eyaniginiyeg?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Would that I might eat it !” he thought. Thereupon he spoke to the mice, saying : “ Could you bring it about so that I might be of the same size as you ?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Apegish miijiyaan (would that I might eat it)!” inendam (he thought) mii dash ezhi-ganoonaad waawaabiganoojiiya’ (and so he spoke to the mice): “Gidaa-gashkitoonaawaa na (could you bring it about) gaye niin i’iw ji-iniginiyaan (that I too might be the size) eyaniginiyeg (that you are)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) “Ā, kāwīn!” udigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aa, gaawiin!” odigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Ah, no!” he was told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aa (ah), gaawiin (no)!” odigoon (he was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) “Uzām kīmindit,” udigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ozaam gi-mindid,” odigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Of too large a size are you,” he was told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ozaam gi-mindid (you are too big),” odigoon (he was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) “Ā, mānū kayänīn niwīpīndigä imāⁿ mōⁿs ustigwāning!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aa, maanoo gaye niin niwii-piindige imaa mooz oshtigwaaning!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Ah, please let me go into the head of the moose too!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aa (ah), maanoo (please) gaye niin niwii-piindige (I too want to go in) imaa mooz oshtigwaaning (there into the moose’s head)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) “Awāwisa’,” udigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ahaaw isa,” odigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“All right, then,” he was told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ahaaw isa (all right then),” odigoon (he was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(42) “Omāⁿ yä‘ta kistigwāning kīgadagāⁿcī’ⁿyᵉ.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Omaa eta gi-shtigwaaning giga-dagaashiiniy”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Only here at your head will you be made small.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Omaa eta gi-shtigwaaning (only here at your head) giga-dagaashiiniy (you will be made small).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(43) Mīdec wāwābiganōdcīyink gī∙i∙ni‘kuguictigwānät.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash waawaabiganoojiiying gii-inikogo ishtigwaaned.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon like unto the head of a mouse was the size of his head.*</span>
