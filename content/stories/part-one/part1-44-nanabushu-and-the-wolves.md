44\. NÄNABUSHU AND THE WOLVES.³

Nänabushu and the Wolves (3)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penessi — Series 7 (Nos. 44–56), Fort William (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Nīnguting a∙i∙ntāawạg wīgiwāming Nänabōju osạni, Kwäsind, wīwạn gayä nīⁿj uskinawäg gayä, Nänabōju mīnawādac Pāninī.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding ayindaawag wiigiwaaming Nenaboozhoo oosan, Gwesind, wiiwan gaye niizh oskinaweg gaye, Nenaboozhoo miinawaa dash Baaninii.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once on a time in a wigwam dwelt Nänabushu’s father Kwäsind, with his wife and two youths, Nänabushu and also Panini.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding ayindaawag (once on a time they were dwelling) wiigiwaaming (in a wigwam) Nenaboozhoo oosan, Gwesind (Nenaboozhoo’s father, Gwesind/Kwesind), wiiwan gaye (and his wife) niizh oskinaweg gaye (and two young men too), Nenaboozhoo miinawaa dash Baaninii (Nenaboozhoo and Baaninii/Panini).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mōⁿjạg nạndawändcigäwạg, awäsīyan nisāwāt; mi‘tigwābīn odābạtci∙ā∙wān pimwāwāt awäsīan — pinäwạn, cīcibạn, nī´kạn, ạnōtcigägo ōni‘tōnāwa.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Moozhag nandawenjigewag, awesiiyan nisaawaad; mitigwaabiin odaabaji’aawaan bimwaawaad awesiian – binewan, zhiishiiban, nikan, anooji gego onitoonaawaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Often they went hunting, (and) game they killed; bow and arrows they used when they shot at the game-kind, - at ruffed grouse, ducks, geese, - and various kinds of them they killed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Moozhag nandawenjigewag (often they hunted), awesiiyan nisaawaad (killing game animals); mitigwaabiin odaabaji’aawaan (they used bow and arrow) bimwaawaad awesiian (when they shot at the game-kind) – binewan, zhiishiiban, nikan (ruffed grouse, ducks, geese), anooji gego onitoonaawaa (all sorts of things they killed).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Kinwäⁿj ki‘tāwạg imāⁿ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ginwenzh gii-daawag imaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*A long while they tarried there.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ginwenzh gii-daawag imaa (a long while they dwelt there).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Käga‘pī Nänạbojō odinān ōsạn: “Nōse, ningamādcā.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii Nenaboozhoo odinaan oosan: “Noose, ninga-maajaa.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*At last Nänabushu said to his father: “O father! I am going away.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii Nenaboozhoo odinaan oosan (at last Nenaboozhoo said to his father): “Noose, ninga-maajaa.” (“my father, I am going away.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Āndi wā∙i∙jāyạn?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aandi waa-izhaayan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Where do you expect to go?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aandi waa-izhaayan?” (“where do you intend to go?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Nīwībạbāmādis.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Niwii-babaamaadis.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“I want to go upon a journey.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Niwii-babaamaadis.” (“I want to travel about.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) “Pōcgä gīn mādcān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Booshke giin maajaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“If it be your pleasure, then go on.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Booshke giin maajaan.” (“as you please, then go on.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Nänabōju kā∙i∙jimādcāt; miziwe gī∙i∙cā, omi‘tigwābīn udayāwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenaboozhoo gaa-izhi-maajaad; miziwe gii-izhaa, omitigwaabiin odayaawaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nänabushu then started away; everywhere he went, his bow and arrows he took along.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenaboozhoo gaa-izhi-maajaad (then Nenaboozhoo started off); miziwe gii-izhaa (everywhere he went), omitigwaabiin odayaawaan (he took his bow and arrows along).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Onōdcigägō wạni‘tōn mādcît.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Onooji gegoo [wa] nitoon maajid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Of the various kinds (of game) he killed he ate.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Onooji gegoo [wa] nitoon maajid (the various things he killed he ate as he went) [*editorial [wa] preserved as printed*].</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Ningodingidac wīgiwām ōwāmbạndān, mēdạc udōdā‘pinān obigwa‘k umạdwä∙ā∙n ickwāndäm.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash wiigiwaam owaabandaan, mii dash odoo-daapinaan obikwak omadwe’aan ishkwaandem.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So once on a time a wigwam he saw, whereupon he took his arrow (and) with it was heard knocking on the door.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash wiigiwaam owaabandaan (and once he saw a wigwam), mii dash odoo-daapinaan obikwak (so he took up his arrow) omadwe’aan ishkwaandem (and with it knocked on the door).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Mạdwä gīgito i‘kwä: “Pīndigän!” i‘kido.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Madwe-giigido ikwe: “Biindigen!” ikido.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The voice of a woman called: “Come in!” she said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Madwe-giigido ikwe (a woman’s voice was heard): “Biindigen!” ikido (“come in!” she said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Kā∙i∙jipīndigät Nänabōjo, owābạmān i‘kwäwạn nīⁿj kayä ạbinōtcīyaᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-izhi-piindiged Nenaboozhoo, owaabamaan ikwewan niizh gaye abinoojiiya’.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After Nänabushu went in, he saw a woman and two children.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-izhi-piindiged Nenaboozhoo (after Nenaboozhoo went in), owaabamaan ikwewan (he saw a woman) niizh gaye abinoojiiya’ (and two children too).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) I‘kwä ōgīodā‘pînān mi‘tig; i‘kwä upa‘ki‘täwān o‘kāding, mämäckut.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ikwe ogii-odaapinaan mitig; ikwe obakitewaan okaading memeshkod.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The woman picked up a stick, the woman struck them on the leg; first one, then the other.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ikwe ogii-odaapinaan mitig (the woman picked up a stick); ikwe obakitewaan okaading (the woman struck them on the leg) memeshkod (one after the other).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) “Kägu´, kägu´!” i‘kido Nänabōju.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gego, gego!” ikido Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Don’t, don’t!” said Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gego, gego!” ikido Nenaboozhoo (“don’t, don’t!” said Nenaboozhoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) “Ningạntawändcigä,” i‘kito Nänabōju.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ninga-awi-nandawenjige,” ikido Nenaboozhoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“I am going off on a hunt for game,” said Nänabushu.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ninga-awi-nandawenjige,” ikido Nenaboozhoo (“I am going off to hunt for game,” said Nenaboozhoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Mēdạc kā∙i∙jinisāt unītcānisạn bäjak, mī∙i∙dạc kā∙i∙ji∙o∙ci‘tōd ku‘kä∙a∙wạn, kīⁿgōⁿ ku‘kä∙a∙wạn, nībiwa gayä wīgup pīmạna‘kwān, migiskạnạn gayä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gaa-izhi-nisaad oniijaanisan bezhig, mii dash gaa-izhi-ozhitood goke’awan, giingoonh goke’awan, niibiwa gaye wiigob biiminakwaan, migiskanan gaye.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so after she had slain one of her children, he then fixed up some bait, some fish-bait, and also a large amount of linden-bark twine, and some hooks.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gaa-izhi-nisaad oniijaanisan bezhig (and so after she had killed one of her children), mii dash gaa-izhi-ozhitood goke’awan (he then made some bait), giingoonh goke’awan (fish-bait), niibiwa gaye wiigob biiminakwaan (and a lot of basswood-bark twine), migiskanan gaye (and some hooks).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) Midạc gī∙u∙ci‘tōd ubimiwạnān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-ozhitood obimiwanaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he made up his pack.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-ozhitood obimiwanaan (thereupon he made up his pack).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) “Ki‘tcigitcigạmīng ningabạgitābi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gichi-gichi-gamiing ninga-bagidaabii.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“In the sea I am going to lay a line of bone hooks.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gichi-gichi-gamiing ninga-bagidaabii. (“in the great sea I am going to set my line.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Nisagwa kīⁿgōⁿyag nīngabināg, tciwīsiniwād abinōntci∙ạ∙g.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nisagwaa giingoonyag ninga-biinaag, ji-wiisiniwaad abinoonjiyag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The fish I kill I will fetch home, that the children may eat.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nisagwaa giingoonyag ninga-biinaag (the fish I kill I will bring home), ji-wiisiniwaad abinoonjiyag (that the children may eat).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) Nānagōnạga‘k ningatạgwicin.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Naanagonagak ninga-dagwishin.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In five days I will return.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Naanagonagak ninga-dagwishin.” (“in five days I will return.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Mēdac kīmādcād. Nītạmidac täpi‘kạdinig kīgabäci; kīniba.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-maajaad. Nitam idash dibikadinig gii-gabeshi; gii-nibaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he started away. The first time that night came on, there he stopped to camp; he slept.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-maajaad (thereupon he started off). Nitam idash dibikadinig gii-gabeshi (and the first time night came on he made camp); gii-nibaa (he slept).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Wayābạnīnigidạc kī∙a∙nimādcā; kwaya‘k kīwädinung ijā.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wayaabaninig idash gii-ani-maajaa; gwayak giiwedinong izhaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then in the morning he started on; straight towards the region of the north wind he went.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wayaabaninig idash gii-ani-maajaa (and in the morning he went on his way); gwayak giiwedinong izhaa (straight toward the north he went).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Kikijäb, tcībwānāwe‘kwäg, kīmạdābi sāga∙i∙gạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gikizheb, jibwaa-naawakweg, gii-madaabii zaaga’igan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In the morning, before it was noon, he came out upon a lake.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gikizheb, jibwaa-naawakweg (in the morning, before noon), gii-madaabii zaaga’igan (he came out onto a lake).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) Ugīwābạmān niswi mā∙ī∙ngạnaᵉ pimi‘pa‘tōnit; ōgạnawābamān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-waabamaan niswi ma’iingana’ bimipatoonid; oganawaabamaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He saw three Wolves running along; he gazed at them.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-waabamaan niswi ma’iingana’ bimipatoonid (he saw three Wolves running along); oganawaabamaan (he watched them).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Ō, pīpāgi Nänabōju: “Ä, pä‘ka, nītci! Pīciyu‘k!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Oo, biibaagi Nenaboozhoo: “E, bekaa, niijii! Bii’ishiyok!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Oh, then out cried Nänabushu: “Hey, hold on, my friends! Wait for me!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Oo, biibaagi Nenaboozhoo (oh, Nenaboozhoo called out): “E, bekaa, niijii! Bii’ishiyok!” (“hey, hold on, my friends! Wait for me!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) Mā∙i∙ngạnạg kạnōnitiwạg: “Ā, mīawe Nänaboju!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ma’inganag ganoonidiwag: “Aa, mii awe Nenaboozhoo!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The Wolves spoke one to another, saying: “Why, that is Nänabushu!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ma’inganag ganoonidiwag (the Wolves spoke to one another): “Aa, mii awe Nenaboozhoo!” (“ah, that is Nenaboozhoo!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Päjik a‘kiwäⁿzi ma∙i∙ngạn, nījwi ugwisaᵉ ma∙i∙ngạnạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bezhig akiwenzii ma’iingan, niizh ogwisan ma’inganag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*One was an old Wolf, and two (other) Wolves (were) his sons.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bezhig akiwenzii ma’iingan (one was an old Wolf), niizh ogwisan ma’inganag (two of the Wolves were his sons).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) “Kägu´, kägu´ kạnōnā‘kägun. Mādcāg, mādcāg!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gego, gego ganoonaakegon. Maajaag, maajaag!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Don’t, don’t you speak to him! Keep on going, keep on going!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gego, gego ganoonaakegon. (“don’t, don’t you speak to him.) Maajaag, maajaag!” (“keep going, keep going!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) A, ki‘tcipimipa‘tō Nänaboju!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aah, gichi-pimipatoo Nenaboozhoo!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Oh, with what great speed then ran Nänabushu!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aah, gichi-pimipatoo Nenaboozhoo! (ah, with great speed ran Nenaboozhoo!)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Mīnawa bibāgi: “Pä‘kā, pä‘kā, nītci! Pī∙i∙cîn a‘kawe, kīwi‘kạnōnin!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miinawa biibaagi: “Bekaa, bekaa, niijii! Bii’ishin akawe, giwii-kanoonin!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once more he cried aloud: “Hold on, hold on, my friends! Wait for me awhile, I wish to speak with you!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miinawa biibaagi (again he called out): “Bekaa, bekaa, niijii! (“hold on, hold on, my friends!) Bii’ishin akawe, giwii-kanoonin!” (“wait for me awhile, I want to speak to you!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Käga‘pī kī‘kibitcigāpāwi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii gii-kibijigaabawi.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Finally (the old Wolf) stopped and stood.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii gii-kibijigaabawi (at last he stopped and stood still).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Ä Nänaboju gītạgwicin īmā. “Ā, bōⁿjo, bōⁿjo, nītci!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**E Nenaboozhoo gii-dagwishin imaa. Aah, boozhoo, boozhoo, niijii!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So Nänabushu arrived over there. “Well, halloo, halloo my friends!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">E Nenaboozhoo gii-dagwishin imaa (so Nenaboozhoo arrived there). Aah, boozhoo, boozhoo, niijii!” (“ah, hello, hello, my friends!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) Kayä odōjimaᵉ Nänabōju ogạgwädcimān: “Āndi äjāyäg?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaye odoozhima’ Nenaboozhoo ogagwejimaan: “Aandi e-zhaayeg?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And of his nephews Nänabushu inquired: “Where are you going?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaye odoozhima’ Nenaboozhoo ogagwejimaan (and Nenaboozhoo questioned his nephews): “Aandi e-zhaayeg?” (“where are you going?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) “O, wâsa wābạnung.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Oh, waasa waabanong.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*„Oh, far away towards the dawn.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Oh, waasa waabanong.” (“oh, far away toward the east/dawn.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) “Ānīn äjini‘kādäg?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniin ezhinikaadeg?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“What is the name of the place?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniin ezhinikaadeg?” (“what is the place called?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) “Kīci‘kidigwạnicing.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Giizhikidigwanishing.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Place of Cedar-Knots.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Giizhikidigwanishing.” (“Place-of-Cedar-Knots.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) “Wä!” Nänaboju i‘ketō, “mī∙i∙wide gayä nīn ijāyān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“We!” Nenaboozhoo ikido, “mii iwe gaye niin izhaayaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Oh!” Nänabushu said, “that is the very place where I too am going.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“We!” Nenaboozhoo ikido (“oh!” Nenaboozhoo said), “mii iwe gaye niin izhaayaan.” (“that is the very place I too am going.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Ma∙ī∙ngạn ōgagwädcimān Nänabojōn: “Wägonän kābimōndạmạn?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ma’iingan ogagwejimaan Nenaboozhoo: “Wegonen gaa-bimoondaman?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The Wolf asked Nänabushu: “What are you carrying on your back?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ma’iingan ogagwejimaan Nenaboozhoo (the Wolf questioned Nenaboozhoo): “Wegonen gaa-bimoondaman?” (“what is it you are carrying on your back?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) “Nīmbīndcigosān kābimōndamān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nim-biinjigosaan gaa-bimoondamaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My bag (with personal belongings) is what I have on my back.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nim-biinjigosaan gaa-bimoondamaan.” (“it is my bag I am carrying on my back.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) ‘Ā, Nänaboju i‘kido: “Kīgawīdcīwininim. Wägunän nändawābạndamäg? Kayä nīn mī∙i∙widi äjāyān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aa, Nenaboozhoo ikido: “Giga-wiijiiwininim. Wegonen nendawaabandameg? Gaye niin mii iwidi ezhaayaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Why, Nänabushu said: „I will go along with you. What are you looking for? I too am bound for that place.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aa, Nenaboozhoo ikido (well, Nenaboozhoo said): “Giga-wiijiiwininim. (“I will go along with you.) Wegonen nendawaabandameg? (“what are you looking for?) Gaye niin mii iwidi ezhaayaan.” (“I too am going there.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) “Nībinung kī‘ki∙o∙säwạg kidōcimạg; nībiwa awäsīyạn oginisāwān; mīdạc nībiwa wiyās pā‘täg pimitä gayä mīdạc kīạsạndcigowād; mīwänjiicayang.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Niibinong gii-kii’osewag gidoozhimag; niibiwa awesiiyan ogii-nisaawaan; mii dash niibiwa wiiyaas baateg bimide gaye mii dash gii-asanjigowaad; mii wendazhi-izhaayang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Last summer on a hunt were your nephews; much game they killed, and a good deal of dry meat and grease was what they cached; that is what we are going there for.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Niibinong gii-kii’osewag gidoozhimag (“last summer your nephews went on a hunt); niibiwa awesiiyan ogii-nisaawaan (much game they killed); mii dash niibiwa wiiyaas baateg bimide gaye (and a lot of dried meat and grease) mii dash gii-asanjigowaad (is what they cached); mii wendazhi-izhaayang. (“that is why we are going there.)</span>
