23\. NÄNABUSHU EATS THE ARTICHOKES.¹

Nenabosho eats the Artichokes (2)

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by probably Midāsugaⁿj (Michelson’s own hedge, carried) — Series 2 (Nos. 19–32), Bois Fort (per Michelson’s roster, Part I Preface p. XX; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1917).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ningutingigu papimōtät owâbạndān kägō sāga‘kinig, kumā omōna‘ā‘n; kägō äjimi‘kwāni‘kātank, kumāgu a‘kwāni.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo babimooded owaabandaan gegoo zaagakiinig, gomaa omoona’aan; gegoo ezhi-mikwaanikaadang, go maa go akwaani.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And once, when he was crawling about, he saw something that grew out of the ground, accordingly he dug it up; something he then found on digging it up, a certain length it extended.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo babimooded (and once, as he was crawling about) owaabandaan gegoo zaagakiinig (he saw something growing out of the ground), gomaa omoona’aan (so he dug it up a little); gegoo ezhi-mikwaanikaadang (something he found on digging it up), go maa go akwaani (a certain length it extended).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mīdạc ‘iᵉi’ᵘ äjikạnōtạnk: “Kāwīnina kītạmagōsīn? Kīⁿwīzumina? ānīn iᵘ äcini‘kāsuyäg?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash i’iw ezhi-ganoodang: “Gaawiin ina giitamagoosiin? Giinwiizom ina? Aaniin iw ezhinikaazoyeg?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon he then spoke to it, saying: “Are you not eaten for food? Have you a name? What are you called?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash i’iw ezhi-ganoodang (whereupon he spoke to it): “Gaawiin ina giitamagoosiin? (“are you not eaten for food? — *passive stem flagged*) Giinwiizom ina? (have you a name?) Aaniin iw ezhinikaazoyeg?” (what are you called?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) “Askibwā nintigōmin.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Askibwaa (Ashkibwaa) nindigoomin.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Artichoke we are called.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Askibwaa (Ashkibwaa) nindigoomin.” (“Artichoke we are called.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) “Ānīn äni‘kā‘käyäg kicpin nibiwa āmukōyäkun? Kägōna kitini‘kāgäm?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniin enikaakeyeg giishpin niibiwa emokooyegon? Gegoo na gidinikaagen?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“What physical effect do you produce if much of you be eaten? Do you cause some sort of bodily ill?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniin enikaakeyeg (“what effect do you produce — *stem flagged*) giishpin niibiwa emokooyegon? (if much of you be eaten?) Gegoo na gidinikaagen?” (do you cause some bodily ill?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Kāwinsạgu; ningīciwackatä∙i∙wämin, kayä dạc nimpōgisi‘kāgämin. Ānic mīsạgu iᵘ äni‘kāgäyāng.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gaawiin sa go; ningiizhiwashkade’iwemin, gaye dash nimpoogisikaagemin. Aaniish mii sa go iw enikaageyaang.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Not at all; we produce a pain in the stomach, and we cause wind. Now, that is the bodily effect we produce.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaawiin sa go (“not at all); ningiizhiwashkade’iwemin (we cause a pain in the stomach), gaye dash nimpoogisikaagemin (and we cause wind). Aaniish mii sa go iw enikaageyaang.” (now, that is the effect we produce.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Nänabucu omā äjimādci‘tāt, nībiwa omōna‘wā ‘iᵉi’wä ạskibwāᵉ; äcigisībīgināt, äjimādạntcigä, wäntcitạgu täwīsini.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenabosho omaa ezhi-maajitaad, niibiwa omoonawaa i’iwe ashkibwaa; ezhi-giziibiiginaad, ezhi-maadanjige, wenjida go de-wiisini.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nenabosho then here set to work, he gathered many artichokes; when he wiped them clean with his hands, then he began eating them, till he was quite satisfied he ate.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenabosho omaa ezhi-maajitaad (Nenabosho here set to work), niibiwa omoonawaa i’iwe ashkibwaa (he dug up many of those artichokes); ezhi-giziibiiginaad (when he washed them clean with his hands), ezhi-maadanjige (he began eating), wenjida go de-wiisini (until he ate quite to his fill).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Ā‘pidci nibiwa kā‘kitạmwāt kudcipạsigwī.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji niibiwa gaagidamwaad goji-bazigwii.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When he had eaten a bountiful supply of them, he tried to rise to his feet.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji niibiwa gaagidamwaad (when he had eaten a great many of them) goji-bazigwii (he tried to rise to his feet).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Kāwin kạnagä minān ici∙a∙yāsī, kägä‘t minumạdci∙ō∙.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin ganage minaan izhi-ayaasii, geget minomaji’oo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He was feeling very well, truly very well was he feeling.***</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin ganage minaan izhi-ayaasii (not in the least did he feel unwell — *minaan flagged*), geget minomaji’oo (truly well did he feel). **</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Äjimādcāt, kägä‘t minopimusä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ezhi-maajaad, geget minobimose.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When he started to go, really he went walking easily.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ezhi-maajaad (when he started off), geget minobimose (truly he walked along easily).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Kumāgu a‘pī tägwicing kägä‘t kīciwackạtä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gomaa go apii degwishing geget giizhiwashkade.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*After he was come a certain distance, truly his stomach ached.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gomaa go apii degwishing (after he had come a certain distance) geget giizhiwashkade (truly his stomach ached).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Cigwasa ā‘pidci kīciwackạtä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa sa aapiji giizhiwashkade.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Presently he had a very painful ache in the stomach.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa sa aapiji giizhiwashkade (presently his stomach ached very much).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Ningutingigu mägwā pimusät äjipōgitit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo megwaa bimosed ezhi-boogidid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then by and by, while walking along he broke wind.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo megwaa bimosed (then by and by, while he was walking along) ezhi-boogidid (he broke wind).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Kägä‘t kuckupạgiso; äjikwä‘kipạgisut.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Geget goshkopagizo; ezhi-kwekipagizod.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Truly was he startled with surprise; then he whirled about.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Geget goshkopagizo (truly he was startled); ezhi-kwekipagizod (then he whirled about).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) “Awänän känōcit?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Awenen genoozhid?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Who spoke to me?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Awenen genoozhid?” (“who spoke to me?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Āca mīnawā iwiti upi‘kwanāng.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aazha miinawaa iwidi obikwannaang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The same thing over again (he heard) there behind him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aazha miinawaa iwidi obikwannaang (again the same thing, there behind him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Kāga‘pī umi‘tigwābīn uti‘kwạtäwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii omitigwaabiin odikwadewaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Finally he strung his bow.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii omitigwaabiin odikwadewaan (finally he strung his bow — *odikwadewaan flagged*).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) “‘Āᵘ, kạnōciciyu‘k.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaw, ganoozhishiyog.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Now, do you speak to me.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaw, ganoozhishiyog.” (“now, do you speak to me.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) Mägwāgu wī‘pimutcigät, u‘pi‘kwanāng āca mīnawa, “Pūⁿ!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Megwaa go wiipimojiged, obikwanaang aazha miinawaa, “Poo!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And as he was going to shoot, then behind him once again, “poo!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Megwaa go wiipimojiged (and as he was about to shoot), obikwanaang aazha miinawaa, “Poo!” (behind him once again, “poo!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Iwiti u‘pi‘kwanāng Nänabucu kāwīn kägō owâbandạⁿzīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Iwidi obikwannaang Nenabosho gaawiin gegoo owaabandaziin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Over there at his back Nenabosho saw not a thing.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Iwidi obikwannaang (over there at his back) Nenabosho gaawiin gegoo owaabandaziin (Nenabosho saw not a thing).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) “Wägunän dạc iᵘ nwāntạmān?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Wegonen dash iw nwaandamaan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“And what do I hear?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Wegonen dash iw nwaandamaan?” (“and what is this I hear?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Ācagu mīnawā, mīsạgu pācigwạnung äni‘tạnk.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aazha go miinawaa, mii sa go bezhigwanong enitang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And the same thing over again (he heard), and it was the same sound that he heard.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aazha go miinawaa (again the same thing), mii sa go bezhigwanong enitang (and it was the same sound that he heard).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Käga‘pī mādcība‘to, mīgu iᵘ äjikātäpạgisut, “Puⁿᵉ, puⁿᵉ, puⁿᵉ, puⁿᵉ,” inwäni.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii maajiibatoo, mii go iw ezhi-kaade-bagizod, “Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh” inweni.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Finally he started running, whereupon at every step he made, “Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh”, was the sound made.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii maajiibatoo (finally he started running), mii go iw ezhi-kaade-bagizod (and so at every step he took), “Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh” inweni (“Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh” was the sound made).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Ningutingigu pimipa‘tōd käga‘pī nōgiba‘tō, kibi‘tcisä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo bimipatood gegapii noogibatoo, gibijise.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And once, as he was running along, he came to a sudden halt, he ceased running.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo bimipatood (and once, as he was running along) gegapii noogibatoo (at last he came to a halt), gibijise (he stopped short).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) Nänabucu äjikikitut: “‘Āᵘ, mīgāciciyu‘k!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenabosho ezhi-giigidod: “Aaw, miigaazhishiyog!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nenabosho then said: “Now, do you fight me!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenabosho ezhi-giigidod (Nenabosho then said): “Aaw, miigaazhishiyog!” (“now, do you fight me!”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Ānīc kāwīn kägō owâbạndaⁿzīn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aaniish gaawiin gegoo owaabandaziin.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Of course, not a thing did he see.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aaniish gaawiin gegoo owaabandaziin (of course, not a thing did he see).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) Ningutingigu mīnawā mīdạc kägä‘t änigu‘k äjimādcība‘tōt, mīsa untcita, “Puⁿᵉ, puⁿ‘, puⁿᵉ, puⁿ‘,” inwänik.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo miinawaa mii dash gegt enigok ezhi-maajiibatood, mii sa onjida, “Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh”, inwenig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So another time, when he really started to run with all his speed, the same thing happened as before, “Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh”, was the sound that was made.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo miinawaa (so another time again) mii dash gegt enigok ezhi-maajiibatood (when he really set off running with all his speed — *gegt printed; read geget*), mii sa onjida (the same thing happened on purpose), “Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh”, inwenig (“Ponh, ponh, ponh, ponh” was the sound made).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Nänabucu kibi‘tcisä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nenabosho gibijise.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nenabosho stopped in his career.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nenabosho gibijise (Nenabosho stopped short).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) Kā‘kibi‘tcisät, mi‘kwäntān ‘iᵉi’ᵘ kī∙i∙gut īni’ᵘ askibwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaagibijised, mikwendaan i’iw gii-igod iiniw ashkibwaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When running he had come to a stop, he thought of what he had been told by the Artichoke.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaagibijised (when he had come to a stop), mikwendaan i’iw gii-igod iiniw ashkibwaan (he thought of what he had been told by that Artichoke).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) “Māgicānanta nimpōgit.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Maagizhaa nanda nimboogid.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Perhaps I am breaking wind.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Maagizhaa nanda nimboogid.” (“perhaps it is that I am breaking wind.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Äni∙ä∙jimādcāt; äninōmạgäskāt mīnawā pōgiti.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eni-ezhi-maajaad; eni-noomageshkaad miinawaa boogidi.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then he started on his way; when he was come a little ways, again he broke wind.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eni-ezhi-maajaad (then he started on his way); eni-noomageshkaad (when he had gone a little ways) miinawaa boogidi (again he broke wind).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) “Ēⁱ, nimpōgit! Mīsa kä∙i∙cini‘kātạng pitcīnạg kä∙ạ∙nipimādisit, ‘nimpōgit,’ ta∙i∙kito.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ii!, nimboogid! Mii sa ge-izhi-nikaadang bijiinag ge-ani-bimaadizid, ‘nimboogid,’ da-ikido.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Hallo! I am breaking wind! And this is what he who will live in the future shall call it, ‘I am breaking wind,’ he will say.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ii!, nimboogid! (“hallo! I am breaking wind!) Mii sa ge-izhi-nikaadang bijiinag ge-ani-bimaadizid (and this is what the one who is yet to live will call it), ‘nimboogid,’ da-ikido. (‘I am breaking wind,’ he will say.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Päpäcig tapōgisi‘kāgäwạn, askibwā ka‘kinạgu kägō, kayä u‘pinīg kayä wā‘kunạg.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bebezhig da-poogisikaagewan, ashkibwaa gakina go gegoo, gaye opiniig gaye waakonag.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Various things will make (one) windy, (such as) the artichoke and all sorts of things, and potatoes and lichens.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bebezhig da-poogisikaagewan (one thing and another will make one windy), ashkibwaa gakina go gegoo (the artichoke and all sorts of things), gaye opiniig gaye waakonag.” (and potatoes and lichens.”)</span>
