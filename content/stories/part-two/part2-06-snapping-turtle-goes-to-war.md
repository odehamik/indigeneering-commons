MI‘KINĀ‘K NẠNDUBẠNI

<span class="l-fiero">**MIKINAAK NANDOBANI**</span>

SNAPPING-TURTLE GOES TO WAR

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ninguding kīnwä mi‘kinā‘k wīnä‘tagu päejik.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding giiwen’ mikinaak wiin eta go bezhig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once on a time they say a Snapping-Turtle (was) all alone.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding (once) giiwen’ (they say) mikinaak (Snapping-Turtle) wiin eta go (he alone) bezhig (one).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mīdạc kā∙i∙nändạng:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gaa-inendang:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And this was what he thought:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gaa-inendang (what he thought).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) “Pānimān ningadạninạndawâbạmāg käwīdcīwạgwā.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Baanimaa ningad-ani-nandawaabamaag ge-wiijiiwagwaa”.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“After a while I will go seek for those in whose company I shall go.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Baanimaa (after a while) ningad-ani-nandawaabamaag (I will go looking for them) ge-wiijiiwagwaa (those I shall go with).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Mīdạc kīmādcād; ningudingīdạc ogī∙ạ∙ni∙u∙di‘tān wạdciwänz; mēdạc imān kā∙i∙jād ugidā‘ki, mēidạc äjipībāgit:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-maajaad; Ningoding idash ogii-ani-oditaan wajiwens; mii dash imaa gaa-izhaad ogidaaki, mii dash ezhi-biibaagid:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon he departed; and by and by he came to a little hill on the way; and when he got to yonder hill-top, he then cried with a loud voice, saying:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gii-maajaad (he set out); ningoding idash (and by and by) ogii-ani-oditaan (he came along to) wajiwens (a little hill); mii dash imaa (and so there) gaa-izhaad (when he got to) ogidaaki (the hilltop), mii dash (and so) ezhi-biibaagid (he cried aloud):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Awänän käwīdcīwạg tcînạndubạniyān?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Awenen ge-wiijiiwag ji-nandobaniyaan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“With whom shall I go when I set out for war?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Awenen (who) ge-wiijiiwag (will I go with) ji-nandobaniyaan (when I go to war)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Awi∙a∙dạc omạdwäna‘kwä‘tagōn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Awiya dash omadwe-nakwetaagoon:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then came the sound of some one’s voice answering him:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Awiya dash (then someone) omadwe-nakwetaagoon (was heard answering him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) “Nīn kīgawīdcīwîn tcînạndubạniyạn!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Niin giga-wiijiiwin ji-nandobaniyan!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“I will go with you when you go to war!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Niin (I) giga-wiijiiwin (will go with you) ji-nandobaniyan (when you go to war)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) “‘Āu, ondās!” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaw, ondaas!” odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“All right, come hither!” he said to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaw (all right), ondaas (come here)!” odinaan (he said to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Päcudạc pā∙ạ∙yānit īni’u ininiwạn, ogạnōnān mi‘kinā‘k:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Besho dash baa-ayaanid iniw ininiwan, oganoonaan mikinaak:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when near by the man was come, to him Snapping-Turtle spoke, saying:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Besho dash (and when nearby) baa-ayaanid (he was coming about) iniw ininiwan (that man), oganoonaan (spoke to him) mikinaak (Snapping-Turtle):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) “Ckumān pigagwädcīn käta∙i∙jīwumbạnän mīgāzoyạng!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Shkomaa bi-gagwejiin ge-daa-izhiiwambanen miigaazoyang.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Just you come and show what you would do should we get into a fight!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Shkomaa (just) bi-gagwejiin (come and try) ge-daa-izhiiwambanen (what you would do) miigaazoyang (should we fight)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Pạgamāgạndac uda‘kunān ‘aga’u inini.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bagamaagan dash odakonaan a’aw inini.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, a war-club in his hand the man held.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bagamaagan dash (now a war-club) odakonaan (he held) a’aw inini (that man).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Mīdạc käegā‘t pigagwädcīt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget bi-gagwejiid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon truly hither he came, making a show (of what he would do).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) geget (truly) bi-gagwejiid (he came making a show).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) “Hahahu’, hahahu’, hahahu’, hahahu’!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Hahaho’, hahaho’, hahaho’, hahaho’!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Hahahu, hahahu, hahahu, hahaho!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Hahaho’, hahaho’, hahaho’, hahaho’!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Cīgwa umān pạdạgwicininit īni’u ininiwạn ugīpîminīma∙u∙gōn ‘igi’u pạgạmāgạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zhigwa omaa ba-dagwishininid iniw ininiwan ogii-bimi-niima’ogoon i’iw bagamaagan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When here the man was come, at (Snapping-Turtle) he came brandishing the club as if to strike him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa (when) omaa (here) ba-dagwishininid (he was come) iniw ininiwan (that man) ogii-bimi-niima’ogoon (he brandished at him in passing) i’iw bagamaagan (that war-club).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) “Mēdạc kägat gīnibu, mī‘kinā‘k!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii dash geget ginibo mikinaak!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“And now truly you shall die, Snapping-Turtle!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii dash (and now) geget (truly) ginibo (you shall die), mikinaak (Snapping-Turtle)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Mīdạc kīgusāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-goosad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*At that he became afraid of him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gii-goosad (he became afraid of him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) “Intawātci kīwän,” udinān, “māgicā kidānisigō mīgāding,” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Indawaaj giiwen,” odinaan, “maagizhaa gid-anisigoo miigaading,” odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“You had better go back home,” he said to him, “lest perhaps you be slain in battle,” he said to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Indawaaj (you had better) giiwen (go home),” odinaan (he said to him), “maagizhaa (lest perhaps) gid-anisigoo (you be slain) miigaading (in battle),” odinaan (he said to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) Mīdạc kī‘kīwäd ‘aga’u inini kī∙ạ∙nimādcā dac mī‘kinā‘k; mīnawā ninguding kī∙ạ∙nia‘kwāmạdciwä wạdciwing.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-giiwed a’aw inini gii-ani-maajaa dash mikinaak; miinawaa ningoding gii-ani-akwaamajiwe wajiwing.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so, when back home went the man, then on his way went Snapping-Turtle; on up another hill he climbed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gii-giiwed (when he went home) a’aw inini (that man), gii-ani-maajaa dash (then on his way set out) mikinaak (Snapping-Turtle); miinawaa ningoding (and again at one point) gii-ani-akwaamajiwe (he climbed up) wajiwing (a hill).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Mīnawā gīpīpāgi:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miinawaa gii-biibaagi:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Again he called with a loud voice:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miinawaa (again) gii-biibaagi (he called aloud):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) “Awänän gäwīdcīwạgē tcînạndūpạniyān?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Awenen ge-wiijiiwaage ji-nandobaniyaan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“With whom shall I go when I set out for war?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Awenen (who) ge-wiijiiwaage (will I go with) ji-nandobaniyaan (when I go to war)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Mēdạc mạdwäbībāgiwạn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash madwe-biibaagiwan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon came the sound of voices calling aloud:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) madwe-biibaagiwan (came the sound of voices calling).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) “Nīnawind!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Niinawind!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“With us!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Niinawind (with us)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) “Ondās, ondās!” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ondaas, ondaas!” odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Hither, hither!” he said to them.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ondaas (come here), ondaas (come here)!” odinaan (he said to them).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) Kīpitạgwicinōgidạc mîskwādäsiwạg nībiwa.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-bi-dagwishinoog idash miskwaadesiwag niibiwa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So came the Painted-Turtles, arriving in throngs.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-bi-dagwishinoog idash (and they came arriving) miskwaadesiwag (the Painted-Turtles) niibiwa (many).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Mīdạc änād:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash enaad:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then he said to them:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) enaad (he said to them):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) “Skomān kạgwätcīyu‘k kädōdamägobạn mīgāsoyạnk!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Skomaa gagwejiiyog ge-doodamegoban miigaazoyang!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Just you show what you would do should we get into a fight!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Skomaa (just) gagwejiiyog (try, you) ge-doodamegoban (what you would do) miigaazoyang (should we fight)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Mīdac ka‘kina kā∙i∙ji∙o∙dci‘tāwād, tibickō asînīg kījināguziwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gakina gaa-izhi-ojitaawaad, dibishkoo asiniig gii-izhinaagoziwag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon all withdrew into their shells, like stones was their look.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gakina (all) gaa-izhi-ojitaawaad (when they drew into their shells), dibishkoo asiniig (like stones) gii-izhinaagoziwag (they looked).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) “Ānīc, mīsa’ ogōu käwīdcī∙ạ∙gwā,” i‘kido mi‘kinā‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniish mii saa ogow ge-wiijii’agwaa,” ikido mikinaak.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Well, it is with these that I will go,” said Snapping-Turtle.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniish (well), mii saa (it is) ogow (these) ge-wiijii’agwaa (that I will go with),” ikido mikinaak (said Snapping-Turtle).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) Mīdạc kīmādcāwād nạndubạniwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-maajaawaad nandobaniwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon they set out to go to war.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gii-maajaawaad (they set out) nandobaniwaad (going to war).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Ningutingidạc ạninîbāwād tibi‘kạdinig mạdwänạgạmō päejik mîskwādäsi, mīdạc äna∙ạ∙nk: —</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash ani-nibaawaad dibikadinig madwe-nagamo bezhig miskwaadesi, mii dash ena’ang: -**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And one night when they were going to sleep, a certain Turtle was heard singing, and thus he sang:—*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash (and one night) ani-nibaawaad (as they were going to sleep) dibikadinig (when night fell) madwe-nagamo (was heard singing) bezhig miskwaadesi (a certain Turtle), mii dash (and so) ena’ang (this he sang):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) “Mîcī‘känwā! ōdänāngi kīdā‘tawänigōmin yāaha!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mishiikenwaa! oodenaangi gidaatawenigoomin yaaha!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“O Snapping-Turtle! in a town are we prophesied an evil fate, yaaha!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mishiikenwaa! (O Snapping-Turtle!) oodenaangi (in a town) gidaatawenigoomin (we are foretold an evil fate) yaaha!</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Nīndinā‘pawä yāaha, nīndinā‘pawä yāaha, yāaha!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nindinaabawe yaaha, nindinaabawe yaaha!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Such was my dream, yaaha! such was my dream, yaaha, yaaha!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nindinaabawe (such was my dream) yaaha, nindinaabawe (such was my dream) yaaha!</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) Mī‘kinā‘kidạc nwāndawād unīckimigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mikinaak idash nwaandawaad onishkimigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when Snapping-Turtle heard him, he was angered.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mikinaak idash (and Snapping-Turtle) nwaandawaad (when he heard him) onishkimigoon (was angered by him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Mēdạc kā∙i∙ji∙u∙dā‘pinạng umō‘komān kī∙i∙nōdä i∙i∙‘mān ayānit mīdạc gạgwädcimād:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gaa-izhi-odaapinang omookomaan gii-inoode imaa ayaanid mii dash gagwejimaad:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon, taking up his knife, he crawled over to where he was, and then asked him:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) gaa-izhi-odaapinang (taking up) omookomaan (his knife) gii-inoode (he crawled) imaa ayaanid (to where he was) mii dash (and so) gagwejimaad (he asked him):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) “ānīn ä‘kidoyan?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniin ekidoyan?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“What did you say?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniin (what) ekidoyan (did you say)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) “Kā, ūdänāng iīzạn ka‘kina kīnisigōmin nindinābạndạm,” i‘kido.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa, oodenaang iizan gakina ginisigoomin nindinaabandam,” ikido.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Why, that in a town we shall probably all be slain, was what I dreamed,” he said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaa (why), oodenaang (in a town) iizan (it seems) gakina (all) ginisigoomin (we shall be slain) nindinaabandam (was what I dreamed),” ikido (he said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) Mēdạc mi‘kinā‘k kā∙i∙jikīckigwäjwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash mikinaak gaa-izhi-giishkigwezhwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon Snapping-Turtle cut off his head.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) mikinaak (Snapping-Turtle) gaa-izhi-giishkigwezhwaad (cut off his head).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Mēdạc minawā kī∙ạ∙nimādcāwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash miinawaa gii-ani-maajaawaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And then they continued on their way.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) miinawaa (again) gii-ani-maajaawaad (they went on their way).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) A‘pī∙i∙dạc wädisāwād anicinābä odä‘tōnit, mi‘kinā‘k ogī∙i∙nāg ka‘kina ucîmagạnīcimag:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii dash wedisowaad anishinaabe oodetoonid, mikinaak ogii-inaag gakina ozhimaaganishima’:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when they came to where some people had a town, Snapping-Turtle said to all his soldiers:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii dash (and when) wedisowaad (they came to) anishinaabe oodetoonid (where people had a town), mikinaak (Snapping-Turtle) ogii-inaag (said to) gakina (all) ozhimaaganishima’ (his soldiers):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) “Mī∙u∙mān ayaiyu‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii omaa ayaayog.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“In this place do you remain.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii omaa (here) ayaayog (do you stay).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) Ningạtijā, ningạnadawā‘to.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningad-izhaa, ninga-nadawaatoo.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*I will go thither, I will go to reconnoitre.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningad-izhaa (I will go), ninga-nadawaatoo (I will go to scout).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(42) Päejik uwīdcīwan umijinawämạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bezhig owiijiiwaan omizhinaweman.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He went along with one of his attendants.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bezhig (one) owiijiiwaan (he went along with) omizhinaweman (his attendant).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(43) Mīdạc ijāwād ayānik wigiwāmạn, päejikidạc cābundawāning kī‘pīndigäwạg nībādîbi‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash izhaawaad ayaanig wiigiwaaman, bezhig idash zhaabondawaaning gii-biindigewag nibaa-dibik.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And going over to where there were some wigwams, into a certain long-lodge they entered during the silence of the night.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) izhaawaad (going to) ayaanig wiigiwaaman (where there were lodges), bezhig idash (and into a certain) zhaabondawaaning (long-lodge) gii-biindigewag (they entered) nibaa-dibik (in the dead of night).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(44) Mēdạc änād uwīdcīwāgạnạn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash enaad owiijiiwaaganan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And this he said to his comrade:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and so) enaad (he said to) owiijiiwaaganan (his comrade):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(45) “Bäbäjik kīckigwäjwādā!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Bebezhig giishkigwezhwaadaa!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Let us each cut off a head!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Bebezhig (each one) giishkigwezhwaadaa (let us cut off a head)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(46) Päpäjikidạc ogīnisāwān ạnicinābän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bebezhig idash ogii-nisaawaan anishinaaben.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So each slew a person.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bebezhig idash (and each one) ogii-nisaawaan (slew) anishinaaben (a person).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(47) Ōdayā∙u∙bīnōmunāwān ustigwānan.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Oodayaa’obiinoomonaawaan oshtigwaanan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*They concealed the heads in the bosom of their garments.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Oodayaa’obiinoomonaawaan (they hid in their bosoms) oshtigwaanan (the heads).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(48) Mīdạc kā∙i∙jikāzowād; mi‘tigwanāgạn a‘tä, imān dạc pīndik mī∙i∙mā kī‘kāzōwād.</span>
