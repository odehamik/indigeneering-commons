WÄNDCĪWĀT ẠNIMUCẠG.

<span class="l-fiero">**WENJIIWAAD ANIMOOSHAG**</span>

THE ORIGIN OF DOGS

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ninguding nīⁿj anicinābäg pîmickāwād tcīmāning kistcigạming iwidi kīwädinunk ina‘ka‘kä mī∙i∙’ᵘ kā∙i∙jikistcinōdininik; mī∙i∙dec kā∙i∙jiwäbāciwāt micawạgām kistcigạmīng; kāwīn ugīwâbạndaⁿzīnāwa a‘ki.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding niizh anishinaabeg bimishkaawaad jiimaaning gichigaming iwidi giiwedinong inakake mii iw gaa-izhi-gichi-noodininig; mii dash gaa-izhiwebaazhiwaad mizhawagaam gichigaming; gaawiin ogii-waabandanziinaawaa aki.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once on a time two men were paddling along in their canoe on the sea by a northerly route, when there arose a mighty wind; accordingly they were blown by the wind far out at sea; they did not see land.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding (once) niizh anishinaabeg (two people) bimishkaawaad jiimaaning (were paddling along in a canoe) gichigaming (on the sea) iwidi giiwedinong inakake (yonder toward the north) mii iw gaa-izhi-gichi-noodin (when there arose a great wind).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Ā‘pidci kīkistcinōdinini, kāwīn kīkunạbiskāsīwạg; kînwäⁿj kistcigạmīng kī‘pabā∙a∙yāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aapiji gii-gichi-noodinini, gaawiin gii-gonaboshkaasiiwag; ginwenzh gichigaming gii-babaa-ayaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Very strong blew the wind, but they were not capsized; a long while over the sea they continued aimlessly.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aapiji gii-gichi-noodinini (very strongly it blew), gaawiin gii-gonaboshkaasiiwag (they were not capsized); ginwenzh gichigaming (for a long while on the sea) gii-babaa-ayaawag (they went about aimlessly).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Ninguding kī∙ạ∙gwāwāpahōwạg ạgāma‘kīng.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding gii-agwaaweba’oowag agaamakiing.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In course of time they were carried by the sea upon shore.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding (in time) gii-agwaaweba’oowag (they were washed ashore by the waves) agaamakiing (onto the land across the water).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Kī‘kabâwāt owâbạndānāwa awiya pimi‘kawänit kistanicinābän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-gabaawaad owaabandaanawaa awiya bimikawenid gichi-anishinaaben.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When they landed, they saw the footprints of some mighty human being.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-gabaawaad (when they had landed) owaabandaanawaa (they saw) awiya bimikawenid (someone’s tracks passing) gichi-anishinaaben (of a great person).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Mīdạc kīsāgisiwad ugīku‘piwidōnāwa udcīmāniwa, mīdạc i∙i∙māⁿ unāmōnag kī‘kāsowād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-zegiziwaad ogii-gopiwidoonaawaa ojiimaaniwaa, mii dash i’imaa anaamoonag gii-gaazowaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Therefore, becoming alarmed, they carried their canoe up from the shore, and then there underneath they hid themselves.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-zegiziwaad (and so, being afraid) ogii-gopiwidoonaawaa ojiimaaniwaa (they carried their canoe up from the shore), mii dash i’imaa anaamoonag (and then there underneath it) gii-gaazowaad (they hid themselves).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Ninguding unūndānāwa kägō mạdwäsîninig; mīdec inābiwād wâbạndānāwa kistcipigwa‘k a‘tänig.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding onoondaanaawaa gegoo madwesininig; mii dash inaabiwaad waabandaanaawaa gichi-bikwak atenig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*By and by they heard the sound of something fall; and when they looked, they saw a huge arrow there.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding (by and by) onoondaanaawaa gegoo madwesininig (they heard something fall with a sound); mii dash inaabiwaad (and when they peeked) waabandaanaawaa gichi-bikwak atenig (they saw a huge arrow lying there).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Mīdạc käᵒgä‘t sägisiwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget zegiziwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon, to be sure, were they afraid.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget zegiziwaad (and then truly they were afraid).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Wībạgu ugīpi∙u∙disigūwän kistci∙u∙nicinābän, ugạnōnigūwän:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wiiba go ogii-bi-odisikoowaan gichi-anishinaaben, oganoonigowaan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And in a little while to where they were came a great human being, and they were addressed by him saying:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wiiba go (in a little while) ogii-bi-odisikoowaan (they were come upon by) gichi-anishinaaben (a great person), oganoonigowaan (and they were spoken to by him):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) “Nicīmä, kägu sägisi‘kägun!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nishiime, gego zegizikegon!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My little brothers, do not be afraid!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nishiime (my little brothers), gego zegizikegon! (do not be afraid!)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Nīn misābä ägōyān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Niin misaabe egooyaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*I am he who is called Giant,*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Niin misaabe egooyaan (I am the one called Giant).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Kāwīn nīn nimbạnādci∙ā∙sīg ạnicinābäg,” udigōwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin niin nimbanaaji’aasiig anishinaabeg,” odigowaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*I do not destroy people,” they were told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin niin nimbanaaji’aasiig anishinaabeg (I do not harm people),” odigowaan (they were told by him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Owâbamāwān adi‘kwan cägwazonit, mī wīn ini’ᵘ päbāpîmwāt wī∙a∙mwät ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mîsābä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Owaabamaawaan adikwan zhegwazonid, mii wiin iniw bebaabimwaad wii-amwaad a’aw misaabe.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*They saw a caribou hanging from the girdle at his side; and that was what Giant had wandered off to shoot (with his arrow), that he might have it to eat.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Owaabamaawaan adikwan zhegwazonid (they saw a caribou hanging at his side), mii wiin iniw bebaabimwaad wii-amwaad a’aw misaabe (that was the thing Giant had gone roving to shoot, wanting to eat it).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Mī∙i∙dec kā∙i∙ji∙u∙dā‘piniguwād ā‘pidci mindidōwạn ini’ᵘ mîsābän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii idash gaa-izhi-odaapinigowaad aapiji mindidowan iniw misaaben.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon were they taken up by Giant, who was so very huge.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii idash gaa-izhi-odaapinigowaad (and so they were taken up) aapiji mindidowan iniw misaaben (by that Giant who was so very big).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Ugīpīndūmunān ini’ᵘ unicinābän ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mîsābä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-biindomonaan iniw anishinaaben a’aw misaabe.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*In the bosom of his garment Giant carried the people.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-biindomonaan iniw anishinaaben a’aw misaabe (Giant carried the people in the bosom of his garment).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Mīdạc kī‘kīwäwinigowāt ändānit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-giiwewinigowaad endaanid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so they were carried back to where he lived.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-giiwewinigowaad endaanid (and so they were carried back to where he lived).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) A‘pī∙i∙dạc kā‘pīndigäwād, nānāgä kī‘pīndigä ‘aᵘ wīndigō.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii idash gaa-biindigewaad, naanaage gii-biindige aw wiindigoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when they had gone in, then after a while in went the Wīndigō.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii idash gaa-biindigewaad (and when they had gone in), naanaage gii-biindige aw wiindigoo (after a while the Wiindigoo came in).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) Mīdạc änāt ini’ᵘ mîsābän ‘aᵉa’ᵘ wīndigō:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash enaad iniw misaaben a’aw wiindigoo:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon to Giant said the Wīndigō:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash enaad iniw misaaben a’aw wiindigoo (and so the Wiindigoo said to Giant):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) “Anicinābäg kîdayāwāg,” udinān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Anishinaabeg gidayaawaag,” odinaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Some people you have,” he said to him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Anishinaabeg gidayaawaag (you have people),” odinaan (he said to him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Ugīkāniguwän pīndig āndānit ini’ᵘ mîsābän tcîbwāpīndigänit ini’ᵘ wīndigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-gaanigowaan biindig endaanid iniw misaaben jibwaa-biindigenid iniw wiindigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*They had been concealed inside of where Giant lived before the Wīndigō came in.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-gaanigowaan biindig endaanid iniw misaaben (they had been hidden inside Giant’s dwelling by him) jibwaa-biindigenid iniw wiindigoon (before the Wiindigoo came in).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) “Kāwīn anicinābäg nindayāwāsīg,” i‘kidō ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mîsābä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Gaawiin anishinaabeg nindayaawaasiig,” ikido a’aw misaabe.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“No people have I,” said Giant.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Gaawiin anishinaabeg nindayaawaasiig (I have no people),” ikido a’aw misaabe (said Giant).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) “Käᵒgä‘t,” i‘kido ‘aᵉa’ᵘ wīndigō.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Geget,” ikido a’aw wiindigoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Yes, (you have),” said the Wīndigō.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Geget (truly, you do),” ikido a’aw wiindigoo (said the Wiindigoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Mīgu äjinōndāguzit i‘kwanāmut.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii go ezhi-noondaagozid ikwanaamod.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly the sound of him was heard as he drew in his breath.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii go ezhi-noondaagozid (and then he was heard making a sound) ikwanaamod (as he drew in his breath).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) “Ūp!”¹ i‘kunāmut ‘aᵉa’ᵘ wīndigō, ä‘pîtcimîsawänimāt anicinābän wī∙ạ∙mwät.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Oop!” ikoonamod a’aw wiindigoo, epiichi-misawenimaad anishinaaben wii-amwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Up!”1 was the way the Wīndigō sucked in his breath, so eager was he to have the people to eat.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Oop!” ikoonamod a’aw wiindigoo (“Oop!” was the sound the Wiindigoo made drawing in his breath), epiichi-misawenimaad anishinaaben wii-amwaad (so much did he crave the people, wanting to eat them).</span>

*1 Uttered by drawing in the breath.*

<span class="l-orig">(24) Käga‘pī∙i∙gu nickādisi kînwäⁿj kạgạnōnitiwāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii igo nishkaadizi ginwenzh gaganoonidiwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*At length he grew angry while in long conversation with (Giant).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii igo nishkaadizi (at last he grew angry) ginwenzh gaganoonidiwaad (as the two of them talked together a long while).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Käga‘pī kayä wīn mîsābä nîckādisi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii gaye wiin misaabe nishkaadizi.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Finally Giant too became angry.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii gaye wiin misaabe nishkaadizi (finally Giant too grew angry).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) Mi‘tigōnāgạn a‘täni imāⁿ pīndik āndāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mitigonaagan ateni imaa biindig endaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*A wooden bowl was there inside of where he lived.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mitigonaagan ateni imaa biindig endaad (a wooden bowl lay there inside where he lived).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Mīdạc i∙i∙’ᵘ kāpā‘ki‘kwādạnk, mīdạc imāⁿ ạnimōⁿs ayāt i∙i∙māⁿ anāmaya∙ī∙unāgạning, udayänsạn ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mîsābä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash i’iw gabaagigwaadang, mii dash imaa animoons ayaad imaa anaamaya’ii onaaganing, odayaansan a’aw misaabe.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so, prying it up with a stick, behold! a dog was there underneath the bowl, the pet of Giant.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash i’iw gabaagigwaadang (and so, prying it up with a stick), mii dash imaa animoons ayaad (there was a little dog) imaa anaamaya’ii onaaganing (there beneath the bowl), odayaansan a’aw misaabe (the little pet of Giant).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) “Taga, packwadāc, awimīgāc ‘aᵉa’ᵘ mạdcianicinābä.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Daga, bashkwadaash, awi-miigaazh a’aw maji-anishinaabe.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Up, Hairless, go fight the impious man!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Daga, bashkwadaash (come now, Hairless), awi-miigaazh a’aw maji-anishinaabe (go fight that bad person).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) Mī∙i∙’ᵘ käᵒgät pazigwīt ‘aᵉa’ᵘ animōⁿs;</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii iw geget bazigwiid a’aw animoons;**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So then truly up to his feet rose the dog;*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii iw geget bazigwiid a’aw animoons (and then truly the little dog rose to his feet);</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) kī‘pa‘pawi, mīdạc kī∙ạ∙nimindidut.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**gii-babawi, mii dash gii-ani-mindidod.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*he shook himself, whereupon he began growing in size.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">gii-babawi (he shook himself), mii dash gii-ani-mindidod (and then he began growing big).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Äckạm kī∙ạ∙nipa‘pawi ‘aᵉa’ᵘ animuc, a‘pidci kī∙ạ∙niminditu ‘aᵉa’ᵘ ạnimuc.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eshkam gii-ani-babawi a’aw animoosh, aapiji gii-ani-mindidod a’aw animoosh.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*The more the dog shook himself, ever so much larger he continued to grow.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eshkam gii-ani-babawi a’aw animoosh (the more the dog kept shaking himself), aapiji gii-ani-mindidod a’aw animoosh (the bigger the dog kept growing).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) A‘pī∙i∙dac wayâbạmāt ạnimūn mindidunit kī∙ạ∙nisāga∙ạ∙m ‘aᵉa’ᵘ wīndigō.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii dash wayaabamaad animoon mindidonid gii-ani-zaaga’am a’aw wiindigoo.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So, when the Wīndigō saw the dog that now was (so) big, then on out of doors he went.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii dash wayaabamaad animoon mindidonid (and when he saw the dog grown big) gii-ani-zaaga’am a’aw wiindigoo (the Wiindigoo went out of doors).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) Ugīkạgānzumān udayạn tcînîsāt wīndigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-gagaanzomaan odayaan ji-nisaad wiindigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*(Giant) urged on his pet to slay the Wīndigō.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-gagaanzomaan odayaan ji-nisaad wiindigoon (he urged on his pet to kill the Wiindigoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Mī∙i∙dạc kīmīgānāt ini’ᵘ wīndigōn ‘aᵉa’ᵘ animuc pīnic uginisān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-miigaanaad iniw wiindigoon a’aw animoosh biinish ogii-nisaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly the dog fought with the Wīndigō till he slew him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-miigaanaad iniw wiindigoon a’aw animoosh (and so the dog fought that Wiindigoo) biinish ogii-nisaan (until he killed him).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) A‘pī∙i∙dac kānisāt, minawā kī‘pīndigä ‘aᵉa’ᵘ ạnimuc.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii idash gaa-nisaad, miinawaa gii-biindige a’aw animoosh.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when he had slain him, then back inside came the dog.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii idash gaa-nisaad (and when he had killed him), miinawaa gii-biindige a’aw animoosh (the dog came back inside again).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) Kī‘pa‘pạwi minawā; äckạmigu kī∙a∙gācīⁿyiⁿ, änigini‘pạn mīnawā ki∙i∙nigini.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-babawi miinawaa; eshkam igo gii-agaashiinyi eniginiban miinawaa gii-inigini.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He shook himself once more; and smaller he grew, as big as he was before was now his size again.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-babawi miinawaa (he shook himself again); eshkam igo gii-agaashiinyi (steadily he grew smaller) eniginiban miinawaa gii-inigini (and was once more the size he had been).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) Minawā kī‘pīndigä imāⁿ anāmaya∙ī∙ unāgạnicing.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miinawaa gii-biindige imaa anaamaya’ii onaaganishing.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Back he went beneath the miserable bowl.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miinawaa gii-biindige imaa anaamaya’ii onaaganishing (again he went in there beneath the wretched bowl).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Mī∙i∙dạc ägōwāt ini’ᵘ mîsābän:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash egowaad iniw misaaben:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon they were told by Giant:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash egowaad iniw misaaben (and so they were told by Giant):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) “Nicīmä∙ī∙dug, undcida ningī∙i∙nändam tcibījaiyäg o∙o∙māⁿ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nishiimeyidog, onjida ningii-inendam ji-bi-izhaayeg o’omaa.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My little brothers, a purpose did I have in mind, that you should come here.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nishiimeyidog (my little brothers), onjida ningii-inendam ji-bi-izhaayeg o’omaa (on purpose I had it in mind that you should come here).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) Wīwâbạmininạgu‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wii-waabamininagog.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*I wanted to see you.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wii-waabamininagog (I wanted to see you).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) Mīsai indawā tcîgīwäyäg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa indawaa ji-giiweyeg.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Therefore now you may go back home.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa indawaa ji-giiweyeg (so now you may go home).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(42) Waᵉawä nindayäⁿs kimīninim.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Wa’awe nindayaans gimiininim.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*This little pet of mine I will give to you.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Wa’awe nindayaans gimiininim (this little pet of mine I give to you).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(43) Kāwīn minawā tabigīwäsi.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin miinawaa da-bi-giiwesii.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Not back here will he come again.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin miinawaa da-bi-giiwesii (he will not come back here again).</span>
