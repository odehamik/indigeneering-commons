NAMÄ WÄDÄCKANIT.

<span class="l-fiero">**NAME WEDESHKANID.**</span>

THE HORNED STURGEON.

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ninguding ạnicinābäg kī‘pōsiwạg nạndawâbạmāwād nạmäwạn; ạni‘tīn udayānāwān; mîcawạgān ijāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding anishinaabeg gii-booziwag nandawaabamaawaad namewan; anitiin odayaanaawaan; mishawagaan izhaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once some people got into (their canoes) to look for sturgeons; some spears they had; far out at sea they went.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding (once) anishinaabeg (the people) gii-booziwag (got into their canoes) nandawaabamaawaad (seeking to see) namewan (sturgeon); anitiin (spears) odayaanaawaan (they had them); mishawagaan (far out on the water) izhaawag (they went).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mī∙i∙dạc inābiwād ạnāmīndîm, ayā‘pīwâbạmāwān nạmäwạn, mī∙i∙’ᵘ pạcibawāwād nạmäwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii idash inaabiwaad anaamiindim, ayaapii-waabamaawaan namewan, mii iw bazhibawaawaad namewan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And while looking down into the water, every now and then they beheld a sturgeon, whereupon they thrust a spear at the sturgeon.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii idash (and so) inaabiwaad (as they looked) anaamiindim (down into the depths), ayaapii-waabamaawaan (every now and then they saw) namewan (a sturgeon), mii iw (whereupon) bazhibawaawaad (they speared) namewan (the sturgeon).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Ninguding päᵉjik ạnicinābä mạdwägīgito: “Ō, naskä kuca’ äjināguzit waᵉa’ᵘ nạmä! Utäckạni indigu mōⁿsōnk äjināgusinit udäckạni!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding bezhig anishinaabe madwe-giigido: “Oo, nashke gosha ezhinaagozid wa’aw name! Odeshkani indigo moozoong ezhinaagozinid odeshkani!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*By and by a certain man was heard saying: “Oh, behold the form of this sturgeon! It has horns in the same manner as a moose!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding (by and by) bezhig anishinaabe (one person) madwe-giigido (was heard saying): “Oo, nashke gosha (oh, just look) ezhinaagozid (how it looks) wa’aw name (this sturgeon)! Odeshkani (it has horns) indigo moozoong (just like a moose) ezhinaagozinid (is how they look) odeshkani (its horns)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Mī∙i∙dac ka‘kina kī‘pījāwād tcīmānạn tciwâbạmāwād äjināgusinit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii idash gakina gii-bi-izhaawaad jiimaanan ji-waabamaawaad ezhinaagozinid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon all came in canoes to see how (the sturgeon) looked.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii idash gakina (and then all) gii-bi-izhaawaad (came over) jiimaanan (in canoes) ji-waabamaawaad (to see it) ezhinaagozinid (how it looked).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) “Käᵉgä‘t udäckạni!” i‘kidōwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Geget odeshkani!” ikidowag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Verily, it is horned!” they said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Geget (truly) odeshkani (it has horns)!” ikidowag (they said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Mīsaᵉ mạnidō nạmä!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii sa manidoo name!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“That is a manitou sturgeon!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii sa (that is) manidoo name (a manidoo sturgeon)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Mēdạc sạgaswāwād, sämān gayä gī∙a∙sāwād nibī‘kāng.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash zagaswaawaad, asemaan gaye gii-asaawaad nibiikaang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly they smoked, some tobacco too they put into the water.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash zagaswaawaad (and so they smoked), asemaan gaye (and tobacco) gii-asaawaad (they placed him) nibiikaang (into the water).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Kā∙i∙ckwāsạgaswāwād ingudci kī∙a∙ni∙i∙jāwag.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-ishkwaa-zagaswaawaad ingoji gii-ani-izhaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When they had finished smoking, they went away.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-ishkwaa-zagaswaawaad (when they had finished smoking) ingoji (off somewhere) gii-ani-izhaawag (they went away).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Päᵉjik idạc tcīmān mīnawā kī∙i∙jāwạg, mīnawā owâbạmāwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bezhig idash jiimaan miinawaa gii-izhaawag, miinawaa owaabamaawaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, another canoe (of people) went thither, and they saw (the sturgeon).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bezhig idash jiimaan (and another canoe) miinawaa gii-izhaawag (went there again), miinawaa owaabamaawaan (and again they saw it).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Mī∙i∙’ᵘ äji∙o∙dā‘pinạng odạni‘t, ‘aᵉa’ᵘ ạnicinābä ogīpajibawān īni’ᵘ nạmäwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii iw ezhi-odaapinang odanit, a’aw anishinaabe ogii-bazhibawaan iniw namewan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Then, taking up his spear, (one of) the men thrust it into the sturgeon.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii iw ezhi-odaapinang (then taking up) odanit (his spear), a’aw anishinaabe (that man) ogii-bazhibawaan (speared) iniw namewan (the sturgeon).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) A‘pī∙i∙dac mwā‘kibwīnnāt¹ m∙ī∙’ᵘ wâbạmāt mīgisiwạn ạgu‘kyänit imāⁿ nạmä ustigwāning.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii idash mwaakibiinaad mii iw waabamaad migiziwan agokyenid imaa name oshtigwaaning.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when they brought it up from the water, then they beheld a bald eagle clinging fast to the head of the sturgeon.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii idash (and when) mwaakibiinaad (he pulled it up out of the water) mii iw waabamaad (then he saw) migiziwan (a bald eagle) agokyenid (clinging fast) imaa name oshtigwaaning (there on the sturgeon’s head).</span>

*¹ Evidently an error. I cannot suggest the correct word. — T. M. (Michelson’s 1919 note; Jones printed “mwā‘kibwīnnāt”, read here as mookibiin- “pull s.o. up out of the water” with initial change.)*

<span class="l-orig">(12) Mīdạc kīkistcipā‘piwād ka‘kina ạnicinābäg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-gichi-baapiwaad gakina anishinaabeg.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon heartily did all the people laugh.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash (and then) gii-gichi-baapiwaad (heartily did they laugh) gakina anishinaabeg (all the people).</span>
