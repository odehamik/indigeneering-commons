PĪJYᵘ.

<span class="l-fiero">**BIZHIW.**</span>

THE LYNX.

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Nīguding isa kīⁿwäⁿ, ā∙ī∙ndāgobạn pījyᵘ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding isa giiwenh, ayindaagoban bizhiw.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once on a time, they say, there used to live a Lynx.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding isa giiwenh (now, once upon a time, they say), ayindaagoban bizhiw (there used to dwell a Lynx).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mī∙i∙māⁿ ändạjinạndawändcigäd, ạnōdcigägōn oní‘tōn—wâbōzōn pinäwạn kayä cīcībạn kayä ninguding onisān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii imaa endazhi-nandawenjiged, anooji gegoon onitoon - waaboozoon binewan gaye zhiishiiban gaye ningoding onisaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*There at the place where he hunted, every kind of things he killed, - hares and ruffed grouse and ducks he sometimes killed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii imaa endazhi-nandawenjiged (and there at the place where he hunted), anooji gegoon onitoon (every kind of thing he killed) — waaboozoon binewan gaye zhiishiiban gaye (hares and ruffed grouse and ducks) ningoding onisaan (he sometimes killed).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Ningudingidạc ogī∙u∙disigōn wīdcipijiwạn, mī∙i∙dạc änādcimu‘tāgud:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash ogii-odisigoon wiiji-bizhiwan, mii dash enajimotaagod:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once he was visited by a fellow-Lynx. And this was what he was told:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash (and once) ogii-odisigoon wiiji-bizhiwan (he was visited by a fellow Lynx), mii dash enajimotaagod (and this was what he was told):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) “Päji‘k wạdciᵘ i‘widi ayāmạdgạd, kīckā‘pi‘kā, gäga‘tsa ki‘tci∙u∙nicicin inābing wāsa a‘ki täbābạmināgwạd.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Bezhig wajiw iwidi ayaamagad, giishkaabikaa, geget sa gichi-onizhishin inaabing waasa aki debaabaminaagwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“There is a mountain off yonder, it has steep sides, and truly a very fine view of distant country can be seen.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Bezhig wajiw iwidi ayaamagad (there is a mountain off yonder), giishkaabikaa (it has steep, sheer rock sides), geget sa gichi-onizhishin (truly it is very fine), inaabing (when one looks) waasa aki debaabaminaagwad (the far-off country can be seen at a distance).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Nạckä ijān kawâbạndān.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Nashke izhaan ga-waabandaan.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Better go and see it.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Nashke izhaan (go ahead, go) ga-waabandaan (and you will see it).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Äⁿᵉ, nīngạdijā,” i‘kido ‘aᵉaᵘ pījyᵘ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Enh’, ningad-izhaa,” ikido a’aw bizhiw.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Yes, I will go,” said Lynx.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Enh’, ningad-izhaa (yes, I will go),” ikido a’aw bizhiw (said that Lynx).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Mī∙i∙dạc wayābạng kīmādcād pījyᵘ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash wayaabang gii-maajaad bizhiw.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so on the morrow away started Lynx.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash wayaabang (and so on the morrow) gii-maajaad bizhiw (away started Lynx).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) A‘pī∙i∙dạc kāsāgạtciwäd mī∙i∙’ᵘ kīwâbạndạng wāsa’ a‘ki täbināgwạdinig.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii dash gaa-zaagajiwed mii iw gii-waabandang waasa aki debinaagwadinig.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when he came out upon the mountain, he thereupon saw afar the country that could be seen at a distance.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii dash gaa-zaagajiwed (and when he came up out onto the mountain), mii iw gii-waabandang (thereupon he saw) waasa aki debinaagwadinig (the far-off country that could be seen at a distance).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Mī∙i∙dạc kā∙i∙ji∙o∙nạbid inābit, pä‘kic nīskīngwänit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gaa-izhi-onabid inaabid, bekish niiskiingwenid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so, after he was seated, he looked, and at the same time he was squinting,*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gaa-izhi-onabid (and so, after he sat down) inaabid (he looked), bekish niiskiingwenid (and at the same time he was squinting).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Mīsa∙ⁱ∙ ä‘kōsit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa ekoozid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*That is as far as (the story) goes.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa ekoozid (that is as far as the story goes).</span>
