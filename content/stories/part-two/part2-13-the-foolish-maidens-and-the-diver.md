<span class="l-fiero">**Majikikwewisag Zhingibis Gaye.**</span>

The Foolish Maidens and the Diver

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ningudingisa’, kīⁿwäⁿ ạnicinābäg tāᵒwag, ōdä‘tōwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding sa, giiwenh anishinaabeg daawag, oodetoowag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once on a time they say that some people were abiding there, they were living in a town.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding sa (now once on a time) giiwenh (so they say) anishinaabeg daawag (some people dwelt), oodetoowag (they lived in a town).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Päjik idạc a‘kiwänzi wīwạn gayä a‘yāwạn, nīⁿjidạc uckinīgi‘kwän udayāwâwān odānisiwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bezhig idash akiwenzii wiiwan gaye ayaawan, niizh idash oshkiniigikwen odayaawaawaan, odaanisiwaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*There was an old man, and his wife was there, and two maidens who were their daughters.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bezhig idash akiwenzii (and one old man) wiiwan gaye ayaawan (and his wife was there too), niizh idash oshkiniigikwen odayaawaawaan (and two young women they had), odaanisiwaan (their daughters).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) A‘pī∙i∙dạc nä‘tāwīgiwād, īgi’ᵘ i‘kwäwạg ogạnōnigowān ugīwān:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii idash netaawigiwaad, igiw ikwewag oganoonigowaan ogiiwaan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when they had come to maturity, the women were addressed by their mother saying:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii idash netaawigiwaad (and when they had grown up) igiw ikwewag (those women) oganoonigowaan ogiiwaan (were spoken to by their mother), saying:</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) “Nindānis, ningudci awi∙ä∙yāyu‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nindaanis, ningoji awi-ayaayok.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My daughters, somewhere shall you go and stay.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nindaanis (my daughters) ningoji awi-ayaayok (go off and stay somewhere).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Kägu’ omāⁿ odänāng ayā‘kyägun.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gego omaanh odenaang ayaakyegon.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Do not remain in this town.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gego omaanh odenaang ayaakyegon (do not stay here in this town).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Mī∙i∙dạc kīmādcāwād īgi’ᵘ uckînīgi‘kwäg, pabāmosäwạg; mī∙i∙dac i‘ku nībāwād pīna‘kạmigāng.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-maajaawaad igiw oshkiniigikweg, babaamosewag; mii idash iko nibaawaad biinakamigaang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon away started the maidens, about over the country they went tramping; and then it was continually their custom to sleep in a clean open place in the forest.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-maajaawaad (and so off they went) igiw oshkiniigikweg (those young women), babaamosewag (they went tramping about); mii idash iko nibaawaad (and so it was their custom to sleep) biinakamigaang (in a clean open place on the ground).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Ningudingidạc täpi‘kạdinig cingicinowād ā‘pitcimica‘kwạtini; mē∙i∙dạc kạnawâbamāwād ạnāngōᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash debikadinig zhingishinowaad aapiji mizhakwadini; mii idash ganawaabamaawaad anangoo’.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, one night while they lay abed, the sky was thick with stars; and so they gazed up at the stars.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash debikadinig (now once when night fell) zhingishinowaad (as they lay down) aapiji mizhakwadini (the sky was very clear); mii idash ganawaabamaawaad anangoo’ (and so they gazed at the stars).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Mēdạc ä‘kidot ‘aᵉaᵘ säzī‘kizit i‘kwä:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ekidod a’aw zeziikizid ikwe:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And now said the elder woman:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ekidod (and so she said) a’aw zeziikizid ikwe (the older woman):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) “Nyā, nicīⁿmä, nạskäkuca ạnāngō‘k äjināgusiwād!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Niyaa, nishiime, nashke gosha anangoog ezhinaagoziwaad!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Dear me, my little sister, do behold the sight of the stars!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Niyaa (dear me), nishiime (my little sister), nashke gosha (do but look) anangoog ezhinaagoziwaad (how the stars appear)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Mēdạc kägä‘t inābît ‘aᵉaᵘ ucīmä∙i∙māⁿ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget inaabid a’aw oshiimenh imaanh.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon indeed then looked the younger sister.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget inaabid (and so indeed she looked up) a’aw oshiimenh imaanh (the younger sister, over there).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Mīdạc ä‘kidot ‘aᵉaᵘ säzī‘kizît:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ekidod a’aw zeziikizid:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So then said the elder one:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ekidod (and so she said) a’aw zeziikizid (the older one):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) “Ānīn gīn ‘aᵉaᵘ a’‘pägic wī‘pämạg änändạmạn?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniin giin a’aw apegish wiipemag enendaman?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Which of them, in your mind, would you wish to sleep with?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniin giin a’aw (which one would you) apegish wiipemag enendaman (wish to lie with, in your mind)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) “Nyāⁿ, pîsān taga!</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Niyaanh, bizaan daga!**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Gracious me, do keep still!*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Niyaanh (gracious me), bizaan daga (do be still)!</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Kägu anōdc i‘kito‘kän!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gego anooj ikidoken!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Cease talking such nonsense!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gego anooj ikidoken (do not talk such nonsense)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) “Mānōpinạnān, nicīmäⁿ!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Maanoo bina naan, nishiimenh!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Oh, let us keep up the subject, my little sister!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Maanoo bina naan (oh, let us keep it up), nishiimenh (my little sister)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Käga‘pī∙i∙dac kī∙i∙‘kido ‘aᵉaᵘ wäcīmä∙i∙mind:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii idash gii-ikido a’aw weshiime’imind:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So at last said the younger sister:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii idash gii-ikido (and at last she spoke) a’aw weshiime’imind (the one who was the younger sister):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) “Mîsạnī∙i∙nā ‘aᵉawä ā‘pitci kāwâpiskizît ānāng käwī‘pämạg,” i‘kido.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii sa nii[n] inaa a’awe aapiji gaa-waabishkizid anang ge-wiipemag,” ikido.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Now, the one I should prefer to lie with is the star that looks so very white,” she said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii sa niin inaa a’awe (the one for me would be) aapiji gaa-waabishkizid anang (the star that looks so very white) ge-wiipemag (that I would lie with),” ikido (she said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) “Kī’nidạc?” udinān umisäⁿyạn, “ānīn gīn ‘aᵉaᵘ?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Giin dash?” odinaan omisenyan, “aaniin giin a’aw?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“And you? “she said to her big sister, “which would you?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Giin dash (and you)?” odinaan omisenyan (she said to her older sister), “aaniin giin a’aw (which one for you)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Mēdạc ä‘kidot:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ekidod:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon she said:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ekidod (and so she said):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) “Mīsạni∙ī∙nā a’we ā‘pitci kāmîskwāⁿjät.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii sa nii[n] inaa awe aapiji gaa-miskwaanzhed.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My choice is the one that looks so very red.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii sa niin inaa awe (the one for me is that) aapiji gaa-miskwaanzhed (one that looks so very red).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Kā∙i∙ckwā i‘kidowād idạc, mī∙i∙’u kīnibāwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaa-ishkwaa-ikidowaad idash, mii iw gii-nibaawaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And after they had ceased talking, they then went to sleep.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaa-ishkwaa-ikidowaad idash (and after they had finished talking), mii iw gii-nibaawaad (then they slept).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Ạ‘pī∙i∙dạc kwäckusiwād kigijäp päpäjik ininiwa owī‘pämāwād; ‘aᵉaᵘ wäcīmä∙i∙mind uskinawän owī‘pämān, awidạc wämisäⁿ∙i∙mind ā‘pitci a‘kiwäⁿzīyạn owī‘pämān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii idash gweshkoziwaad gigizheb bebezhig ininiwa’ owiipemaawaad; a’aw weshiime’imind oshkinawen owiipemaan, aw idash wemisenimind aapiji akiwenziiyan owiipemaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when they woke in the morning, each was lying in bed with a man; she that was the younger lay with a youth, and she that was the elder lay with an exceedingly aged man.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii idash gweshkoziwaad gigizheb (and when they woke in the morning) bebezhig ininiwa’ owiipemaawaan (each was lying with a man); a’aw weshiime’imind (she who was the younger) oshkinawen owiipemaan (lay with a young man), aw idash wemisenimind (and she who was the elder) aapiji akiwenziiyan owiipemaan (lay with a very old man).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Mī∙i∙’ᵘ kī∙o∙dā‘pinigowād icpîming ạnạngōᵉ, mī∙i∙dạc kīwīdciwāwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii iw gii-odaapinigowaad ishpiming anangoo’, mii dash gii-wiijiwaawaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Therefore were they received up into the sky by the stars, and there they continued with them.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii iw gii-odaapinigowaad ishpiming anangoo’ (so they had been taken up into the sky by the stars), mii dash gii-wiijiwaawaad (and so they dwelt with them).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) ‘Aᵉawidạc säzī‘kizit i‘kwä kāwīn ominwänimāsīn īni’ᵘ a‘kiwäⁿzīyạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**A’aw idash zeziikizid ikwe gaawiin ominwenimaasiin iniw akiwenziiyan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, the elder woman was not pleased with the old man.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">A’aw idash zeziikizid ikwe (now the older woman) gaawiin ominwenimaasiin (was not pleased with) iniw akiwenziiyan (that old man).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) Ningudingidạc kāmādcāwād īgi’∙u∙ ininiwạg, ogạnōnān ucīmäyạn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash gaa-maajaawaad igiw ikwewag, oganoonaan oshiimenyan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So once, when the men were gone, she addressed her little sister, saying:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash gaa-maajaawaad igiw ikwewag (and once when the men had gone off), oganoonaan oshiimenyan (she spoke to her little sister), saying:</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) “Nicīmäⁿ, nindawa mādcātāwᵒ ningudcⁱ!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Nishiimenh, nindawaa maajaadaa ningoji!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“My little sister, do therefore let us go to some place!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Nishiimenh (my little sister), nindawaa maajaadaa ningoji (let us therefore go off somewhere)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Mēdạc kägä‘t kīmādcāwād, wāsa’ kī∙ạ∙ni∙i∙jāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget gii-maajaawaad waasa gii-ani-izhaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly, indeed, away they started, a long way off they went.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget gii-maajaawaad (and so indeed they set off) waasa gii-ani-izhaawag (far away they went).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) Ningudingidạc mīnawā papā∙ā∙ī∙ndiwād ogīmî‘kawāwān mīgwanan.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash miinawaa babaa-ayindiwaad ogii-mikawaawaan miigwanan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now once, as they were simply going along, they discovered a feather.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash miinawaa babaa-ayindiwaad (and once as they were going along) ogii-mikawaawaan miigwanan (they found a feather).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) Mēdạc ä‘kidōwād:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ekidowaad:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon they said:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ekidowaad (and so they said):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) “Tibi wändcīgwän ‘aᵉaᵘ mīgwạn!”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Dibi wenjiigwen a’aw miigwan!”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Wonder from whence came the feather!”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Dibi wenjiigwen a’aw miigwan (wonder where this feather came from)!”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Mīnawādec wībạgu omi‘kawāwān mīgwạnan; käga‘pī∙i∙go ā‘pīdci nībiwa mīgwạnaᵉ owâbạmāwān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miinawaa dash wiiba go omikawaawaan miigwanan; gegapii igo aapiji niibiwa miigwana’ owaabamaawaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And not long afterwards they found another feather; and then at last ever so many feathers they saw.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miinawaa dash wiiba go omikawaawaan miigwanan (and soon they found another feather); gegapii igo aapiji niibiwa miigwana’ owaabamaawaan (at last they saw a great many feathers).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Ningudingidạc owâbạndānāwā wīgiwāmäns mîndimōⁿyäⁿyạn imāⁿ tāwạn; kīpīndigäwạg īgi’ᵘ i‘kwäwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash owaabandaanaawaa wiigiwaamens mindimooyenyan imaanh daawan; gii-biindigewag igiw ikwewag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And presently they saw a small wigwam with an old woman abiding there; inside went the women.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash owaabandaanaawaa wiigiwaamens (and presently they saw a little wigwam) mindimooyenyan imaanh daawan (with an old woman living there); gii-biindigewag igiw ikwewag (the women went inside).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) “Nạmạdapiyu‘k, nōjitug,” i‘kido mîndimōⁿyä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Namadabiyok, noozhidog,” ikido mindimooyenh.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Be seated, my grandchildren,” said the old woman.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Namadabiyok (be seated), noozhidog (my grandchildren),” ikido mindimooyenh (said the old woman).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Nībiwa odayān mīdcîm, pīdcīgigwạnä wiyās ogi∙ạ∙camigowān idạc.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Niibiwa odayaan miijim, biijiigigwane-wiiyaas ogii-ashamigowaan idash.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Much she had of food, and the flesh of a fish-hawk they were fed.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Niibiwa odayaan miijim (much food she had), biijiigigwane-wiiyaas ogii-ashamigowaan idash (and fish-hawk flesh she fed them).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) Ningudingigu nạmạdapit ‘aᵉa mindimōⁿyäⁿ owī‘kupidōn pīmạna‘kwān, mēdạc îmāⁿ kīnîsād pīdcigigwạnän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding igo namadabid a’aw mindimooyenh owiikobidoon biimanakwaan, mii dash imaanh gii-nisaad biijigigwanen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now presently, while the old woman was seated, she pulled upon a cord, and then there she killed a Fish-hawk.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding igo namadabid a’aw mindimooyenh (now once as the old woman sat) owiikobidoon biimanakwaan (she pulled on a cord), mii dash imaanh gii-nisaad biijigigwanen (and so there she killed a fish-hawk).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) Pạgunäyāni a‘ki îmāⁿ nạmạdạpit ‘aᵉaᵘ mindimōyäᵉ; mī∙i∙māⁿ wändābigạmunig omīgiskạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Bagoneyaani aki imaanh namadabid a’aw mindimooyenh; mii imaanh wendaabigamonig omigiskan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*There was an opening in the ground at the place where the old woman sat; it was from there that her hook came.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bagoneyaani aki imaanh (there was a hole in the ground there) namadabid a’aw mindimooyenh (where the old woman sat); mii imaanh wendaabigamonig omigiskan (it was from there that her hook came on its line).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) Ōgạgwädcimigowān dạc mindimōyäⁿyạn:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogagwejimigowaan dash mindimooyenyan:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And they were asked by the old woman:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogagwejimigowaan dash mindimooyenyan (and they were asked by the old woman):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) “Ānīdiwädcīyäg?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniindi wenjiiyeg?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“From whence came you?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniindi wenjiiyeg (where do you come from)?”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) Mī∙i∙dạc kīwīndạmawāwad kī∙o∙dā‘pinigowād ạnạngōᵉ.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-wiindamawaawad gii-odaapinigowaad anangoo’.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon they informed her that they had been carried away by the stars.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-wiindamawaawad (and so they told her) gii-odaapinigowaad anangoo’ (that they had been taken away by the stars).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) Mī∙i∙dạc ä‘kidot mindimōyä:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ekidod mindimooye:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon said the old woman:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ekidod mindimooye (and so said the old woman):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) “Kīcpîn uji‘tōyäg nībiwa wīgu‘p kạbōni‘kiyābīkinininim ändana‘kīyägubạn tcī∙i∙jāyäg.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Giishpin ozhitooyeg niibiwa wiigob ga-boonikiyaabikinininim endanakiiyegoban ji-ayaayeg.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“If you will make a long cord, I will let you down, so that you can go back to the place where you live.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Giishpin ozhitooyeg niibiwa wiigob (if you make a lot of basswood cord) ga-boonikiyaabikinininim (I will let you down by the line) endanakiiyegoban (to where you used to live) ji-ayaayeg (so you may be there).”</span>

<span class="l-orig">(42) Mēdac kägä‘t kī∙o∙ji‘tōwād nībiwa wīgu‘p.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget gii-ozhitoowaad niibiwa wiigob.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Therefore truly they made a long cord.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget gii-ozhitoowaad niibiwa wiigob (and so indeed they made much basswood cord).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(43) “Uji‘tōyu‘k wạdapīwạc.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Ozhitooyok wadapiiwazh.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Make a basket of spruce-root.*</span>
