Wīndigō Näsint.

<span class="l-fiero">**Wiin digoo Nesind**</span>

When a Wiin digoo was Slain.

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ninguding päjik ạnicinäbä kī‘pi∙u∙ndcipägobạn ōwidi Pā∙u∙‘ting; pạngī pō‘kwāwigạnä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding bezhig anishinaabe gii-bi-onjibaagoban owidi Baa’oting; bangii bookwaawigane.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once a certain man came from yonder Sault; he was slightly hunched in the back.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding bezhig anishinaabe (once a certain man) gii-bi-onjibaagoban (came from) owidi Baa’oting (yonder Sault); bangii bookwaawigane (he was slightly hunched in the back).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Mīdạc¹ omäⁿ kīna∙ā∙ngabigobạnän, i‘kwäwạn ōmā kāwīdigämād mägwä ạnōdc kī∙i∙jictcigäwād ạnicinäbäg, kīmạnidōkāsowād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash omaanh gii-na’aangabigobanen, ikwewan omaa gaa-wiidigemaad megwaa anooj gii-izhichigewaad anishinaabeg, gii-manidookaazowaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And so here* he dwelt with his wife’s family; for here he had married a woman at a time when thepeople were accomplishing all sorts of things, when they did miracles through the manitou.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash omaanh gii-na’aangabigobanen (and so here he settled and dwelt), ikwewan omaa gaa-wiidigemaad (having married a woman here) megwaa anooj gii-izhichigewaad anishinaabeg (at a time when the people were doing all sorts of things), gii-manidookaazowaad (when they worked wonders through the manitou).</span>

*¹ On the north shore of Lake Superior, either at Nipigun or Kaministiqua.*

<span class="l-orig">(3) Mōⁿjạg kīkistciwī‘kundiwạg wänicicing mīdcỉm; kāwī‘kasa ogi∙a∙camāsiwāwān īni‘ᵘ ạnicinäbän, kāwī‘kāgạnạgä oginạndumāsiwāwān kägō wā∙i∙jutcigäwādỉn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Moozhag gii-gischi-wiikondiwag wenizhishing miijim; gaa wiikaa sa ogii-ashamaasiwaawaan iniw anishinaaben, gaawiin ganage ogii-nandomaasiwaawaan gegoo waa-izhochigewaadin [waa-izhichigewaadin].**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Many a great feast they celebrated with food that was choice; yet never did they feed that man, not even did they ever invite him when they were going to bring something to pass.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Moozhag gii-gischi-wiikondiwag (many a great feast they held) wenizhishing miijim (with food that was choice); gaa wiikaa sa ogii-ashamaasiwaawaan iniw anishinaaben (yet never did they feed that man), gaawiin ganage ogii-nandomaasiwaawaan (nor did they ever invite him) gegoo waa-izhochigewaadin [waa-izhichigewaadin] (when they were going to do something).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Ningudingidạc päbōng kī∙ạ∙mạnisōwạg kīstcitcingwänik a‘ki, kīmạmāsi‘kā a‘ki.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash beboong gii-amanisowaag gischi-jiingwenig aki, gii-mamaazikaa aki.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once in the winter-time they became alarmed at a great rumbling in the earth, the earth shook.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash beboong (now once in the winter) gii-amanisowaag (they became alarmed) gischi-jiingwenig aki (at a great rumbling in the earth), gii-mamaazikaa aki (the earth shook).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Mīdạc kīsägisiwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash gii-zegiziwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon they became afraid.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-zegiziwaad (thereupon they became afraid).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) “Mīsa awä wīndigō,” kī∙i∙‘kidowạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii sa awe wiindigoo,” gi-ikidowag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“It is the Wiindigoo, “ they said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii sa awe wiindigoo,” (“it is the Wiindigoo,”) gi-ikidowag (they said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) Ackạmigo pācu’ kī‘pi∙a∙yāwạn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eshkam igo besho gii-bi-ayaawan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Nearer it kept coming.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eshkam igo besho gii-bi-ayaawan (nearer and nearer it kept coming).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Mīdạc iwa‘pī kī∙a∙‘tōwād anō‘kā‘tcigạn; ānugīgạgwädcindiwād; kāwīn dạc awiya ugīkaskitōsīn tcimīgānād wīndigōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash iw apii gii-atoowaad anookaajigan; aano-gii-gagwejindiwaad; gaawiin dash awiya ogii-gashkitoosiin ji-miigaanaad wiindigoon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly then they made an offering of goods; they put questions one to another, but to no purpose; and no one had power to fight against the Wiindigoo.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash iw apii gii-atoowaad anookaajigan (and then they made an offering of goods); aano-gii-gagwejindiwaad (they questioned one another in vain); gaawiin dash awiya ogii-gashkitoosiin (but no one was able) ji-miigaanaad wiindigoon (to fight the Wiindigoo).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) Käga‘pī∙i∙gu ā‘pidci kīmạmāsi‘kā a‘ki.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii igo aapiji gii-mamaazikaa aki.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And at last ever so much did the earth tremble.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii igo aapiji gii-mamaazikaa aki (and at last ever so much did the earth tremble).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) Kāwīn gayä ogaskitōsīnāwa iskotä tcipỉskạnäg; ānind gayä ạnicinabäg kāwīn mạmi‘kawisiwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin gaye ogas[h]kitoosiinawaa is[h]kote ji-biskaneg; aanind gaye anishinaabeg gaawiin mamikawisiiwag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And they were unable to start a fire into a blaze; and some of the people too were (scared) out of their senses.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin gaye ogas[h]kitoosiinawaa is[h]kote ji-biskaneg (and they could not make a fire blaze up); aanind gaye anishinaabeg gaawiin mamikawisiiwag (and some of the people were out of their senses).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Käga‘pī∙i∙dac onạndumāwān īni‘ᵘ ạnicinäbän pwā‘kwāwigạnänit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii idash onandomaawaan iniw anishinaaben bwaakwaawiganenid.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So finally they sent for the man with the hunch in his back.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii idash onandomaawaan iniw anishinaaben (so finally they sent for the man) bwaakwaawiganenid (with the hunch in his back).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Ogīpạgidỉnạmạwāwān ī∙i∙‘ᵘ obạgidcigäwiniwa, ogī∙ī∙guwān idạc:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-bagidinamawaawaan iniw obagijigewiniwaa, ogii-igowaan idash:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*They presented him with the offerings they had made. And they were told:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-bagidinamawaawaan iniw obagijigewiniwaa (they presented him with the offerings they had made), ogii-igowaan idash (and they were told by him):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) “Mīna iᵘ pỉdcīnạg mi‘kwänimiyäg nōngum sägiziyäg?”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Mii na iw bijiinag mikwenimiyeg noongom zegiziyeg?”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Is it only now, when you are scared, that you think of me?”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Mii na iw bijiinag mikwenimiyeg (“is it only now that you think of me) noongom zegiziyeg?” (now that you are afraid?”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Kāwīn ogi∙o∙dā‘pinaⁿzīnạn ini‘ᵘ ānumīnint.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin ogii-odaapinanziinan iniw aano-miinind.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He did not accept what was vainly offered him.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin ogii-odaapinanziinan (he did not accept) iniw aano-miinind (what was offered him in vain).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Gī∙ạ∙nisaga∙ạ∙m, ändāt kī∙i∙jād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gii-ani-zaaga’am, endaad gii-izhaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He went on out of doors, to where he dwelt he went.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gii-ani-zaaga’am (he went on out of doors), endaad gii-izhaad (to where he dwelt he went).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) Ogi∙ạ∙nōnān wīwạn wäwīp oma‘kizinạn tci∙o∙ji‘tōnit.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ogii-anoonaan wiiwan wewiib omakizinan ji-ozhitoonid.**</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ogii-anoonaan wiiwan (he had his wife) wewiib omakizinan ji-ozhitoonid (quickly make him moccasins).</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And the woman hurriedly went to work upon the moccasins.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">A’aw idash ikwe (and the woman) wewiib gii-makizinike (hurriedly went to work on the moccasins).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Zhigwa aapiji besho bi-ayaa wiindigoo (already very close was the Wiindigoo now coming).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Bebezhigweg bas[h]gweginoon (there was a dressed skin for each foot).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash gii-maajaad a’aw bwaakwaawigang (thereupon the hunchback departed).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aazha aanind anishinaabeg (already some of the people) gaawiin ganage maajiisiiwag (did not even move).</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Ayaangwaamizin!” (“do you take care!”) odinaan wiiwan (he said to his wife).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Sāga∙i∙gạn i∙i∙māⁿ kī∙a∙yāni, wä‘kwāgamīng cingwā‘kwạg ayāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Zaaga’igan imaanh gii-ayaani, wekwaagamiing zhingwaakwag ayaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) “Wābạng kegicäp pī∙i∙nābi‘kạn i∙i∙māⁿ cingwā‘kwạg kī∙a∙yāwād.</span>
