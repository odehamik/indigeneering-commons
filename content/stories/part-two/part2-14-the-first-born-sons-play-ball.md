MADCĪ‘KIWISẠG KĀPĀGA∙Ā∙DOWÄWĀD.

<span class="l-fiero">**MAJIIKIWISAG GAA-BAAGA’ADOWEWAAD.**</span>

THE FIRST-BORN SONS PLAY BALL.

<span class="l-eng">*Narrated by Penesi — Series 2 (Nos. 4–43), Fort William (per Jones Part II Introduction p. ix; not credited on the story page itself).*</span>

<span class="l-fiero">***from Ojibwa Texts collected by William Jones (1919).***</span>

<span class="l-orig">(1) Ningudingsa kīnwän kīdāwạg mạdcī‘kiwisạg; udätōwạg; ā‘pidci kistciōdäna ī∙i∙mān ayāwāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding sa giiwen’ gii-daawag majiikiwisag; oodetoowag; aapiji gichi-oodena imaa ayaawaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Once on a time, as the story goes, there lived some first-born sons; in a town they dwelt; exceedingly large was the town where they were.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding sa giiwen’ (once on a time, they say) gii-daawag majiikiwisag (there lived some first-born sons); oodetoowag (they had a town); aapiji gichi-oodena (very large was the town) imaa ayaawaad (where they were).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(2) Ạnōdc ijitcigäwạg udạminōwāt; tạsing kājigadinigin udaminōwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Anooj izhichigewag odaminoowaad; dasing gaazhigadinigin odaminowag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*All sorts of things they did in the way of games; as often as the days came round, they played at games.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Anooj izhichigewag odaminoowaad (all sorts of things they did in the way of play); dasing gaazhigadinigin (as often as the days came round) odaminowag (they played).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(3) Ningudingidạc unākunigä ‘agaw’u mạdcī‘kiwis tci∙a∙‘tādiwād tcibāga∙ā∙dowäwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Ningoding idash onaakonige a’aw majiikiwis ji-ataadiwaad ji-baaga’adowewaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, once (one of) the first-born announced that there would be a ball-game.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Ningoding idash onaakonige (now once he made the arrangement) a’aw majiikiwis (that first-born) ji-ataadiwaad (that they would have a contest) ji-baaga’adowewaad (that they would play ball).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(4) Mīdạc gäegä‘t äjimādcitāwāt wī∙a∙‘tādiwāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash geget ezhi-maajitaawaad wii-ataadiwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Whereupon truly began they to get ready for the contest.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash geget ezhi-maajitaawaad (and truly so they began getting ready) wii-ataadiwaad (to have the contest).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(5) Kayä wīn ‘aga’u päejik mạdcī‘kiwis päpạngī pa‘kān aiyendiwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaye wiin a’aw bezhig majiikiwis bebangii bakaan ayiyendiwag(?).**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, another first-born (and his friends) did a little differently.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaye wiin a’aw bezhig majiikiwis (and another first-born too) bebangii bakaan (a little differently) ayiyendiwag (they were minded — *fiero printed with editorial query*).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(6) Ābi‘ta ändạciwāt uwīwītcīwâwān wīpāga∙ā∙towäwāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Aabita endazhiwaad owii-wiijiiwaawaan wii-baaga’adowewaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Half of them were on one side to play ball (against the other half).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Aabita endazhiwaad (half of them on their side) owii-wiijiiwaawaan (would go along with them) wii-baaga’adowewaad (to play ball).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(7) ‘Agau päjig mạdcī‘kiwis ugī∙u∙ndinān pigwā‘kwạt wā∙ā∙bạdci‘tōwād, ujāwackuminạgạt ī∙i∙’u bigwā‘kwạt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**A’aw bezhig majiikiwis ogii-ondinaan bikwaakwad waa-aabajitoowaad, ozhaawashkominagad i’iw bikwaakwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*One of the first-born took out the ball which they were to use, blue was the color of the ball.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">A’aw bezhig majiikiwis (one of the first-born) ogii-ondinaan bikwaakwad (took out the ball) waa-aabajitoowaad (which they were to use), ozhaawashkominagad i’iw bikwaakwad (blue in color was that ball).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(8) Mīdạc ä‘kidot ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash ekidod a’aw majiikiwis:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon said the first-born:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash ekidod a’aw majiikiwis (thereupon said that first-born):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(9) “Wâbạnūng nīn nīngạtinạgatō,” i‘kido.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Waabanoong niin ningad-inagadoo,” ikido,**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Towards the east will I play for goal.” he said.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Waabanoong niin (“towards the east I) ningad-inagadoo,” (will play for goal,”) ikido (he said).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(10) “Kīnidạc,” udinān īni’u wā∙a∙‘tawāt, “nīngābī∙a∙nung ina‘kakäyā.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Giin idash,” odinaan iniw waa-atawaad, “ningaabii’anong inakakeyaa.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“And you,” he said to them against whom he was to play, “toward the west.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Giin idash,” (“and you,”) odinaan iniw waa-atawaad (he said to those he was to play against), “ningaabii’anong inakakeyaa.” (“toward the west.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(11) Mīdạc kī‘kagīgi∙i∙nāwāt kägicī‘kānit ininiwag.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii idash gii-gagiiginaawaad ge-gizhiikaanid ininiwag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Accordingly they picked out the men that were fleet of foot.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii idash gii-gagiiginaawaad (accordingly they picked out) ge-gizhiikaanid ininiwag (the men that would be fleet of foot).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(12) Weyâbang kigijäb kīmādci‘tāwạg.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Weyaabang gigizheb gii-maajitaawag.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*On the morning of the next day they began (playing).*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Weyaabang gigizheb (on the morning of the next day, early) gii-maajitaawag (they began).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(13) Mēdạc cigwa nimbawāwāt, kāwīn nayānj awiya mijạgạdōsī.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash zhigwa nimbawaawaad, gaawiin nayaanzh awiya mizhagadoosii.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when they started the ball going, it was a long while before any one could make a goal.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash zhigwa nimbawaawaad (and so now they set the ball going), gaawiin nayaanzh awiya mizhagadoosii (for a long while no one could make a goal).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(14) Änī∙i∙ckwānā∙u∙‘kwag mī cigwa cāgōdci∙i∙nt, ocāgōdci∙i∙gōn īni’u pipōnisän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Eni-ishkwaanaa’okweg mii zhigwa zhaagooji’ind, ozhaagooji’igoon iniw biboonisen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Along in the afternoon was when (one of the first-born) was being beaten, he was being beaten by Winter-Wind.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Eni-ishkwaanaa’okweg (along in the afternoon) mii zhigwa zhaagooji’ind (then was one being beaten), ozhaagooji’igoon iniw biboonisen (he was being beaten by Winter-Wind).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(15) Käga‘pī kī∙ạ∙nicāgōdciwinā ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis, wīn dạc pipōnisä kīmījạgadō ningābī∙a∙nunk ina‘kakä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii gii-ani-zhaagooji’winaa a’aw majiikiwis, wiin dash biboonise gii-mizhagadoo ningaabii’anong inagake.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*At last was the first-born being beaten, for Winter-Wind had made a goal on the side toward the west.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii gii-ani-zhaagooji’winaa a’aw majiikiwis (at last that first-born was being beaten), wiin dash biboonise (and Winter-Wind for his part) gii-mizhagadoo ningaabii’anong inagake (made a goal at the side toward the west).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(16) A‘pā∙i∙dạc kāpa‘kinawint ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis ugīkạnōnigōn pipōnisän:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii idash gaa-bakinawind a’aw majiikiwis ogii-ganoonigoon biboonisen:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And when the first-born was beaten, he was addressed by Winter-Wind saying:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii idash gaa-bakinawind a’aw majiikiwis (and when that first-born was beaten) ogii-ganoonigoon biboonisen (he was addressed by Winter-Wind):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(17) “Ānīc, mīsa kīpa‘kinōnān,” īnā ‘aga’u mādcī‘kiwis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniish, mii sa gii-bakinoonaan,” inaa a’aw majiikiwis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Well, therefore have I beaten you,” was the first-born told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniish, mii sa gii-bakinoonaan,” (“well, therefore have I beaten you,”) inaa a’aw majiikiwis (that first-born was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(18) “Pîdcīnạgigu wâbạnunk pā∙ū∙ndānima‘kin mīgō∙i∙’u cigwa tcîbinīskādagōtäg ‘ugu kījig tcigîmiwunk.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Bijiinag igo waabanong ba-ondaanimakin mii goo iw zhigwa ji-bi-niiskaadagooteg o’o giizhig ji-gimiwang.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“As soon as ever the wind blows from the east, then will foul weather hang aloft in this sky for the rain to fall.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Bijiinag igo waabanong ba-ondaanimakin (“as soon as ever the wind blows hither from the east) mii goo iw zhigwa (then it is that) ji-bi-niiskaadagooteg o’o giizhig (foul weather will hang aloft in this sky) ji-gimiwang. (for the rain to fall.)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(19) Mīsa∙i∙ äjipa‘kinōnān,” inān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa i’ ezhi-bakinoonaan,” inaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Therefore such is what I have won from you,” he was told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa i’ ezhi-bakinoonaan,” (“therefore such is how I have won from you,”) inaan (he was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(20) Mīdạc ī∙i∙’u kā∙u∙ndci∙i∙jiwäba‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash i’iw gaa-onji-izhiwebak.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*And that is what happens.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash i’iw gaa-onji-izhiwebak (and that is why it happens so).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(21) Kīcpîn wâbạnunk wändānima‘kin mīgu∙i∙’u cigwa mạdcigījiga‘k.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Giishpin waabanong wendaanimakin mii go iw zhigwa maji-giizhigak.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When the wind blows from the east, then that is a sign for a bad day.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Giishpin waabanong wendaanimakin (when the wind blows from the east) mii go iw zhigwa maji-giizhigak (then it is that the day turns bad).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(22) Mī∙i∙’u kā∙i∙jipa‘kinawān-windibạnän ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii iw gaa-izhi-bakinawaan windibanen a’aw majiikiwis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*It is because the first-born was once beaten in a contest.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii iw gaa-izhi-bakinawaan windibanen a’aw majiikiwis (it is because that first-born was once beaten in a contest — *preterite participle*).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(23) Kāwīn kiminwändazī pa‘kinawint.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gaawiin giminwendazii bakinawind.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*He was not pleased to be beaten.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gaawiin giminwendazii bakinawind (he was not pleased to be beaten).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(24) Minawā wī∙ā∙ndcī∙e∙ ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miinawaa wii-aanjii’e a’aw majiikiwis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Over again did the first-born wish to play.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miinawaa wii-aanjii’e a’aw majiikiwis (over again did that first-born wish to play).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(25) “Tạga, mīnawā a‘tādidāe!” i‘kido ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Daga, miinawaa ataadidaa!” ikido a’aw majiikiwis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Come, let us have another game!” said the first-born.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Daga, miinawaa ataadidaa!” (“come, let us have another game!”) ikido a’aw majiikiwis (said that first-born).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(26) “Āwâwisa’,” udigōn pipōnisän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aawaawisa.” odigoon biboonisen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Very well,” he was told by Winter-Wind.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aawaawisa.” (“very well.”) odigoon biboonisen (he was told by Winter-Wind).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(27) Weyâbạning mī∙i∙’u minawā ujigābawiwād wībāga∙ā∙dowäwād.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Weyaabaninig mii iw miinawaa ozhigaabawiwaad wii-baaga’adowewaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*On the morrow they then took their places for another game of ball.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Weyaabaninig mii iw miinawaa (on the morrow then again) ozhigaabawiwaad (they took their places) wii-baaga’adowewaad (to play ball).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(28) “Kīwädinūnk nīn ningạtinạgatū,” i‘kidō ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Giiwedinong niin ningad-inagadoo,” ikido a’aw majiikiwis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Towards the north will I play for goal,” said the first-born.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Giiwedinong niin ningad-inagadoo,” (“towards the north I will play for goal,”) ikido a’aw majiikiwis (said that first-born).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(29) “Gīn idạc, pipōnisä, câwanūnk ina‘kakä inagạdōn,” undinān pipōnisän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Giin idash, biboonise, zhaawanong inagake inagadoon.” odinaan biboonisen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“And you, Winter-Wind, towards the south do you play for goal,” he said to Winter-Wind.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Giin idash, biboonise, (“and you, Winter-Wind,) zhaawanong inagake inagadoon.” (toward the south do you play for goal.”) odinaan biboonisen (he said to Winter-Wind).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(30) Mī jigwa umbawâwāt, usāsā‘kwānigōwa känawâbamigowāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii zhigwa ombawaawaad, ozaasagwaanigoowa genawaabamigowaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*So when they began playing, they were cheered on by the yells of them who were watching them.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii zhigwa ombawaawaad (so now they set the ball going), ozaasagwaanigoowa genawaabamigowaad (they were cheered on by the yells of those watching them).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(31) Kạbägījik menawā ubạbāmiwäpa∙ā∙nāwa pi‘kwā‘kwạt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gabe-giizhik miinawaa obabaami-webaanaawa bikwaakwad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*All day long again they carried the ball back and forth and all around.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gabe-giizhik miinawaa (all day long again) obabaami-webaanaawa bikwaakwad (they threw the ball about back and forth).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(32) Mîskuminagạt dạc ī’∙i∙’u pigwā‘kwāt.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Miskominagad dash iw bikwaakwaad.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Red was the color of the ball.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Miskominagad dash i’iw bikwaakwaad (red in color was that ball).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(33) Ā‘pī∙ä∙ni∙u∙nāgucininig mī∙i∙’u cigwa minawā câgōdci∙i∙nt ‘agau mạdcī‘kiwis.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Apii eni-onaagoshininig mii iw zhigwa miinawaa zhaagooji’ind a’aw majiikiwis.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*When it was getting well on towards the evening, then again was the first-born being beaten.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Apii eni-onaagoshininig (when it was getting on toward evening) mii i’iw zhigwa miinawaa zhaagooji’ind a’aw majiikiwis (then again was that first-born being beaten).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(34) Käga‘pī mīnawā kīmījagạdō pipōnisä cāwạnunk ina‘kakä.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gegapii miinawaa gii-mizhagadoo biboonise zhaawanong inagake.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*At length again did Winter-Wind make a goal at the end towards the south.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gegapii miinawaa gii-mizhagadoo biboonise (at length again did Winter-Wind make a goal) zhaawanong inagake (at the side toward the south).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(35) Mīdạc mīnawā äjikạnōnint ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii dash miinawaa ezhi-ganoonind a’aw majiikiwis:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Thereupon once more was the first-born addressed:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii dash miinawaa ezhi-ganoonind a’aw majiikiwis (thereupon once more was that first-born addressed):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(36) “Ānīc, mīsa iu mīnawā kīpa‘kinōnān,” inān.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Aaniish, mii sa iw miinawaa gii-bakinoonaan,” inaan.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“Well, therefore again have I beaten you,” he was told.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Aaniish, mii sa iw miinawaa gii-bakinoonaan,” (“well, therefore again have I beaten you,”) inaan (he was told).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(37) “Pîdcīnagigu kīwädonūnk pā∙u∙dānima‘kin mī∙i∙’u ka‘kina tcî∙u∙jimōwād kidockinīgīmạg, nīnidạc kāwīn ogaku‘tạnzīnāwa nindockinīgīmạg.”</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**“Bijiinaagigo giiwedinong ba-odaanimakin mii iw gakina ji-ozhimoowaad gidoshkiniigiimag, niin idash gaawiin oga-gotanziinaawa nindoshkiniigiimag.”**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*“As soon as ever the wind blows from the north, then will all your youths flee away, but of me will my youths not be afraid.”*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">“Bijiinaagigo giiwedinong ba-odaanimakin (“as soon as ever the wind blows hither from the north) mii i’iw gakina ji-ozhimoowaad gidoshkiniigiimag (then will all your youths flee away), niin idash gaawiin oga-gotanziinaawa nindoshkiniigiimag.” (but my youths will not be afraid.”)</span>

<span class="l-orig">(38) Mīsa īgiu ka‘kina päbāmisätcig pinäsiwạg, mīwag īgi’u kā∙a∙‘tadiwagobạnän.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii sa igiw gakina bebaamisejig binesiwag, miiwag igiw gaa-atadiwagobanen.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*Now, they were all the birds that fly about in the air, it was they that were in the contest.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii sa igiw gakina bebaamisejig binesiwag (now, they were all the birds that fly about), miiwag igiw gaa-atadiwagobanen (it was they that were in the contest — *preterite participle*).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(39) Ka‘kina nībinisän mīwanini’u kawīdcīwāgubạnän ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis kāku‘tạmuwād pîbōn.</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Gakina niibinisen miiwan iniw gaa-wiijiiwaagobanen a’aw majiikiwis gaa-gotamowaad biboon.**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*All the birds of summer with whom the first-born played were the ones that feared the winter.*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Gakina niibinisen (all the summer birds) miiwan iniw gaa-wiijiiwaagobanen a’aw majiikiwis (were the ones the first-born played alongside — *preterite participle*) gaa-gotamowaad biboon (the ones that feared the winter).</span>

<span class="l-orig">(40) Mī∙i∙’u kā∙i∙nāwindibạnän ‘aga’u mạdcī‘kiwis:</span>

<span class="l-fiero">**Mii iw gaa-inaawindibanen a’aw majiikiwis:**</span>

<span class="l-eng">*This, then, was the first-born told at the time:*</span>

<span class="l-gloss">Mii iw gaa-inaawindibanen a’aw majiikiwis (this, then, was that first-born told at the time — *preterite participle*):</span>

<span class="l-orig">(41) “Nīnidạc pipōnisä.</span>
